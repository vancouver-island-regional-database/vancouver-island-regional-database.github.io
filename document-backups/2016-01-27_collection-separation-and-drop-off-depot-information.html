Please note: 
 All loads must be secure and covered by a tarp or other overlay so materials cannot blow off or fall out while in transit.  
Unsecure loads may be fined! 
 All waste cannot exceed 1.25 m in length or 100 kg in weight and must be free from recyclables. 
 No person shall deposit prohibited waste at the CVRD depots.  Call the recycling hotline for more information. 
 All fiberglass insulation and dusty material must be contained in a double bagged system. 
 Vermiculite is not accepted at any CVRD recycling depot unless proper documentation is provided certifying that it is 
asbestos-free. 
 Children and pets must remain in the vehicle at all times.
