LADYSMITH - A natural choice for business 
 
 
A proud recipient of Canada’s Greenest Employers award four years in a row! 
 
There are over 500 licensed businesses contributing to our local economy.  Our businesses cover 
the spectrum from retail to construction  and services to manufacturing.  Council has seized 
every opportunity we can to support our local businesses and to help them be successful.  
Measures we have taken include: 
1. Promote local tax incentives such as the Revitalization Tax Exemption program for downtown 
business owners who carry out property improvements and the Economic Revitalization Tax 
Exemption Program for the creation of new businesses and jobs. 
2. Created DL2016 Hold ing Corp which paved the way for funding and an operating agreement and 
servicing to Ladysmith Maritime Society to support the Community Marina visitor moorage and 
Welcome Centre. 
3. Investing in significant water and sewer infrastructure improvements to ensu re services exist for 
business and provide other core services like the Fire Department and RCMP. 
4. Created the Rocky Creek light industrial / business park with reduced Development Cost Charges for 
investors. 
5. Work with property owners and Stz’uminus First Nation to develop the South Ladysmith Industrial 
Park. 
6. Collaborate with other agencies to bring programs to Ladysmith such as the ‘Climate Smart for 
Business’ program completed by the “49th Parallel Grocery Store”. 
7. Maintain a “Business and Development Portal” on the Town’s website to provide a simple point of 
entry to local business information and resources ( www.ladysmith.ca/business-
development/overview) 
8. Installed new parking lot signage to highlight free public parking lots in the Downtown. 
9. Offer the “BizPal” service through the Town’s website which offers a quick assessment or local, 
provincial and federal permit requirements for business. 
10. Support the “ 10% Shift” Shop Local Initiative in partnership with the Chamber and the LDBA and 
promote the program on the Town’s website. 


 
11. Participate in the Partnership for an Economically Diverse Community with the Ladysmith Chamber 
of Commerce, the Ladysmith Downtown  Business Association and the Stz’uminus First Nation.  
Council hosts two meetings per year.  
12. The Town is introducing social procurement into its purchasing policy. 
13. Introduced business-friendly checklists for business licensing and business signage.  
14. Initiated business licence program options such as the Inter -Municipal Business Licence (Cowichan 
Region) and Inter-Community Business Licence (Malahat north) for mobile businesses. 
15. Support the Chamber of Commerce to operate the Visitor Centre and start up a Business Centre 
through funding an annual service contract which sees all Business Licence revenue going to the 
Chamber (unique in the Cowichan Region). 
16. Publish an annual Community Profile of economic and community information providing local 
information for businesses looking to expand or investment in Ladysmith. 
17. Involved the business community in the development of the new Zoning Bylaw. 
18. Facilitated regional transit service locally to provide a way for our less mobile citizens to support 
local small businesses from one end of Ladysmith to the other. 
19. Promoted Ladysmith as an  investment ready community through the business -led “BC Small 
Business Roundtable” .  I n 2011 , the Town  was presented the award for the Most Small Business 
Friendly Community in the Vancouver Island/Coast Region. We were proud to make a submission for 
this award with letters of support from the Chamber of Commerce and the LDBA.  
20. Continue to reduce the overall reliance on non-residential taxation classes. 
21. Developed a made-in-Ladysmith Economic Development Strategy with the support of the Ladysmith 
Economic Development Commission. 
22. Partner with and contribute financially to the services delivered through the Cowichan Valley 
Regional District Economic Development Cowichan Department – economic development, regional 
tourism and film office.  
23. Produced a new Ladysmith Investor Profile in collaboration with Economic Development Cowichan . 
24. Provide Ladysmith collateral to the Economic Development Cowichan for trade-shows. 
25. Welcome investors locally and internationally and provide Ladysmith’s community profile, economic 
sectors, and plain language business investment information.   
26. Involve the development community in planning for infrastructure to support new neighbourhoods. 
27. Created an Industrial Land Inventory to assist investor inquiries. 

 
28. Working with the Chamber and the LDBA in the update and refresh of the Town’s signage program 
to invite visitors off the TransCanada Highway and into Downtown Ladysmith. 
29. Work with Ladysmith Secondary art class for student art banners in the Downtown. 
30. Promote two heritage walks centred on the heritage downtown :  Heritage Walk I – Artifacts and 
Heritage Walk II – Buildings. 
31. Host the Tourism Ladysmith website (www.tourismladysmith.ca) with a mobile-friendly platform. 
32. Participate in regional and local tourism promotion. 
33. Promote BC Heritage Week to invite residents and visitors to our living heritage downtown. 
34. Maintain a professional photography inventory for economic development and tourism promotion. 
35. Built Forrest Field as a community asset to support sports tourism and participate in the regional 
sports tourism network.
