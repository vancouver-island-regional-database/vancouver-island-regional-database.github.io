DELEGATIONS Special Presentation to John Wilson, Retiring Chair of the Liquid Waste 
Management Committee 
Mayor Stone introduced John Wilson, retiring chair of the Liquid Waste 
Management Committee , summarizing his past contributions and 
service to the Town and recognizing the dedication and stewardship he 
demonstrated in his most recent role as chair of the largest capital 
project in the Town’s history, the Waste Water Treatment Plant. 
 
Council thanked Mr. Wilson for his years of service and commitment  to 
the committee, and expressed their appreciation with a plaque.
