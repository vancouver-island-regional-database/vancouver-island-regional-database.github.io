MINUTES 
Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission (HRAC) 
Thursday, April 16, 2009 at 7:00 p.m. 
Council Chambers, City Hall 
 
 
LADYSMITH 
 
PRESENT: Chair – Maureen Martin; Members – John Hinde, Bruce Laxdal, Ann Rogers, 
Peter Blumel; Council Liaison – Lori Evans; Manager & Recorder – Felicity 
Adams 
 
 
The Chair called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m. 
 
1.         AGENDA       
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of April 16, 2009 be approved.  
 
2.   MINUTES 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of March 17, 2009 be approved. 
 
3. BUSINESS ARISING FROM THE MINUTES 
 None. 
 
4.        COUNCIL REFERRALS/UPDATES 
Councillor, L. Evans reported that the He ritage Tourism workshop final report was 
presented to Council on April 6, 2009.  A. Rogers reported that there is continued 
interest and she will follow-up with the members of Tourism Advisory Committee that 
were present at the workshop. 
 
5.  SIGN/FAÇADE APPLICATIONS 
 a. Façade application - Lorne & Verna Hastings – 626-622 First Avenue
The Development Permit proposal (D P 09-02) for the façade improvement 
was circulated to members of the HRAC. 
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission 
supports the façade improvement for the Hastings Buildings, and respectfully wonders if the 
colour “Blair Gold” is compatible with the awning. 
 
6.  CURRENT PROJECT UPDATES/NEXT STEPS  
a. Heritage Protection/Statements of Significance 
B. Laxdal reported that the Province has funding available.  The members 
discussed the project and resources. 
 
The focus is on First Avenue buildings by preparing SOS and identifying values 
to property owners.   
 
B. Laxdal will work on the project proposal and consult the staff. 
 
b. Extension Mine Explosion

7. CORRESPONDENCE 
None.

The next meeting is scheduled for Thursday, May 14, 2009 at 7:00 p.m.

It was moved, seconded and carried that the meeting be adjourned at 8:35 p.m.
