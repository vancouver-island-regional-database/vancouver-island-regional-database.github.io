e. CMF Construction – 521 First Avenue 
Members reviewed and accepted the proposed changes to the siding material, 
including replacing the existing 3” cedar siding with 4” hardi -plank on the south and 
west-facing facades, with the exception of the lower portion of the façade to the 
west.  
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission support 
the replacement of cedar siding with Hardi-plank as proposed at 521 First Avenue. 
 
Members discussed the benefits of upgrading the existing façade colours to coincide 
with the new siding. The following recommendations were made:    
 
 Select a paint colour that is not used in excess in the downtown (i.e. not grey or 
blue).  
 Colour suggestions include sage green with off -white trim, or cream/yellow with 
blue trim, etc., with a third or fourth complementary colour. 
 Consider reducing the visual impact of a large building by painting as two 
attached buildings, or using different paint colours , shades, or material  in the 
upper or lower aspects of the building. 
 
Once available, members will review the proposed paint colours via email to hasten 
the process.
