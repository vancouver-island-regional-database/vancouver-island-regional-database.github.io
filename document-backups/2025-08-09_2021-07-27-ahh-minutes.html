1.Introduction: Mayor Stone recognized Stz’uminus territory and welcomed everyone to the meeting. 
2.Words From Chief Harris: Chief Harris thanked everyone for attending, and gave a short opening statement:  
- It is important for people to be aware that the Stz’uminus people are present and are part of the 
community.
- Duncan is a good example of a strong indigenous presence.
- The Stz’uminus have lived in the area for thousands of years. There is much they could share with the 
community, which would be a great chance to strengthen the bonds between the Stz’uminus and the 
Town of Ladysmith. This would be a great example for youth, so they can continue to make connections 
and build a stronger more uniﬁed community.

3.Words from Mayor Stone: Mayor Stone gave a short opening statement: 
- It is important we move forward with “a good head and a good heart.”
- We can come together to reconcile our past and move forward together into a brighter future. This 
project is a chance to come together to celebrate Arts and Culture and to understand our History. 
4.What we Heard: Brett gave a short summary of the comments received during the previous Steering 
Committee meeting, as well as the follow up conversations had with each stakeholder group individually.
5.Project Update: Ben gave a short project update outline the progress made on the Artists Studio relocation 
investigation: 
- The geotechnical investigation has been completed. 
- Based on preliminary conversations with the geotechnical consultant, the structural consultant 
developed preliminary foundation designs for the purposes of comparing the cost difference between the 
original Artists Studio location and the proposed relocation. These designs were sent to the cost 
consultant to determine what the difference in cost would be between the two locations. 
- Following receipt of the cost estimate, the ﬁnal geotechnical report was issued, which slightly revised 
the requirements for the structural design. The updated design has been forwarded to the cost 
consultant to determine the impacts of the new design on the cost estimate, however it is not expected 
to signiﬁcantly alter the cost. 
6.Next Steps: Ben outlined the next steps for the project: 
- The design team is looking forward to spending time with Stz’uminus Elders in the community, in order 
to understand Stz’uminus culture and ensure that it is authentically represented in the project design. 
7.Car and Locomotive Shop Renovations: There is a small renovation allowance in the current funding budget. 
This is just for Phase 1, more funding applications are being submitted for future work.  
8.Historical Society Comments: Quentin noted that the Historical Society is not just focused on Industrial 
History, but also on First Nations history. 
9.Closing Remarks: Mayor Stone thanked all participants for attending, and gave a closing statement.
