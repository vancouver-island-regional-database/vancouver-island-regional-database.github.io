NEW WATER & SEWER RATES
EFFECTIVE APRIL 2017
The utility bill enclosed with this newsletter covers the 
period January to March 2017. When you get your next 
utility bill in July, you will notice an increase, due to new 
water and sewer rates that come into effect with the 
second quarter billing.
The rate increase will help to cover rising costs of providing 
safe, reliable drinking water and treating our waste water 
(sewage), and will also help to build reserves for future 
improvements to our sewer and water supply systems.
The residential sewer rate will go up from $16.80 to 
$22.50 per month. The water rates will increase by 10 
per cent for the base rate and for each usage  block above 
the base rate. Ladysmith uses a block rate structure for 
water billing, meaning that the more water you use, the 
higher the charge per cubic metre (one cubic metre is 220 
gallons.)
The new water rates per quarter are:
Base Rate, to 25 m3   $   29.65 
Next 26 m3 to 50 m3   $    0.7321 per m
3
Next 51 m3 to 75 m3  $    0.8652 per m 3
Next 76 m3 to 100 m3   $    1.0648 per m 3
Next 101 m3 to 125 m3 $    1.3976 per m 3
Over 125 m3     $    1.8634  per m3
For more information, visit the Utility Bills  page on 
our website at www.ladysmith.ca, or call City Hall at 
250.245.6400.

OUR WATERFRONT PLAN 
The T own of Ladysmith and the Stz’uminus First Nation are 
working in partnership to develop a plan for the Ladysmith 
Waterfront Area, in the spirit of continuing to build our 
relationship.  We are working together to create a new 
and exciting plan with a strong focus on implementation.
Three cheers to the 26-member citizen design team 
for an amazing 3-day chattette on the future of the 
waterfront, March 10-12!  And, thank you to the 
many citizens who came by to see the emerging 
ideas in rough sketch form.  A slide show about the 
weekend can be reviewed on the project webpage at:
www.ladysmith.ca/city-hall/waterfront-area-plan.
The project was of ﬁ cially launched on February 1st.  The 
ﬁ rst phase of the project ‘building the vision’ , included 
a Speakers Night, on-line survey and stakeholder 
interviews.  The second phase, ‘developing the plan’ , 
included the design charrette and public drop-in event.  
T o complete the project, staff and the project consultants 
will take your input and begin to prepare a draft plan 
for review in late May / early June.  Public events will 
be announced on the T own’s website and social media.  
Thank you to everyone who has joined the conversation 
to date!
WATER IS A PRECIOUS RESOURCE 
EVERY DROP COUNTS
Water is essential to our health and well-being and a 
precious commodity.  At the T own of Ladysmith, we 
take our responsibility to provide safe drinking water to 
current and future generations with the utmost serious-
ness.  
Water restrictions will begin in Ladysmith and the 
whole Cowichan Valley Regional District on May 1.
Remember,  being water wise throughout the year can 
also save you money.  With a little effort, we can reduce 
the water we use at home by as much as 40 per cent.  For 
water-wise tips, visit our 
water conservation  page at 
www.ladysmith.ca.
