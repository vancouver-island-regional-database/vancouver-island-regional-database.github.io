Town of Ladysmith
Minutes of the Special Meeting of Council of the Town of Ladysmith held in Council Chambers at City Hall 
on Tuesday, March 3, 2009 at 4:00 p.m.
Mayor Hutchins called the meeting to order at 4:05 p.m.
2009-158: It was moved, seconded and carried that this meeting retire into Executive 
Session (6:00 p.m.) pursuant to Section 90(1) of the Community Charter to consider the 
following items:
- discussions with municipal officers and employees respecting municipal objectives, 
measures and progress reports for the purposes of preparing an annual report under 
section 98 [annual municipal report];
2009-160: It was moved, seconded and carried that the agenda for the Special Tuesday, 
March 3, 2009 be adopted as circulated.
2009-161:  It was moved, seconded and carried to adjourn the meeting at 8:45 p.m.
Corporate Officer (S. Bowden)
CERTIFIED CORRECT: Mayor (R. Hutchins)
2009-159: It was moved, seconded and carried that the Executive Session of Tuesday, 
March 3, 2009 arise without report (9:45 p.m.).
