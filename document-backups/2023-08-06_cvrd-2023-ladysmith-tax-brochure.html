Message from the Chair 
 
The Cowichan Valley Regional District stretches from 
the Nanaimo Airport in the nor th to the Malahat in the 
south, and from the west coast of Vancouver Island to 
Valdes Island to the east.  All of this is divided into 9 
Electoral Areas and 4 Municipalities. Elected 
representatives from the 13 jurisdictions come 
together at the CVRD Board to work with CVRD staff 
to provide 178 local and regional services to the over 
89,000 people in our region.  
 
With a newly elected Bo ard of Directors and new 
senior management staff join ing the organization, we 
are well positioned to respond to the challenges facing 
our region, and continue to  provide a high level of 
service to our residents Although the COVID-19 
pandemic is now behind us, we face a new series of 
financial challenges related to the increased costs of 
inflation, rising interest  rates, and the continued 
housing crisis. 
 
We have made every effort to reduce the increased 
financial burden to taxpayers in our 2023 budget. 
However, the cost associated with the new regional 
recreation funding model approved by referendum, as 
well as the rising costs of maintaining our current 
service levels, have resulted in a 11.49% average 
increase to taxation by the CVRD. This percentage 
increase is in line with the ma jority of regional districts 
across the province, who like us, find themselves 
challenged to meet current demands while striving to 
be fiscally conservative. 
 
I want to assure you that our Board, will continue to 
advocate on behalf of all of the businesses and 
residents in the CVRD for support from our senior 
governments to ensure our region can maintain its 
high standards for quality of life, protection of the 
environment, and forward-thinking policies. If you have 
any questions, please don’t hesitate to contact me at: 
chairperson@cvrd.bc.ca 
 
Aaron Stone, Chair 
Cowichan Valley 
Regional Hospital District 
The Cowichan Valley Regional Hospital District 
(CVRHD) provides capital funding to Island Health 
for the following facilities within its boundaries: 
  
· Cowichan District Hospital 
· Cairnsmore Place 
· Ladysmith Community Health 
· Chemainus Health Care Centre 
  
Capital funding is cost-shared with the province on 
a 60/40 basis, with the CVRHDs portion being 
40%. 
  
Starting in 2010, the CVRHD has been putting 
funds aside to raise the Regi onal Hospital District’s 
40% share of the new hospital.  
 
Construction has begun in 2022 and it is 
anticipated that the hospital will be ready to 
welcome the first patients into the new facility in 
late 2028. 
New Regional Services 
Established 
Following public approval via a regional referenda 
in the 2022 Local Government Elections, the CVRD 
Board of Directors adopted Bylaw No. 4438, 
establishing the Regional Recreation Funding 
Services. 
 
The Regional Recreation Funding Services provide 
an equitable funding model for nine regionally 
significant recreation fa cilities, ba se on each 
jurisdiction’s usage of the facilities. The new 
funding model is being phased in over a three year 
period.
