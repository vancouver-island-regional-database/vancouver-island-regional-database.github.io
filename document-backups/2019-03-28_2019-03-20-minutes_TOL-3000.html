Minutes of the Parks, Recreation & Culture Advisory Committee Wednesday, March 20, 2019 at 7:00pm COMMITTEE MEMBERS PRESENT: STAFF PRESENT: Tim Richards, Chair Gord Horth Clayton Postings Tara Pollock Bruce Mason Robyn McAdam Councillor Duck Paterson Mike Brocklebank REGRETS: AGENDA MINUTES Moved and seconded: 2019-06: That Parks, Recreation and Culture Advisory Committee approve the agenda for the meeting as presented. Motion carried. Moved and seconded: 2019-07: That Parks, Recreation and Culture Advisory Committee approve the minutes of the February 20, 2019 meeting as presented. Motion carried. REFERRED BY COUNCIL Poet Laureate draft policy – Committee members discussed the Staff Report to Council and the Terms of Reference, recommending that the involvement of youth be included in the terms of reference. Moved and seconded: 2019-08: That Parks, Recreation and Culture Advisory Committee support the Poet Laureate draft policy as presented. Motion carried. Moved and seconded: 2019-09: That Parks, Recreation and Culture Advisory Committee recommends that Council consider including the involvement of youth in the terms of reference. Motion carried. Community Driven Capital Project Policy – Committee members agreed that the plan provides guidelines that would help both staf f and groups. The plan helps clarify deliverables and frames timely discussions. Moved and seconded: 2019-10: That Parks, Recreation and Culture Advisory Committee recommends that Council consider approving the Community-Driven Capital Project Policy. Motion carried.

Minutes to the Parks, Recreation and Culture Advisory Committee Meeting March 20, 2019 Page 2 NEW BUSINESS Regional Sport Tourism committee representative – The CVRD requested a representative from the PRCAC committee sit on the Regional Sport Tourism committee. Gord Horth volunteered to attend the next meeting. Lot 108 Fitness Trail – The committee discussed the Lot 108 Fitness Trail plan and agreed that it is a great starting project for various groups to work on together. OLD BUSINESS UPDATES Membership update – New applicants for the PRCAC committee are being interviewed by Council and will be in attendance at a future meeting. Arts & Culture Update – • A Tea is planned for April 17 to celebrate the new kitchen and washroom at the Ladysmith Waterfront Gallery. • The Vancouver Island Vixens and the Terry Boyle Irish night were both a great success at the Little Theatre. • Two difference summer camps are planned for children at the Little Theatre this summer. • ‘Rumours’ starts at the Ladysmith Little Theatre March 21st and runs till April 7th. Meeting adjourned at 8:30PM Next Meeting will be held at 7:00pm, April 24, 2019 at the Frank Jameson Community Centre.
