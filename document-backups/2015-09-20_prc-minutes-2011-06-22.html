Town of Ladysmith 
PARKS, RECREATION & CULTURE COMMISSION 
Minutes of the Commission Meeting held at FJCC Lower Program Room 
Wednesday, June 22, 2011 at 7:00pm 
 
 
PRESENT: Chantal Blumel, Chair; Will Vandergrift; Lorena  Mueller; John Perry; Lynne Brown; Bryon 
Adams; Glen Popenko; Lori Evans, Councillor; Patrick Durban, Staff; Julie Tierney, 
Recorder. 
 
ABSENT:   Lisa Bates; Brad Uytterhagen, Area H; Norm Flinton, Area G; Duck Paterson, Councillor; 
  Scott Bastian, Councillor, 
 
 
 
AGENDA APPROVAL 2011-09:  It was moved, seconded and carried that the agenda for the 
Commission meeting of Wednesday, June 22, 2011 be approved with the 
following additions:  
Photos of Runoff  
Strategic Plan 
 
    
MINUTES APPROVAL 2011-10:  It was moved, seconded and carried that the minutes for the 
Commission meeting held Wednesday, April 27, 2011 be approved as presented. 
 
OLD BUSINESS Forrest Field – Playground will be installed next week. 
 
Spirit Square Update  – Grand Opening will take place Friday, June 24 at 3pm. 
  
Kinsmen Park – $4000 raised during the Eggs Benefit. Construction  for Phase 2 
of the playground project is planned to take place in July.  
 
Dogs Off-Leash Park – Commissioners were given maps showing possible 
locations for a dog off-leash park.  Furthe r discussion will take place at the next 
meeting. 
 
Skate Park – the improvements to the skate park have been completed.  The 
celebration following saw a combination of over 200 spectators and boarders.  
 
NEW BUSINESS Special Occasion License Requests –   Commissioners reviewed the application 
and made the following recommendation:  
 
2011-11:  It was moved, seconded and carried that the Parks, Recreation & 
Culture Commission recommend that Council consider approving request for 
Special Occasion Licences for the Ladysmith Horseshoe Club for tournaments 
taking place on July 28-Aug 1, 2011 at the Horseshoe Club at Transfer Beach. 
 
Parks, Recreation & Culture Strategic Plan – The copies of the Strategic Plan will 
be provided to the Commissioners at the next meeting for review.
