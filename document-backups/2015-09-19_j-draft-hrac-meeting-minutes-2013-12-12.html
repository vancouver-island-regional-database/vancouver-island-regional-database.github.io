c.       Heritage Week 
A. Vincent reviewed HRAC’s Heritage Week activities from 2011 -2014, and 
summarized ideas discussed in the previous meeting. 
 
T. Hutchinson provided an update from B.  Knol, which included identifying 
buildings of interest to be included in the posters, and organizing an article in 
the newspaper by Ed Nichols on. G. Beltgens reported that the Rotary was 
interested in helping to organize an event.  
 
Mayor Stone suggested that HRAC could inform local groups (such as the 
Ladysmith Downtown Business Association  (LDBA) and Chamber of 
Commerce) about the theme and ideas for Heritage Week , and allow the 
groups to organize an event if they wish. HRAC will focus attention on creating 
informative posters and Heritage Week awareness.  
 
Members decided to cha nge the direction of the poster concept  to better 
reflect the general Main Street theme, instead of individual buildings. Archival 
street photographs will be printed at a large scale on posters . Photographs 
may represent various decades, or changes resulting from revitalization 
initiatives. Inf ormation on the posters will include unusual facts, personal 
anecdotes or other interesting information about life on Ladysmith’s main 
streets. 
 
 G. Beltgens and A. McDermid will discuss advertising possibilities with

 M. Craig and T. Hutchinson will discuss event ideas with the LDBA. 
 M. Martin will research costs and details for a banner across First

 A. Vincent will contact the Archives in regards to a  research 
honorarium for locating appropriate archival photographs and

 A sub-committee formed by B. Knol, A. McDermid, M. Martin and M.
