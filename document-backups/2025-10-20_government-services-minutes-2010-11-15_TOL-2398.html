Government Services Committee November 15, 2010 Page 1 TOWN OF LADYSMITH MINUTES OF A REGULAR SESSION OF THE GOVERNMENT SERVICES COMMITTEE MONDAY, NOVEMBER 15, 2010 – 6:00 P.M. COUNCIL MEMBERS PRESENT: Councillor Duck Paterson (Chair) Mayor Rob Hutchins Councillor Steve Arnett Councillor Jillian Dashwood Councillor Lori Evans Councillor Bruce Whittington COUNCIL MEMBERS ABSENT: Councillor Scott Bastian STAFF PRESENT: Ruth Malli Sandy Bowden Felicity Adams Erin Anderson Patrick Durban Joe Friesenhan Chris Trumpy Joanna Winter CALL TO ORDER Councillor Paterson called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m. AGENDA APPROVAL 2010- 107 It was moved, seconded and carried that the agenda be adopted as circulated. MINUTES 2010- 108 It was moved, seconded and carried that the Government Services Committee minutes of October 18, 2010 be adopted as amended. CITY MANAGER’S REPORT The City Manager provided a verbal report on activities taking place since her third quarter report. REPORTS 2010-109 2010-110 Solid Waste Collection It was moved, seconded and carried that it be recommended to Council that the waste collection fee be increased from $13 per household per month to $14 per household per month effective January 1, 2011. Fire Chief’s Report for October 2010 It was moved, seconded and carried that the Fire Chief’s Report for October be received. LADYSMITH

Trolley Report for October 2010 It was moved, seconded and carried that the Trolley Report for October 2010 be received. Animal Control / Pound Report for October 2010 It was moved, seconded and carried that the Animal Control / Pound Report for November 2010 be received. CORRESPONDENCE 2010-114 Elizabeth Newcombe, Central Vancouver Island Crisis Society It was moved, seconded and carried that it be recommended to Council that the correspondence from the Central Vancouver Island Crisis Society be referred to staff to determine if the Ladysmith detachment of the RCMP will be participating in the RCMP appreciation dinner on February 12, 2011, and that the matter be brought back for Council’s consideration.
