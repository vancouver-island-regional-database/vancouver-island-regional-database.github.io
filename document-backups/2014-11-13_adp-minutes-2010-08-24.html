4. DEVELOPMENT APPLICATION REFERRALS  
a. Development Permit Application 3060-10-07 
 524 1st Avenue – AYPQ Architecture 
 Proposed second storey addition to the J. Cochrane Building 
 
Planner, L. Brinkman, provided an introd uction of the proposed second storey 
addition to the J. Cochrane Building at 524 1st Avenue.  
 
Architect, A. Quek, provided information re garding the designs for the living space of 
the proposed second storey addition to the J. Cochrane Building and answered 
questions asked by the Members. 
 
Owners, Jenny and Cal Jarvis, explained the idea  of the loft style design and that it is 
being designed for themselves as their future residence.   
 
A review and discussion was held examining the proposal, and the panel was 
generally satisfied with the design and detail of the project. 
 
HRAC Member, A. Rogers spoke of the importance of the J. Cochrane building as part 
of the streetscape in the Downtown Specif ied Area.  A concern of the HRAC is the 
impact to the streetscape.   
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Advisory Design Panel advises Council 
that it supports the proposal (3060-10-07) for a second storey addition to the J. 
Cochrane Building at 524 1 st Avenue on the basis of it being used for residential use 
only.
