Question 1 – What prompted the Town to conduct this study? 
Answer: The study has been on the Town’s capital list for several years. This last winter, a 
relatively small slough of the soil was observed, so the RFP was initiated.  
 
Question 2 –  Are there any utilities on 2 nd Avenue and have they had any performance 
issues? 
Answer: Yes there are utilities, see map below. There have NOT been any performance 
issues. 
 
Question 3 – Are there any budget limitations for this project? 
Answer: The Town’s budget is $35,000. 
 
Question 4 –  What timelines need to be met for the design and/or construction of this 
project? 
Answer: There is not a deadline for this work, although a reasonable length of time should 
be included in the proposal. Construction needs are not yet identified. If the results lead 
staff to recommend a capital project, it will be in the 2025 budget at the earliest, unless the 
findings were urgent.
