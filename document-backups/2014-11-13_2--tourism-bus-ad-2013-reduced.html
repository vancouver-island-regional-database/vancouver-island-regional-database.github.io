Tourism has emerged as a significant economic pillar in ladysmith due primarily to the 
community’s stunning west Coast location, waterfront recreation, hiking trails, heritage  
downtown, and boutique-style shopping experience.

»   a natural 8 kilometre harbour, which offers spectacular boating and kayaking opportunities.
»   world-class artists, an art gallery, museums and boutique shopping.
»   nature trails, sports fields, parks, and Transfer Beach with extensive playgrounds.
»   a variety of accommodation options from small B&Bs to near-by lodges large enough for 
weddings and business retreats.
»   several dining options ranging from cafes, a bakery, and unique family restaurants.
»   Three marinas both private and community-owned, which continue to grow and  
expand their facilities.
