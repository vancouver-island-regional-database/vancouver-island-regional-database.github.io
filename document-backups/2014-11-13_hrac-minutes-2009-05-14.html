6.  CURRENT PROJECT UPDATES/NEXT STEPS  
a. Heritage Protection/Statements of Significance 
B. Laxdal presented his draft report titled “HRAC Report to Town Council” 
dated 2009/05/13 regarding the Community Heritage Register and 
Statement of Significance Project. 
 
HRAC members discussed the draft report and proposed project including: the 
role of consultant and town staff for a project of this nature; concerns 
regarding a lack of town staff resources for this project; and the scope of the 
project.  
 
It was moved, seconded, and carried that th e Heritage Revitalization Advisory Committee 
recommends that the ‘Community Heritage Register/Statement of Significance Project’ plan 
proceed along the lines of the currently identified scope (see HRAC report to Town Council 
2009/05/13); and that the Herita ge Revitalization Advisory Committee authorized the plan 
to be presented to Town Council with a request for its support, including its authorization for 
the Town to apply for up to $20,000 of funding from the B. C. Heritage Br anch under its 
Community Heritage Registers Program. 
 
b. Extension Mine Explosion Plaque 
J. Hinde presented options from “Achinback Cemetery Memorial Plaques” and 
requested feedback from HRAC members. HRAC members discussed 
possibilities for the Extension Mine Explosion Plaque.  
 
J. Hinde will present a plaque concept and cost quotes at the June HRAC 
meeting.

8. NEW BUSINESS 
a. Nanaimo Coal Town Festival 
HRAC members discussed the idea to participate in the Nanaimo Coal Town 
Festival to be held at the Diana Krall plaza in Nanaimo on August 29, 2009. 
 
b. Slogan: A View to Sea 
HRAC members raised questions and co ncerns regarding the origins of the 
slogan “A View To Sea” seen in advertisements promoting the Town of 
Ladysmith in magazines such as the Spring 2009 “Ladysmith: Cedar and 
Yellow Point” produced by “The Chronicle”. HRAC would like to ensure that the 
slogan “Heritage By The Sea” is maintained and utilized. 
 
Councillor, L. Evans will obtain more information regarding the slogan ‘A View 
To Sea’ and will report back to the HRAC.
