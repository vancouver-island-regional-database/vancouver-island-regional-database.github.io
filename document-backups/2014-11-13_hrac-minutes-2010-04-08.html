b. Heritage Street Signs 
 Staff circulated the most recent pr oofs of the heritage street signs and 
 requested Members’ opinions on whether the font should be changed to upper-
 case letters. Members agreed that the second proof looked fine and to keep the 
 signs with the lower-case letters. 
 
Members inquired whether the “1st Avenue” street signs could be changed to  “First 
Avenue”. Staff explained that “1 st Avenue” is how the street is listed in the Town’s 
street naming policy.  
 
6. CORRESPONDENCE

Members discussed which members would  be interested in attending the 
conference. A. Rogers expressed interest bu t will need to confirm. M. Martin will 
check with P. Blumel regarding his interest in attending. 
 
B. Laxdal noted that the Thursday session, “Condition Surveys for Historic Structures” 
sounded relevant to Ladysmith. 
 
7. NEW BUSINESS  
a. Meeting with Nanaimo Heritage Commission  
 M. Martin reminded Members that last May the HRAC had a joint meeting with 
 the Nanaimo Heritage Commission. She inquired whether Members thought they 
 should have a similar meeting this May. Me mbers agreed it would be  more beneficial 
 to hold such a meeting next Fall when the new Ladysmith Heritage Advisory 
 Commission would be in place. 
 
b. Strategic Plan  
 B. Laxdal suggested that the HRAC review the Heritage Strategic Plan at the next
