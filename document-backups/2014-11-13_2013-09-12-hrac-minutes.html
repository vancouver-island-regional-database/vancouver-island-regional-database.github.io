MINUTES 
Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission (HRAC) 
Thursday, September 12, 2013 at 5:00 p.m. 
Council Chambers, City Hall 
 
  
    
PRESENT: Chair – Jennifer Robinson; Members – Tamara Hutchinson, Gerry Beltgens, 
Allen McDermid, Maureen Martin, Ann Rogers ; Council Liaison – Councillor 
Bill Drysdale; Staff Liaison and Recorder – Angela Vincent 
 
REGRETS: Member – Roman Buta; LDHS Member - Bernardien Knol 
 
GUESTS: Lawrence Marion (Calypso Clothing) 
Cathy Gilroy (Ladysmith Air Cadets) 
 
 
The meeting was called to order at 5:00 p.m.  
 
1. ELECTION OF CHAIR 
Staff Liaison A. Vincent called for nominations three times.   J. Robinson was 
nominated and accepted the nomination as Chair. No further nominations were 
accepted.  J. Robinson was acclaimed as Chair. 
 
2. AGENDA   
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of September 12, 2013 be approved 
with the reordering of 6 (b) Air Cadet Photo Mural to 6 (a), and the addition of 6 (c) 
Community Heritage Register Update.  
                                                                                                                  
3. MINUTES 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of July 24, 2013 be adopted.  
 
4. COUNCIL REFERRALS/UPDATES 
Councillor Drysdale  explained that Commission appointments are currently being 
advertised, and a new HRAC member may be added by the next meeting date. 
 
5. SIGN/FAÇADE APPLICATIONS  
a.  Calypso Clothing – #1 - 11 Buller Street 
Members reviewed the proposed fa scia signage. The applicant, Lawrence 
Marion, answered questions from the members. 
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission 
approve the sign proposal for Calypso Clothing at #1-11 Buller Street. 
 
b.  Telford’s Burial & Cremation Centre – 112 French Street 
Members reviewed the proposed awning design and signage.  
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission 
approve the awning design and lettering for Telford Burial &  Cremation Centre at 112 
French Street, but recommend a slightly darker colour for the awning.

c.  Spice Hut Restaurant – 510 Esplanade 
Members reviewed the proposed signage and provided the following 
comments: 
 
 The amount and type of signage does not suit the heritage downtown 
or the building 
 The decals used on the patio resemble fast-food restaurant signage 
 The free-standing signage pole could be painted to better match the 
sign 
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission 
approve the proposed fascia and free -standing signage with the removal of all decals for 
the Spice Hut Restaurant at 510 Esplanade. 
 
6. HERITAGE PROJECT UPDATES 
a.  Air Cadet Photo Mural 
Cathy Gilroy presented the new photo collage design celebrating the 
Ladysmith Air Cadet’s 70th anniversary.  
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission 
approve the proposed Cadet photo mural design. 
 
b.       Transfer Beach Pictorial  
A. McDermid provided an update to HRAC members and discussed the role of 
archivist Christine Meutzner. Members agreed that she will be hired as 
Project Coordinator, assisted by HRAC members.  A contract and terms will be 
established.   
 
J. Robinson noted that local community groups such as the Kinsmen are 
discussing projects and funding for 2014, and recommended that HRAC send 
a letter requesting support for the Pictorial. J. Robinson will prepare the let ter 
and this will be sent to Ladysmith community groups such as the Kinsmen, 
Rotary, etc. 
 
A. McDermid noted the lack of Transfer Beach recreational photos at the 
Ladysmith Archives and suggested that HRAC consider an informal request 
for photographs on be half of the Archives . T. Hutchinson will advertise 
through social media, and A. McDermid will place a request in Take 5 
magazine. 
 
c.       Community Heritage Register Update 
Members reviewed the results of the scoring completed by HRAC members to 
create a priority list of Town buildings to be included on the Community 
Heritage Register. The highest ranked properties will have Statements of 
Significance prepared this year, subject to budget.

7. NEW BUSINESS 
 a. Harbour Heritage Centre opening 
The Ladysmith Maritime Society’s Harbour Heritage Centre is opening on 
September 2 7, 2013 . HRAC members have been invited to attend the 
opening. An invitation will be sent by email. 
 
8.      CORRESPONDENCE 
 a. Heritage BC 2013 Annual Conference, October 18–19, 2013 (Burnaby) 
The budget includes funding for up to two members to attend the conference 
in Burnaby. G. Beltgens expressed interest in attending. A. McDermid also 
expressed interest in attending if M. Martin is unable to attend. 
 
 b.  BC Heritage Branch 2012 Summary Report 
A. Vincent reviewed the Summary Report from the BC Heritage Branch and 
compared Ladysmith’s results to the province -wide results. Members noted 
that Ladysmith compares favorably to other municipalities, but lacks heritage 
events.  
 
9. UPDATES 
A. McDermid noted that Friends of Morden Mine (FOMM) would like a HRAC member 
to join as a liaison. He also provided information regarding the City of Nanaimo’s 
“Heritage Paint -out/Shoot-out” to be held on September 1 5, 2013.  Members 
expressed interest in organizing a similar event in Ladysmith. 
 
G. Beltgens discussed the ongoing Colliery Dam issue in Nanaimo as a location with 
significant recreational and heritage impact. 
 
A. Vincent provided an update on the façade development permit  for 521 First 
Avenue (CMF Construction), including the paint colour selection. 
 
10. NEXT MEETING 
Thursday, October 10, 2013 at 5:00 pm - Council Chambers, City Hall 
 
11.     ADJOURNMENT 
It was moved seconded and carried that the meeting be adjourned at 6:40 p.m.
