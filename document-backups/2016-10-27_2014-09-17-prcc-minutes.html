PARKS, RECREATION & CULTURE COMMISSION 
Minutes of the Commission Meeting held at Frank Jameson Community Centre 
Wednesday, September 17, 2014 at 7:00pm 
 
 
PRESENT: Will Vandergrift, Chair; John Perry; Chantal Blumel; Alison Bailie; Bryon Adams; Duck

AGENDA  APPROVAL 2014-14:  It was moved, seconded and carried that the agen da for the 
Commission meeting for  Wednesday, September 17 , 2014  be approved as 
presented. 
 
MINUTES APPROVAL 2014-15:  It was moved, seconded and carried that the minutes from the 
Commission meeting of Wednesday, June 18, 2014 be approved as presented. 
 
OLD BUSINESS Sports Tourism Region Select Committee – John Perry informed Commission  
that at the CVRD board meeting held July 30, 2014, a  motion was passed to 
establish a specific sport tourism function under the existing Economic 
Development authority, for  the purpose of developing sport tourism, and 
support for community organizations hosting events, throughout the Cowichan 
Region.   
 
NEW BUSINESS Review Position of Chair – Commission discussed the appointment of Chair. 
 
2014-16:  It was moved, seconded a nd carried that the Commission elect Will 
Vandergrift as chair of the Parks, Recreation and Culture Co mmission until Jun 
30, 2015. 
 
Request from Ladysmith Baseball Association (LBA) –  Rod McLachlan, Vice 
President of LBA presented their request to construc t a scorekeepers building 
and replace batting cage at Holland Creek Ball Fields. 
 
2014-17:  It was moved, seconded and carried that the  Commission 
recommend that Council consider this proposal and that Council request staff to 
confirm specifications prior to construction. 
 
Signage at Parks – Discussion on reduced speed traffic signs required at 
various parks.  Staff forward request to Public Works.
