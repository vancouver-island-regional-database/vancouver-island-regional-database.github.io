- SCHEDULE CHANGE DUE TO WEATHER -  
 
 
September 13th, 2018 
 
Dear Residents and Merchants of Ladysmith, a.k.a. Home of the “Best Street” in Canada, 
 
Due to the weather forecast over the next week, our Production was forced to take weather cover and begi n our 
interior filming on Friday, September 14th. 
 
Hedgehog Films Inc. would like to thank everyone again for welcoming “SONIC” to Ladysmith and everyone ’s patience and 
kind understanding during the creative process.  We have certainly been appreciative of the hospitality we have received thus 
far. 
 
Based on this decision, please review approximate times for how the remainder of our film activities may affect your daily 
routine: 
 
September 12th & 13th - Prep 
 7am - 7pm Prep continues as previously noted. 
 5pm-10pm work trucks begin to arrive and position themselves around the Ladysmith Inn along Buller St & 1 st Ave 
 
September 14th - Filming at 640 1st Ave 
 (Expect intermittent traffic control for up to 3 minutes) 
 7am - 9am Crew and essential work trucks will arrive in the area 
 9am - 8pm Interior filming of dialogue scenes will take place 
 8pm - 10pm Crew quietly packs up and trucks shuffle into position at the Baseball Diamond and lot behind the Travellers 
Hotel 
 
September 16th - PRELIGHT (possible change to September 17th after filming - TBD) 
 Between the hours of 730pm - Midnight our Director of Photography along with the lighting technicians would like to test 
the lighting to ensure all working properly. The lighting will make the area brighter than usual.  
 
September 17th - 24th - Filming on 1st Ave from High St to Roberts St & Gatacre St from 1st Ave to NW Alley 
 (Expected Full Road Closures) 
 Sept 17: 7am - 7pm Filming begins i 
 Sept 18: 5pm - 7am (Protecting for 3pm to 4am if we do not complete day shots from the 17th) - FX Atmospheric Smoke 
 Sept 19 – 24: Wed to Sunday 5pm to 7am - FX Atmospheric Smoke 
 Sept 20 – 24: Special Effects to expect, Atmospheric Smoke, controlled car flip and fireball 
 During our night filming, additional lighting will make the area brighter than usual 
 (Work trucks to be staged at the Baseball Diamond and south lot) 
 
September 25th - 29th - Wrap 
 A reduced crew will remain behind to restore Ladysmith to its original condition. (Some shops will have chosen to remain 
their newly painted colour) 
 
Please review map on the reverse for clarity of proposed closures, detours and parking. We have been mindful of our footprint 
and have requested only what is required to successfully complete our work. Also included are select areas where we ’ve 
indicated the use of ‘drones.’ They will not be flown up and down the street but will simply be directed straight up to obtain high 
angle shots of the “ Town.” We encourage anyone with particular challenges to reach out to us sooner than later. This will 
assist everyone with the process. 
 
Should you have any immediate questions or concerns, please contact me at soniclocations@gmail.com
