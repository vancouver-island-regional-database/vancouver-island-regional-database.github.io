POSITION DESCRIPTION 
 
 
 
TITLE:    PAID-ON-CALL FIREFIGHTER  
DEPARTMENT: FIRE AND RESCUE   
DIVISION:   CORE SERVICES   
CATEGORY:   FIRE AND RESCUE   
BAND:   PAID-ON-CALL  
 
 
GENERAL ACCOUNTABILITY 
Reporting to the Fire Chief and assigned designates through the department’s chain of command, paid -on-
call firefighters serve a key role, providing essential fire and rescue serv ices to the Town of Ladysmith, t he 
Diamond Improvement District, and sections of Saltair.   
 
PRIMARY DUTIES AND ACCOUNTABILITIES 
o Combat, extinguish, and prevent fires and sav e life and property within the Ladysmith Fire Protection 
area to department standards.  
o Participate in training as required by the department training program . 
o Assist with fire prevention programs including public education, fire and life safety inspections, and fire 
cause determination as assigned. 
o Diligently attend and participate in all required meetings and practices. 
o Perform all duties in accordance with fire department operational guidelines, rules and regulations. 
o Familiarize oneself with the handling, care and maintenance of all department equipment . 
o Carry a radio pager at all times when in the Ladysmith Fire Protection Area and attend promptly when 
the alarm is sounded. 
o Lay and connect hose, direct water streams, raise and climb ladders . 
o Use portable extinguishers, self-contained breathing apparatus, and all other firefighting, rescue, and 
safety tools and equipment. 
o Search for and rescue persons from danger and provide emergency medical care.  
o Ventilate premises to release heat and smoke; places salvage covers to prevent water damage.  
o Drive and operate motor driven firefighting equipment as assigned. 
o Receive and record telephone and other types of emergency alarms and dispatches apparatus, 
equipment and labour. 
o Remain at the scene of an emergency or call-out unless given permission to leave by the officer in 
charge. 
o Return after calls and practices to assist in cleaning of equipment and making the apparatus and 
equipment ready for the next alarm; reports the loss or damage of apparatus or equipment.  
o Clean and maintain own equipment and ensure it is ready for use.  
o Serve on any committee to which they may be elected or appointed.  
o Make recommendations to the Fire Chief for the good of the department.  
o Actively participate in training as directed by the Training Officer or Fire Chief.  
o Perform related duties as required. 
 
REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS, KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS AND ABILITY  
1. Must reside within the Ladysmith Fire Protection Area, be a m inimum of eighteen (18) year of age or 
older and be legally permitted to work in Canada. 

 
 
2. Valid B.C. driver’s license - Class 5 with an ability to qualify for and maintain an air brake endorsement. 
3. Demonstrated agility and strength to complete prolonged and arduous tasks under adverse conditions. 
4. Demonstrated initiative with minimum direction; completing tasks by removing barriers and locating 
necessary resources. 
5. Demonstrated ability to communicate effectively in a wide variety of situations applying diplomacy and 
interpersonal skill to establish and maintain productive relationships.  
6. Demonstrated high level of focus on internal and external client service continuously seeking ways to 
meet and exceed expectations. 
7. Demonstrated ability maintaining, leading, and working cooperatively within a team and with Town 
employees, stakeholders and partners to achieve optimal results.  
8. Demonstrated ability to react quickly to any emergency in a calm and competent manner with excellent 
decision-making abilities. 
9. Demonstrates valuing diversity.
