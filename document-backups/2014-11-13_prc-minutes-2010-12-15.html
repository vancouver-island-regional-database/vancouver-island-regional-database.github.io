Town of Ladysmith 
PARKS, RECREATION & CULTURE COMMISSION 
Minutes of the Commission Meeting held at George’s Restaurant 
Wednesday, December 15, 2010 at 6:00pm 
 
 
ATTENDANCE Chantal Blumel, Chair Lisa Bates Pat Durban, Director 
 Glen Popenko Lorena Mueller Julie Tierney, Recorder 
 Will Vandergrift John Perry 
 
ABSENT Steve Arnett, Councillor Brad Uytterhagen 
 Duck Paterson, Councillor 
 
 
AGENDA APPROVAL 2010-21:  It was moved, seconded and carried that the agenda for the 
Commission meeting of Wednesday, December 15, 2010 be approved as 
presented.    
 
MINUTES APPROVAL 2010-22:  It was moved, seconded and carried that the minutes for the 
Commission meeting held Wednesday, November 17, 2010 be approved as 
presented. 
 
OLD BUSINESS Lot 108 Update –  Commissioners discussed personal observations of field use 
noting some inconsistency with users complying to the posted rules.  
 
The inclement weather has necessitated a sediment control plan for the field and 
surrounding property. The artificial turf remains in good condition and has not 
had any pooling due to rain.   
 
Staff will provide commissioners, in preparation for the next meeting, the list of 
names proposed for naming the new field complex.  Commissioners who would 
like to be part of the Lot 108 naming sub-committee should contact Patrick 
Durban. 
 
Needs Assessment Update  – Commissioners Glen Popenko and Lorena Mueller 
provided questions to be included in the Needs Assessment, which will be given 
to staff for implementation with the final report supplied to Commission for 
review. 
 
Davis Road School PAC Request Update – no update.  
 
Spirit Square Update  – progressing and will be completed by March 31, 2011. 
 
NEW BUSINESS Gourlay Jane’s Park – Staff informed the Commission that due to many 
hazardous trees, this park has been closed  to the public until the issues have 
been resolved.  A staff report with recommendations to have an arborist 
complete the work necessary to re-open this park will be reviewed by Council on 
Monday, December 20.  
 
Meeting adjourned at 6:50pm
