Donald and Carol Fyfe-Wilson 
Use of Herbicides and Pesticides 
It was moved, seconded and carried that staff be directed  to 
prepare a  report on the use of pesticides and herbicides , and 
that the report include reference to other Vancouver Island local 
governments that regulate the use of pesticides and herbicides 
by the public; how such restrictions are enforced; whether any BC 
Regional Districts have implemented region -wide herbicide and 
pesticide bans; and what effect , if any, such bans have on local 
businesses.
