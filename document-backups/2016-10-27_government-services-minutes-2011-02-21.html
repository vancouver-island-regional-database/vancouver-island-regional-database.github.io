Public Input Period Prior to Regular Council Meetings 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Committee 
recommend to Council that support in principle be given to the 
establishment of a public input pe riod prior to Council meetings, 
and that a committee be struck to work with staff to establish 
guidelines and terms of reference for the public input period.   
 
Opposed:  Councillor Evans, Councillor Dashwood and Councillor 
Whittington. 
 
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Committee 
recommend to Council that Councillors Bastian and Arnett be 
appointed to form a committee that will draft the Terms of 
Reference for the Public Input Period.

MEMBER SUBMISSIONS 
 
 2011-021 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Registration for the Association of Vancouver  Island & Coastal 
Communities 2011 Convention 
It was moved, seconded and carried that Town of Ladysmith Policy 
No. 05-1790 B, Travel Expenses, be amended to increase the 
number of Council members authorized to attend the annual 
Association of Vancouver Island  Coastal Communities (formerly 
Association of Vancouver Island Municipalities) and the Union of 
BC Municipalities conferences from two to four, in addition to the 
Mayor.  
 
Scouting Event, February 24, 2011 
Councillor Whittington will attend the Ladysmith Division of Scouts 
Canada event on February 24, 2011.
