4. DEVELOPMENT APPLICATION REFERRALS  
 a. Official Community Plan and Zoning Bylaw Amendment Application  
3360-09-03 Holland Creek Holdings – Luke Kolk 
B. Childs left the meeting at 1:10 p.m. due to a conflict of interest. 
 
Planner, L. Brinkman provided a summary of the Official Community Plan and 
Zoning Bylaw Amendment application for 315 Holland Creek Place to the ADP 
Members.  A topographic survey of the parcel was also provided by Planner, L. 
Brinkman.  
 
The Advisory Design Panel discussed the application and asked questions of 
the applicant, L. Kolk. 
 
L. Kolk offered the following information: 
• Lower density with a larger square footage per unit was chosen. 
• The subdivision would be a 55+ retirement setting.  
• The cost of the units will be in the $350,000 - $400,000 price 
range. 
• Noise from Dogwood Drive should not be an issue because of the 
lower elevation of the property. 
 
Councillor, B. Whittington suggested dedicating a portion of the land bordering 
Holland Creek Trail to the Town, as a Park, as opposed to having a covenant 
placed on the Title.  L. Kolk stated he would support this option.

It was moved, seconded and carried that the Advisory Design Panel advises Council that it 
supports the Official Community Plan and Zoning Bylaw Amendment application (3360-09-
03), Holland Creek Holdings for a density of 13 units and support the site plan by delinea 
designs, dated August 28, 2009. 
 
  B. Childs rejoined the meeting. 
 
b. Development Permit Application 
3060-09-09 R. Jackson – White Street and Esplanade Avenue 
Planner, L. Brinkman presented a review of the application for the 11 unit 
project, consisting of a duplex, triplex and sixplex on the corner of White Street 
and Esplanade Avenue. 
 
Planner, L. Brinkman explained the prop osed setbacks which do not meet the 
required setbacks of the R-3 zone. The proposed setbacks are 4.5 metres 
from the front lot line, and 3 metres from the rear and side lot lines. 
 
The ADP asked questions of the project architect, A. Lowe, and discussed the 
proposed plans. 
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Advisory Design Panel advises Council that it 
supports the reduced setbacks for the Development Permit application (3060-09-09). 
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Advisory Design Panel advises Council it 
supports the design as presented for the Development Permit application (3060-09-09), 
White Street and Esplanade Avenue. 
 
c. Development Permit Application 
3060-09-10 Ladysmith Maritime Society – Visitor Centre 
Planner, L. Brinkman provided a summary of the Development Permit 
application (3060-09-10) for the Ladysmith Maritime Society - Visitor Centre. 
 
D. Ehrismann, Executive Director of the Ladysmith Maritime Society, explained 
the uses of the Visitor Centre at the community marina and the specifics of 
the proposed building.  P. Polkinghorn, designer, presented the plans for the 
proposed Visitor Centre. 
 
After a discussion, the members of the Advisory Design Panel had the 
following concerns: 
• No “WOW” factor. 
• Nothing showing West Coast design. 
• Doesn’t look like a Visitor Centre - when approached from the water 
it should be clear where to go (presenting entrance to Ladysmith 
from the water). 
• Would like to see a break up of the massing. 
• Add green building design – water retention (opportunity of interior   
savings). 
 

Advisory Design Panel 
October 13, 2009 
 3
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Advisory Design Panel does not support the 
design as presented for the Development Permit application (3060-09-10), Ladysmith 
Maritime Society – Visitor Centre.
