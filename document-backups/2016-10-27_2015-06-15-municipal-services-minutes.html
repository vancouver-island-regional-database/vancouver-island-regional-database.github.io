Ladysmith Golf Club Maintenance Partnering Agreement 
Moved and seconded: 
That the Committee recommend that Council: 
1. Approve the request from the Ladysmith Golf Club to extend the 
current maintenance agreement with the Town of Ladysmith for 
the provision of maintenance services for the Ladysmith Golf 
Club with no associated fees for a further two-year period; and 
2. Authorize the Mayor and Corporate Officer to sign the renewal 
agreement. 
Motion carried.
