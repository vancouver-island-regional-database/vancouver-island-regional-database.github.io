MS 2015-004   
 
 
 
Low Barrier Access to Showers for Citizens with Limited Means 
Moved and seconded: 
That the Committee recommend that Council direct staff to amend 
Town of Ladysmith Community Centre and Facilities Fees and 
Charges Bylaw 2014, No. 1846 to include provision for a “shower 
only” admissions fee at the rate of half the current adult admission.
