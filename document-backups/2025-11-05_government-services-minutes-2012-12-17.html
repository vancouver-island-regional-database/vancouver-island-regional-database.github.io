 Proposed Bar Watch Program in Ladysmith 
 GS 2012-139 It was moved, seconded and carried that it be recommended to 
Council that Council refer consideration of the proposed Bar Watch 
Program in Ladysmith and the draft Good Neighbours agreement 
as presented to Council on November 26, 2012 to Staff and that 
Staff be directed to review this matter with the RCMP to determine 
if other jurisdictions such as CVRD Areas A, G and H and the 
Regional District of Nanaimo should be included in the agreement.
