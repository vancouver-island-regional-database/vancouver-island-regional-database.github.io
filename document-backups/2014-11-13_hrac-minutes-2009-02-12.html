MINUTES 
Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission (HRAC) 
Thursday, February 12, 2009 at 7:00 p.m. 
Council Chambers, City Hall 
 
 
LADYSMITH 
 
PRESENT: Chair – Maureen Martin; Members –  Bruce Laxdal, Ann Rogers, John Hinde, 
Peter Blumel; Council Liaison – Lori Evans; Manager of Development Services 
& Recorder – Felicity Adams  
 
 
The Chair called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m. 
 
1.         AGENDA       
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda be approved with the addition of 8 b) 
Take 5 Advertising.  
 
2.   MINUTES 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of January 15, 2009 be approved. 
 
3. BUSINESS ARISING FROM THE MINUTES 
a. Park/Building Naming  
The sub-committee met and concluded that a park pavilion was the best idea. 
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission 
recommend to Council that if and when a park pavilion is built at Transfer Beach Park, it be 
named the “Kay Grouhel Pavilion”. 
 
b.        Joint meeting with City of Nanaimo Heritage Commission 
M. Martin will arrange a joint meeting for May 6, 2009 at 5:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m.  
Location to be determined. 
 
4.         COUNCIL REFERRALS/UPDATES 
 a. Heritage Tourism Workshop  
 Councillor L. Evans reported that Council supported the Heritage Tourism Workshop.  
 
b. Commission Vacancies 
Councillor L. Evans reported that Council is reviewing applications.  
 
5.  SIGN/FAÇADE APPLICATIONS 
 None 
 
6.  CURRENT PROJECT UPDATES/NEXT STEPS  
a. Heritage Tourism Workshop  
A. Rogers reported that the project is going well.  About 15 people have confirmed;  
expecting more. 
 
A $1200.00 grant has been received from Heritage BC for the facilitator.

F. Adams reviewed a draft budget.
