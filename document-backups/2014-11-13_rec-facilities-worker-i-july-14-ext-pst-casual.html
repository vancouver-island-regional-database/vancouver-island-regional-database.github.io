TOWN OF LADYSMITH JOB DESCRIPTION 
 
 
TITLE:   RECREATION FACILITIES WORKER I 
 
DEPARTMENT: PARKS, RECREATION AND CULTURE 
 
JOB SUMMARY: 
The tasks performed by the Recreation Facilities Worker I primarily involve custodial maintenance.  This 
person is also responsible for taking various mechanical readings and would report any unusual items to 
the Recreation Facilities Supervisor. 
  
PRIMARY DUTIES & RESPONSIBILITIES:
 
 
• Maintains a high degree of cleanliness of Park s, Recreation and Culture Department facilities; 
• Makes routine equipment checks and reports any pr oblems to the Recreation Facilities Supervisor; 
• Opens various Parks, Recreation and Culture fa cilities and prepares them for use by the public; 
• Locks up Parks, Recreation and Culture facilitie s and ensures that they are safe and secure; 
• Sets up and takes down equipmen t as required by user groups; 
• Performs regular water tests and adjusts chemicals accordingly to meet Health Act and Parks, 
Recreation and Culture Department standards; 
• Deposits the Parks, Recreation and Culture da ily revenue in the night deposit at the bank; and 
• Other duties as required. 
REQUIRED SKILLS, KNOWLEDGE & ABILITIES: 
 
• Pool Operator I and II (or working towards the completion of);  
• Completion of Grade 10 or equivalent;  
• Valid drivers’ license;  
• Ability to work with community groups, organiza tions and individuals utilizing Parks, Recreation and 
Culture facilities;  
• Ability to work with custodial equipment includi ng floor burnishers and mechanical floor scrubbers; 
• Previous experience in custodial work is an asset; 
• A team player. Highly collaborative and collegial;  
• Stamina, energy, and willingness to commit to quality pro-active results; 
• Ability to work with minimal supervision and be self-motivated; and 
• A personal commitment to service, ability to prov ide friendly, helpful, knowledgeable, and timely 
assistance.
