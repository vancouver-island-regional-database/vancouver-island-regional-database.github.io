Should the Permittee fail to satisfy the conditions of this Permit, the Town of Ladysmith may undertake and complete the works required to satisfy the landscaping condition(s) or carry out any work required to correct any unsafe condition, at the cost of the Permittee, and may apply the security in payment of the cost of the works, with any excess to be returned to the Permittee. Requests for the release of the security shall be accompanied by a report from a landscape architect confirming that the landscaping conditions in this permit have been met. 10. Should there be no default as herein provided, or where a Permit lapses, the Town of Ladysmith shall return any security provided to the Permittee. 11.
