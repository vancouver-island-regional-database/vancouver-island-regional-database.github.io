This report provides a regular update on the progress 
towards Councils’ Strategic Priorities as well as a high 
level review of operational priorities. The report is 
presented at a public meeting to facilitate questions. 
Council Strategic Priorities for 2016-2019 are attached 
to this report for reference, including the focus areas 
for 2016. This report supports the Strategic Priorities of 
Prompt Communication, Transparency and Community 
Engagement. Further information is available on the 
T owns’ website at www.ladysmith.ca including the 
latest news and information, annual report, council 
agendas and minutes and other information. 
Follow us on Facebook facebook.com/LadysmithBC 
and T witter @T ownOfLadysmith. Feedback can also be 
sent to info@ladysmith.ca or by mail or in person at 
410 Esplanade (Box 220) Ladysmith, BC  V9G 1A2.
This is my final regular report to Council and I wish to 
personally thank all of the staff, Councils present and past, 
my colleagues in local government, the community and 
my family for their support over the past three decades. I 
am also pleased to welcome Guillermo Ferrero as the new 
City Manager, I wish him every success and I am confident 
that ‘working together to build a future’ is happening 
and is going to the next level. It is an exciting time for 
me personally and for the community of Ladysmith.

OPERATIONAL HIGHLIGHTS
1 Financial Plan Adopted The Financial Plan was adopted on May 2nd. Staff presented 
a draft budget in January and Council provided direction to eventually end with an 
overall budget increase of just over 3%. Council confirmed their direction of allocating 
10% of last year’s municipal taxes to capital projects and continue to set aside funds 
for asset replacement. Details of the financial plan are available on the website 
here: www.ladysmith.ca/discover-ladysmith/community-plans/our-financial-plan. 
Derelict vessels. Town continues to work with higher levels of government and 
impacted parties to address the issue of derelict vessels in the harbour. 
Fire Service Minimum training standards (Provincial Playbook), recommendation 
to May meeting. 
Lease Renewals Several underway.
Annual Report, Including the 2016 Community Profile June.
Downtown Banners Refresh program underway.
