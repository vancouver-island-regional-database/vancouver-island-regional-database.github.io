REFERRED BY 
COUNCIL 
Mid Isle Soccer and Rotary Club of Ladysmith Forrest Field Enhancement 
Proposal 
Committee members discussed proposal at length considering needs of field 
users, future needs and uses of the property, and facility planning for the future. 
 
Move and seconded: 
2017-19:  THAT the Parks, Recreation and Culture Advisory Committee 
support the proposal in principle and recommend that Council consider 
approving the proposed: grandstand improvements, player bench 
improvements and facility improvements;   
AND THAT Council consider engaging with past members of  the Field Steering 
Committee and current field user groups  executives to complete a 
comprehensive review of the existing draft site plan and take into account 
possible changes to trends, demographics and community needs to best 
determine the location and design concept for the proposed 
clubhouse/concession and present to Council for consideration.
