MINUTES 
Economic Development Commission (EDC) 
Tuesday, April 28 2009 at 7:00 a.m. 
Council Chambers, City Hall 
 
LADYSMITH 
 
PRESENT: Chair – Peter Richmond; Members – Rob Waters, Alan Burrows, Joan Phillps, 
Jeremy Lupien; Council Liaison – Steve Arnett; Mayor – Rob Hutchins; City 
Manager – Ruth Malli; Manager of Development Services - Felicity Adams; 
Recorder – Diane Webber 
 
ABSENT/REGRETS:  Moe Lessan, John Craig 
 
 
1. C A L L  T O  O R D E R                     
P. Richmond called the meeting to order at 7:12 a.m.   
 
2. AGENDA APPROVAL  
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of April 28, 2009 be approved. 
 
3. ADOPTION OF MINUTES  
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of the March 17, 2009 meeting be 
adopted. 
 
4. CHAIR’S REPORT 
Chair, P. Richmond reported on the following: 
• Along with J. Craig met with Marilyn Hutchinson, Economic Development 
Officer, City of Nanaimo.  M. Hutchinson shared information on Nanaimo’s 
business application and renewal processes.  Surveys sent out along with the 
renewal forms are found to be useful in helping local businesses. 
• May 14, 2009 American Consul General meeting from 12:00 a.m. – 2:00 p.m.  
M. Lessan, J. Craig and Councillor, S. Arnett will be attending. 
• Home Hardware Grand opening was well attended. 
• Participated in the Ladysmith Chamber of Commerce monthly “Visioning” 
round table.     
• LDBA is looking at local resident discount cards as a way to assist businesses 
during these economic times.   
• Met with A. McGilvary of Employment Navigators along with J. Surtees, R. 
McLeod and other members of local groups.  Funding is available to support 
local business programs such as management training. 
• Vancouver Island Economic Alliance report dated February, 2009 regarding 
collaborative workforce strategy also supports training as a priority.   
 
5. CORPORATE UPDATE 
Mayor Hutchins discussed the following: 
• Top 25 Council priorities.  
• Town projects related to economic development, including the Trolley service.

6. MANAGER’S REPORT  
F. Adams reported on the following topics: 
• Holland Creek Area Plan review.  Meeting with development parties and 
consulting team.   
• TAC tourism signage recommendations presented to Council. 
• Regional signage has been forwarded to CVRD. 
• Marine Sector news release and backgrounder has been sent to sector 
publications, local media and survey respondents. 
• Tourism Vancouver Island initiative to update Ladysmith content on the 
HelloBC website. 
• Take 5 plans to produce a new “Enterprise” magazine later in 2009. 
 
7. BUSINESS ARISING FROM THE MINUTES 
a. A. Burrows reported that he distributed the notes from the Heritage Tourism 
workshop to the members. 
  
8.   COMMITTEE REPORTS                  
a.  Tourism Advisory Committee 
• R. Waters reported that the committee met April 8th and will be meeting again 
on May 6, 2009. 
• Authors of a new book “The Most Beautiful Villages & Towns of the Northwest” 
will be visiting Ladysmith and writing an article on Ladysmith.    
• R. Waters, C. Bancroft, R. Gauthier and G. Millar met to discuss signage at the 
CVRD north end boundary.  C. Bancroft is working on a sign with wording that 
will include the Chemainus First Nation territorial lands and CVRD with a 
direction to the Visitor Information Centre. 
• C. Randall of the TAC has developed a plan for tourism industry development 
and education, including Superhost. 
 
b. Local Harbour Management Committee  
The committee has been awaiting a new Chief for the Chemainus First Nation.  
This election is taking place April 28, 2009.
