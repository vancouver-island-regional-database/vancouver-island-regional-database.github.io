NOTICE 
TIME CHANGE: 
COUNCIL MEETING 
 
 
Pursuant to “Council Procedure Bylaw 2009, No. 1666” notice is provided that the Town 
of Ladysmith Council meeting time for Tuesday, December 17, 2024 has been changed 
from 7:00 pm to 5:30 pm to accommodate an award ceremony and reception honouring 
former Mayor Robert Hutchins, who will be awarded Freedom of the Town as per Civic 
Recognition Program Policy 01-0290-A.
