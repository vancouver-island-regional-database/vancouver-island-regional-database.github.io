Responsibilities 
Citizens 
• Elect Council to conduct the business of the municipality 
• Provide direct input to decisions through participation as committee members or as members of task forces 
• Provide input to Council through the Financial Plan process, elections and ongoing feedback 
• Pay for services through property taxes and utility bills and other fees for services 
Council 
•Makes decisions on policy, service levels and tax levels 
•Directs the City Manager as to where resources (people and money) are to be focused (by policy direction and setting service level 
expectations) 
•Receives information from citizens and staff (technical) 
•Sets strategic corporate direction and goals 
•Directs the preparation, amendment and adoption of the Financial Plan  
City Manager 
Department Heads 
and Staff 
•Provide information to Council (such as recommendations on best practices, technical information, new funding 
opportunities) 
•Take action, within the authority and responsibility given in the Financial Plan, to conduct business in each department 
•Implement the direction of Council 
•Prepare the Financial Plan

Strategy A  
Wise Financial Management 
Provide fair value to taxpayer 
 
Priorities for 2014 
 Establish a Fitness Equipment Reserve Fund 
 Establish an Infrastructure Reserve Fund 
 Implement the Inter-Municipal Business Licence 
Program 
 Introduce a Property Tax Pre-Payment Plan 
 Streamline internal financial processes  
 

Strategy B 
Effective Land Use 
Within the Town Boundaries in accordance with 
Sustainability vision, OCP and legislation 
 
Priorities f or 2014 
 Complete the Zoning Bylaw Update  
 Implement Partnership and Services Agreement with the 
Stz’uminus First Nation 
 Commence update of the Waterfront Area Plan 
 Introduce Community Amenity Contribution Policy 
 Support Naut’sa Mawt Steering Committee 
 Continue Boundary Extension Application process as 
directed  

Strategy C 
Dynamic Economic Development 
Keep & foster local business, attract new business, and 
expand tax & employment base. 
 
Priorities for 2014 
 Support the Economic Development Partnership and assist 
with strategy development and implementation 
 Install new parking lot signage and downtown banners 
 Update www.tourismladysmith.ca website with a mobile-
friendly platform 
 Install First Nation place name signs 
 Support the Ladysmith Chamber of Commerce in 
establishing a new Business Centre in their new premises 
 

Strategy D 
Enhanced Standard of Infrastructure 
Maintaining roads, sidewalks, cemetery, water & sewer 
utilities 
 
Priorities for 2014 
 Commence Construction of Waste Water Treatment Plant 
Phase 3 
 Investigate replacement of Holland Creek Trail bridge 
 Create models for analysis of sewer and water network 
capacity 
 Implement sewer and water servicing agreement with 
Stz’uminus First Nation 
 Repair bank at Gourlay Janes Park 
 

Strategy E 
Responsible Steward of the Environment 
Commitment to environmental sustainability 
 
Priorities for 2014 
 Construction of Waste Water Treatment Plant (completion in 2015) 
 Restore habitat and improve the culvert crossing at Ladysmith golf 
Course 
 Complete the water system chlorination upgrade 
 Investigate options for water filtration 
 Install a weather station at the Public Works Yard 
 Complete watershed modelling 
 Complete the Master Transportation Plan 
 Implement the recyclables collection contract with Multi-Material B.C. 

Strategy F 
Safe & Healthy Community 
Continue to deliver services that support a healthy 
community 
 
Priorities for 2014  
 Maintain a safe and healthy community by ensuring 
adequate equipment is available to respond to 
emergencies. 
 Ensure the Town continues to provide infrastructure and 
services that meets the legislative requirements. 
 Utilize experts in conducting assessment on building, 
bridge and road infrastructure. 
 

Strategy F 
Safe & Healthy Community 
Priorities for 2014 
 Complete energy upgrades at the Frank Jameson Community 
Centre to reduce energy consumption and costs 
 Carry out ongoing upgrades at Forrest Field 
 Reviews fees and charges for Town buildings and facility rentals 
 Maintain Health Living Program with Stz’uminus First Nation 
 Enhance partnerships with community groups and organizations 
 Enhance cultural awareness and understanding 
 Implement parks maintenance efficiencies 
 Collaborate on sport, recreation and healthy community 
initiatives at a regional level 
 Increase participation in fitness and recreation programs 
 Investigate online registration and booking system 
 

Strategy G 
Supportive Corporate Governance 
Support activities of Council. 
 
Priorities for 2014 
 Conduct an Alternative Approval Process for a Boundary Extension 
Application 
 Local Government Election 
 Adopt a succession plan for the organization 
 Conclude contract negotiations with Canadian Union of Public 
Employees Local 401 
 Streamline service delivery in preparation for a new City Hall 
 Implement an organization-wide upgraded records management 
system 
 Install a new telephone system to replace the obsolete one currently in 
use 
 Continue to implement the Information Systems Strategic Plan
