BUILDING PERMIT TIP SHEET  
NEW RESIDENTIAL CONSTRUCTION 
Building a new home or a coach house? Undertaking a substantial residential renovation? 
 
This TIP SHEET will help you with your building permit application. If you have any questions or require 
any additional information or clarifications, please contact the Building Inspector.  
 
    BUILDING PERMIT APPLICATIONS : 
      Please ensure that ALL “must have” items are included with your Building Permit Application. This will help us to 
process all permit applications as quickly as possible. 
Incomplete applications will not be accepted. Review the requirements carefully.

• Information for Surveyors 
• Building siting is to be based on a survey prepared by a BC Land Surveyor (BCLS) 
DRIVEWAY ACCESS PERMIT APPLICATION 
• Driveway Access Permit Application 
• Please ensure that the driveway meets the Town’s specifications including the maximum grade of the driveway 
shall be 20% with a change in rise or fall not exceeding 6% for each horizontal 3.5 m. The rise or fall of the 
driveway shall begin 2 m behind the curb towards the property. Refer to the Town of Ladysmith Engineering 
Standard R10 – Typical Driveway Grades. 
• Ensure that a driveway location, length, width, elevations, parking area and profile are shown on your Site Survey
