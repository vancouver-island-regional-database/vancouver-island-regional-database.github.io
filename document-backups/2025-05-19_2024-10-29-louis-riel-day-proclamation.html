TOWN OF LADYSMITH 
 
PROCLAMATION 
 
Louis Riel Day 
 
 
WHEREAS: The Metis people are recognized as one of Canada’s aboriginal 
peoples; and  
 
WHEREAS: the Metis culture, rich in spiritual beliefs and colourful traditions, is an 
integral part of British Columbia’s multicultural character; and 
 
WHEREAS: throughout history, Metis citizens have made significant contributions 
to the development and success of our province; and 
 
WHEREAS: Louis Riel has made an enormous contribution towards the 
development of this country; and 
 
WHEREAS: British Columbia recognizes “Louis Riel Day”, November 16 th as a 
national Metis holiday in commemoration of the 138th anniversary of 
the death of Louis Riel. 
 
THEREFORE, I, Tricia McKay, Acting Mayor of the Town of Ladysmith, do hereby 
proclaim November 16, 2024 as “Louis Riel Day” in the Town of 
Ladysmith, British Columbia. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
________________________________ 
Acting Mayor T. McKay 
 
October 29, 2024
