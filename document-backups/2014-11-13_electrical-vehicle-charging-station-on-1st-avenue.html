NEW EVR-GREEN DUAL PORT 
LEVEL 2 ELECTRIC VEHICLE 
CHARGING STATION 
 
The Town has launched a new Evr-Green Dual 
Port Level 2 Electric Vehicle Charging Station 
for Public Use.  The Charging Station is 
located at 721 1st Avenue between Buller & 
Kitchener Streets (in front of the Museum). 
 
The Charging Station can charge two vehicles 
simultaneously.  Drivers access & energize 
the Station using a ChargePass Card or 
contactless credit card.  Please see 
www.chargepoint.com to sign up for service. 
 
There is no cost for charging at this time. 
 
Please contact us at 250-245-6400 or 
info@ladysmith.ca if you’d like to leave us 
any feedback on our EV Charging System.
