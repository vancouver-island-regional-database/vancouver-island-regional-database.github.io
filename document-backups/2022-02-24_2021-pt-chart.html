DID YOU KNOW? 
The Town of Ladysmith is only responsible for 
determining a portion of your overall property taxes. 
Altogether, 48 per cent of your bill is set by other 
agencies like Nanaimo-Ladysmith
Public Schools, Vancouver Island Regional Library, 
and Cowichan Valley Regional District. Sewer and 
water parcel taxes make up 18 per cent of taxes we 
collect and help to ensure the Town’s infrastructure 
is properly maintained.
