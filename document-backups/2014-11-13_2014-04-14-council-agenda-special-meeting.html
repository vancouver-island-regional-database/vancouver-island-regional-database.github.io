DELEGATIONS Lori Iannidinardo, Nick Versteeg 
Cittaslow Cowichan Bay 
CVRD Area D Director Lori Iannidinardo and Nick Versteeg, 
President of Cittaslow Cowichan Bay gave a presentation 
regarding the international Cittaslow sustainability and slow foods 
movement, and the local organization in Cowichan Bay, pointing 
out that Cowichan Bay was the first community in North America 
to achieve Cittaslow designation from the international 
organization. 
 
Council asked questions about the program and thanked Ms. 
Iannidinardo and Mr. Versteeg for their presentation. 
 
 
  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
CS 2014-118  
 
Rodney Szasz 
Motorized Vehicles in Ladysmith Watershed 
Rodney Szasz reviewed a letter he had written to Council outlining 
his concerns about the effects of motorized vehicles, especially 
all-terrain vehicles, in the Ladysmith watershed.  He reviewed a 
series of photographs that he had taken to illustrate the damage 
done by vehicles, as well as garbage dumping in the area.   
 
Moved and seconded: 
That Council request staff to review the matter of vehicle/ATV use 
in the L adysmith watershed, as well as the recommendations 
contained in the correspondence from Rodney Szasz received on 
March 24, 2014, in consultation with the Ladysmith Sportsmen’s 
Club and other trail users, and in the context of Oliver Brandes’ 
“Blueprint for Watershed Governance in British Columbia”. 
Motion carried.
