geNerAL INfOrmATION 
This application package is for home owners with a tax 
deferment agreement who wish to add their spouse to title 
and continue deferring their property taxes.
Note:  A spouse is a person who is married to another 
person, or a person who is living with another person in 
a marriage-like relationship and has been living in that 
relationship for a continuous period of at least two years.
If you are a home owner with a tax deferment agreement, 
you may be able to add your spouse to the title of your 
home as a tenant in common, or joint tenant, without having 
to first repay your tax deferment loan.  The portion of your 
interest in the home that is being transferred must be less 
than 100%.  
Note:  If you intend to remove yourself entirely from the title 
of your home, you will have to repay your tax deferment 
loan.  Contact our office for a payout amount. 
To add your spouse to the tax deferment agreement,  
please complete an Application for Adding a Spouse to a 
Property Tax Deferment Agreement (form 1A).  You and 
your spouse, along with any other person who is on title as 
an owner of your home, will also need to sign the Amending 
Agreement (form 4) attached to the form 1A.
Both of the forms need to be sent to the above mailing 
address after they have been completed and signed.  If 
your application is approved, we will return the Amending 
Agreement to you (or your lawyer or notary, if applicable) 
with a letter consenting to the transfer of a portion of 
your interest in the home to your spouse.  The Amending 
Agreement and the consent letter need to be filed at the 
land title office (or Manufactured Home Registry office if you 
have a manufactured home) with the appropriate property 
transfer forms.
When your spouse is added to the tax deferment agreement, 
you will become jointly responsible for all property taxes, 
interest charges and administration fees deferred under it.  
This includes any taxes, interest and fees deferred before 
your spouse was added to the title of the home.
It is important that your spouse understands the debt they 
are taking on by being added to the tax deferment 
agreement.  They should review the latest statement of 
account, which is sent out each spring showing the balance 
owing as of March 31, as well as your copy of the tax 
deferment agreement.  If you would like to know the current 
balance of your account, please phone the general inquiry 
number noted above.  
This information is for your convenience and guidance 
and is not a replacement for the legislation.  The Land Tax 
Deferment Act and Regulations are on our website.

We, the undersigned owner/owners (the Owner) of the property identified in the application (the Property) and the undersigned spouse 
(the Spouse) of the/an Owner, acknowledge that:
• the Owner made an agreement (the Deferment Agreement) under the Land Tax Deferment Act (the Act), with Her Majesty the Queen in 
right of British Columbia (the Province), as represented by the minister responsible for the administration of the Act, permitting the Owner 
to defer property taxes on the Property;
• the Deferment Agreement has been deposited at the Tax Deferment Office and bears Agreement Number            , 
 as assigned to it by the Province; 
• the Spouse is married to the Owner, 
 , or is and, 
for a continuous period of at least two years, has been living with that Owner in a marriage-like relationship;
• the Spouse has read both the entire Deferment Agreement and the most recent statement of account setting out the amount 
outstanding under it;
• we have requested the minister responsible for the administration of the Act to enter into this Amending Agreement and wish to amend the 
Deferment Agreement to add the Spouse as a party to it.
Therefore, we agree with the Province as follows:
• The Deferment Agreement is amended to add the Spouse as a signatory to it, by adding the name of the Spouse in one of the spaces 
provided for an owner’s name.
• The Spouse agrees to be bound by and to perform, satisfy and discharge all of the obligations, liabilities and duties under the Deferment 
Agreement, as hereby amended, as if he/she were an original signatory to it.  Without limiting the generality of the foregoing, the Spouse, 
together with the Owner, shall be liable for all taxes, interest, administration fees and charges that, at any time prior to the time at which 
this Amending Agreement takes effect, have been or may be deferred under the Deferment Agreement. 
• This Amending Agreement shall be read together with the Deferment Agreement, and we confirm that, except as modified by this 
Amending Agreement, all covenants, terms and conditions in the Deferment Agreement shall remain unchanged, unmodified and in full 
force and effect.
• This Amending Agreement will take effect when a transfer, to the Spouse, of a portion of the interest of the Owner, 
              , is registered in the land title office or given effect 
by the filing of a notice of transfer under the Manufactured Home Act.   
We authorize the Province to:
• obtain the Parcel Identifier (P.I.D.) Number(s), registered owner information and legal description of the Property, or the details of the 
manufactured home that correspond with the Property and to endorse that information on this Amending Agreement with the intent that 
the endorsement will form part of this Amending Agreement, and, in the case of the manufactured home, endorse the information on the 
financing change statement;
• register this Amending Agreement as a modification to the registered charge created when the Deferment Agreement was registered in 
the land title office, or, in the case of a manufactured home, to register a financing change statement amending the financing statement 
registered at the Personal Property Registry when the Deferment Agreement was originally made.
