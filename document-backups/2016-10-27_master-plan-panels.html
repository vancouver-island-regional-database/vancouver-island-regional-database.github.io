TOWN OF LADYSMITH PARKS, 
RECREATION & CULTURE MASTER PLAN 
PURPOSE OF THE OPEN HOUSE:
The master plan consulting team has been retained by the Town of Ladysmith to update the 2001 Parks, 
Recreation, & Culture Master Plan. This open house will allow you to provide feedback on the work that has 
been done so far and to review the community input to-date.
WHAT STAGE OF THE MASTER PLAN PROCESS ARE WE AT?
We have undertaken the following items:
•	 An inventory and analysis of the parks, recreation and culture facilities and programs
•	 A review of trends
•	 A community survey
•	 Facilitation of focus group sessions
•	 Conducted key informant interviews
•	 A review of the demographics, trends, and challenges impacting the Town of Ladysmith
•	 The creation of a parks map
•	 The consolidation of community input and recommendations
HOW CAN YOU CONTRIBUTE?
Based on the work to date, we have prepared a list which includes community input and recommenda-
tions pertaining to parks, recreation, and culture in the Town of Ladysmith. 
Please provide your feedback on the community input and recommendations in one of the following ways:
•	 Talk to one of our representatives
•	 Write down your comments on our short questionnaire
•	 Place provided dots on the panels next to the community input and recommendations you agree with.
The Town of Ladysmith Council will be provided the community feedback and input received, along with 
recommendations for consideration. 
THANK YOU!
