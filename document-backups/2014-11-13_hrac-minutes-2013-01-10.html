4. COUNCIL REFERRALS 
 
 a. Monopole Tower Proposal – TELUS building, 11 Roberts Street 
R. Basi of the Altus Group, representing TELUS, presented the proposed 
TELUS monopole tower, to be located at 11 Roberts Street. The proposed 
wireless communication tower is 14.9m high and contains the antennas in 
the pole’s interior. 
 
Three siting options at 11 Roberts Street were provided for the towe r and 
ancillary equipment cabinets , including alternatives with reduced visibility 
from the street, retention of the existing garage, and retention of the mature 
tree on site.  HRAC members reviewed the siting options and sample visual 
impact photos. 
 
The Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission provide d the following 
recommendations to Council for the proposed TELUS Monopole Tower at 11 
Roberts Street: 
1. That the tower be located at Siting Option C;  
2. That the tower be painted dark green to reduce visual impact; and, 
3. That TELUS also consult with the surrounding neighbours. 
 
 b. Outstanding Citizen Display

6. HERITAGE PROJECT UPDATES 
 a. Transfer Beach Pictorial 
 
A. McDermid reported on  his meeting with Mayor Hutchins regarding the 
Transfer Beach Pictorial, and provided a summary of the project for the newer 
members. The pictorial will focus on the historical use of Transfer Beach, in 
particular by the coal industry. 
 
Members discussed ideas for the physical layout of the pictorial, and agreed 
that it would be beneficial to proceed with the creation of a design to (a) help 
inform the pictorial’s content, and (b) assist in securing sources of funding. A. 
Vincent will pursue options for a professional design. 
 
The Transfer Beach Pictorial sub -committee intends to meet on January 30 th 
at the La dysmith Archives to continue work on the content required.  G. 
Beltgens joined the sub-committee.  
 
 b. Heritage Inventory Digitization 
 
A. Vincent provided an update of the photo digitization work completed by the 
Ladysmith Archives. All images contained in the 1990 Heritage Inventory 
have been digitized at a high r esolution, along with the digitization of many 
other photos that may be useful in the updated heritage inventory. Volunteers 
at the Archives completed the task in a short time period, and are 
commended by HRAC members for their hard work. 
 
 c. Heritage Week 
 
Heritage Week  will be held February 18 th to 24 th, and the 2013 theme is 
“Good Neighbours – Heritage Homes and Neighbourhoods”. Members 
discussed options and decided to highlight the uniqueness of Ladysmith’s 
miner’s cottages , transportation of homes,  and former cultural 
neighbourhoods. The Ladysmith Archives will be approached to determine 
interest in conduct ing research into these topics. The research will be 
presented in a poster format to be displayed at several locations in Town, 
such as the libra ry, FJCC, City Hall and 49 th Parallel Grocery Store. Members 
also discussed coordinating with the Chronicle to raise awareness of 
Ladysmith’s heritage homes and the Heritage Week displays.
