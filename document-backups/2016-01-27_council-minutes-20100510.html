2010 – 2014 FINANCIAL PLAN 
 
  
The City Manager, R. Malli, circulated two tax rate options 
for Council’s consideration.  
 
Tax Increase
s      Industrial         Commercial     Residential 
Option 1        4.65%            3.75%            5.99% 
Option 2           0%            2.96%            5.99% 
 
(Over all increase of 4.45%) 
     
Option 2 would require the use of $50,000 from the 
Town’s allocated surplus.  Discussion ensued regarding 
the options as presented.  
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that Council approve 
Option 2 of the 2010-2014 Financial Plan which includes 
the transfer of $50,000 from allocated surplus.

LOT 108 SYNTHETIC TURF TENDER AWARD
 
 
It was moved, seconded and ca rried that the contract for 
the Lot 108 playfield turf be awarded to FieldTurf-Tarkett 
to supply Durospine Pro with Cryogenic Rubber Infill in the 
amount of $495,669.64 and that E. Lees and Associates 
be authorized to negotiate with FieldTurf-Tarkett to finalize 
contract details and documentation; and that the Mayor 
and Director of Corporate Services be authorized to 
execute the required agreements.  
 
LOT 108 PLAYFIELD PROJECT
 
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the purchasing 
policy be waived and that staff be directed to pay MacNutt 
Enterprises $35,776.13 for the removal of brush from Lot 
108.
