PRESENT: Chair – Marnie Craig; Members: Jennifer Robinson, Tamara Hutchinson, Bob Darling, 
Marina Sacht, Harald Cowie, Rob Johnson ; Alternate Council Liaison – Mayor Aaron

The meeting was called to order at 5:05 p.m.  
 
1. AGENDA   
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of September 22, 2016 be approved with the 
addition of 5(b) Façade/sign application for 30 Roberts Street, 7 (a) Recommendation for HRAC 
representative on the Advisory Design Panel (ADP), 7 (b) Heritage BC Annual Awards, 7( c) BC 
Heritage Week, and 7(d) Canada 150 celebrations.  
 
2. NOMINATION/ELECTION OF CHAIR 
 
Current Chair M. Craig called for nominations three times. Nominations were accepted f or M. Craig 
and R. Johnson.  
 
It was agreed that the election of Chair would be carried out by secret ballot.  Staff member M. 
Paridaen distributed, collected and counted the ballots and announced the result, with M. Craig 
receiving the majority of votes, and thus appointed as returning Chair. 
 
3. MINUTES 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of June 23, 2016 be adopted.

7. NEW BUSINESS 
a)  Recommendation for HRAC representative on the Advisory Design Panel (ADP) 
M. Paridaen introduced the terms of reference for the ADP. A . Rogers was the past 
HRAC representative but since she has stepped down from HRAC, a new representative 
is needed. H. Cowie volunteered for the position.   
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that H . Cowie b e recommended to Council as the H eritage 
Revitalization Advisory Commission representative on the Advisory Design Panel. 
 
b)    Heritage BC Annual Awards 
R. Johnson informed the Commission that there are 4 award categories in the 2017 BC 
Heritage Awards Program, to r ecognize outstanding and significant achievements in 
heritage conservation of individuals, organizations, groups , businesses and 
governments; and the deadline for Heritage nominations is November 4th, 2016.  
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that a BC Heritage Awards subcommittee be set up, comprised 
of M. Craig, M. Sacht and R. Johnson, for the purpose of preparing award nominations for the BC 
Heritage Awards, and that these nomination s be brought to the October H eritage Revitalizatio n 
Advisory Commission meeting for Commission review. 
 
c)    BC Heritage Week 2017  
R. Johnson shared Heritage Week ideas from the Vancouver Island & Coast 
Conservation Society. Upon confirmation of the official BC Heritage Week theme, HRAC 
can begin planning for the event. Planning for Heritage Week is to be put on the October 
2017 HRAC meeting agenda. 
 
d)    Canada 150 celebrations 
R. Johnson suggested some ideas for celebrating Canada 150: work together with LDHS 
to research and honour pioneer families in Ladysmith (the 245 phone exchange) over 
75 years; creation of a Ladysmith Historical Families book ;  oral histories of families; 
give heritage family members commemorative medals. 
 
It was moved seconded and carried that the H eritage Revitalization Advisory Commission 
recommends that Council authorize $250 toward the purchase of medals or certificates for the 
recognition of pioneer families as part of the Canada 150 celebrations.
