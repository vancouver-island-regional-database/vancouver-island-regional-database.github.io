LODGE ON 4TH   
 
Staff, family members and residents of the Lodge on 4 th were 
in attendance to present to Council their concerns regarding 
‘contracting out’ of services at the Lodge and their worries 
about quality/consistency of care with Health Employees 
Union employees being replaced by contract workers.  They 
requested that Council support them by adopting a resolution 
regarding the quality of care of seniors and informing 
Vancouver Island Health Authority, the Premier and the 
Ministry of Health of their support.  
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that: 
 
WHEREAS how we care for seniors is an important 
benchmark in measuring how our society treats its citizens; 
 
AND WHEREAS the Town of Ladysmith has an interest in 
ensuring that Ladysmith care facilities provide a high level of 
service to Ladysmith citizens; 
 
AND WHEREAS Ladysmith, like other parts of Vancouver 
Island, is a retirement destination for seniors who will require 
quality care, and demand for quality seniors’ care facilities in 
the region is only going to increase in the future; 
 
AND WHEREAS the contracting out of seniors’ care at other 
seniors’ care facilities in  the Cowichan Valley and on the 
Island has already been shown to be harmful and disruptive 
for seniors; 
 
AND WHEREAS continuity of care is extremely important to 
seniors living in care facilities, and staff and clients often 
develop very close relationships built on care and trust; 
 
THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that the Town of Ladysmith 
write letters to the offices of the Premier and Minister of

out of seniors’ care and urging the provincial government to
