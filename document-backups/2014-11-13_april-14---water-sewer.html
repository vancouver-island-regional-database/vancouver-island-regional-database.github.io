Water Utility Fees 

 It is recommended that Council direct Staff to draft a 
Water Rates bylaw to increase the quarter base charge by 
$1.05 and increase each step by 5¢, effective for the 3rd 
Quarter billing. 
AND 
 It is recommended that Council direct Staff to include in 
the 2014-2018 Financial Plan a policy of increasing the 
Water Rates, by bylaw, by $1.00 to the quarterly base 
charge and increase the each step by 3¢, effective each 
year on January 1st.
