Undertaking a residential renovation or addition?
Adding a garage, shed or deck?
This CHECKLIST will help you with your building permit application. If you have questions or require 
any additional information or clariﬁ  cation, please contact the Building Inspector at 250.245.6443 or by 
email at cbollinger@ladysmith.ca. Pre-application meetings are welcomed.
MUST HAVE ITEMS:
Once we receive these “must have” items, we will start to process your application. This will help us to process all 
permit applications as quickly as possible. Typical processing time once your application is complete is 3 weeks.
