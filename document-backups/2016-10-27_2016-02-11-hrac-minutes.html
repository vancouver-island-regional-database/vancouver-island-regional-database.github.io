6. NEW BUSINESS 
a) Planning for Heritage Week 2016 
  M. Paridaen provided an overview of preparations completed by the Town: 
 Heritage Week posters were produced and distributed;  
 Heritage Walk I and II brochures were reprinted and distributed;  
 A ¼ page colour ad was placed in the Take 5 Magazine;  
 Heritage Week Proclamation was made by Mayor Stone;  
 Announcement of Heritage Week was placed on the Town website and 
Facebook page , on the  Chamber of Commerce calendar and on the 
Explore Cowichan website.

M. Paridaen gave special thanks to A.  McDermid for distributing the poster and 
brochures to several businesses and organizations.   Commission members also 
took posters and brochures for further distribution after the meeting. 
 
       Commission members discussed other activities planned for Heritage Week:  
 A free Heritage Waterfront slide show will take place on Friday, February 
19th evening at the Legion Hall with Rob Johnson as speaker.  The HRAC 
Heritage Week budget will support refreshments. 
 B. Knol indicated the Ladysmith Mus eum will be open from noon to 
4:00pm on Saturday. 
 
b) 2016 Heritage Budget 
       M. Paridaen provided an overview of the draft HRAC 2016 budget request , as 
part of the 2016 Town of Ladysmith budget under deliberation by Council.   
 
The first priority for staff is to complete the Transfer Beach Interpretive project  
which is partially  grant funded . The next priority is to complete the building 
plaques for Community Heritage Register buildings. 
 
The members recommended that the Heritage Inventory Update projec t and 
Heritage Strategic Plan be their next priorities; and that the 2016 budget include 
funds for the Heritage Strategic Plan.
