MINUTES 
Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission (HRAC) 
Thursday, December 10, 2009 at 7:00 p.m. 
Council Chambers, City Hall 
 
 
   
PRESENT: Chair – Maureen Martin;  Members – Bruce Laxdal, John Hinde, Ann Rogers, Peter 
Blumel; Council Liaison – Lori Evans; Director of Development Services & Recorder – 
Felicity Adams  
 
ABSENT:   Jennifer Lush 
 
GUEST: Christine Meuntzner, Consultant 
 
 
The Chair called the meeting to order at 7:05 p.m. 
 
1.         AGENDA       
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of December 10, 2009 be approved, with the 
addition of New Business 7 a) Heritage Brochure,  7 b) Resignation. 
 
2. MINUTES 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of October 15, 2009 be approved. 
  
3.         COUNCIL REFERRALS/UPDATES 
            a. Heritage Street Signs. 
L. Evans reported that the proposed heritage street sign design was approved by 
Council. 
 
b. Community Heritage Register Records – Building Recommendations (see 5a) 
L. Evans reported that Council requested that the HRAC develop building 
recommendations for the community heritage register project. 
 
4.         SIGN/FAÇADE APPLICATIONS 
            None. 
  
5.  CURRENT PROJECT UPDATES/NEXT STEPS  
a. Community Heritage Register Update Project 
M. Martin reported on the process undertaken by HRAC members to prioritize 
downtown buildings.  C. Meuntzner visited the buildings and spoke to the important 
features of a Statement of Significance. 
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission 
recommend to Council that the five buildings for Statements of Significance for the update project 
are: 
- Nicholson Building 
- Island Hotel 
- Trading Company 
- Miles Brothers Block 
- Mainstreet Building 
 
C. Muentzner will provide draft material to S. Blumensaat for distribution to the HRAC 
members, by the February HRAC meeting or earlier.
