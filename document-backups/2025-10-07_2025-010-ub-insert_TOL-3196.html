MACKIE & COLONIA WEIRS 
REMOVED
The Town’s contractors, Ecora and Spider Mountain 
Excavators, have successfully removed the Colonia and 
Mackie weirs from Holland Creek, restoring roughly 1 
kilometre of the creek to its natural condition. The work 
was required to be done by September 16 to ensure 
fish activities within the creek would not be adversely 
impacted. The Town recieved a $2.75 million provincial 
grant to support the remidiation of three weirs along 
Holland Creek. Phase one is now complete, and work 
on the final weir is forthcoming. In the coming weeks, 
environmental specialists will carry out plantings and 
erosion control measures to wrap up this portion of the  
project. The Holland Creek trail system adjacent to the 
creek is now fully open to the public.
Learn more at https://www.ladysmith.ca/discover-

FIREWORKS PERMIT
PROCESS
Ladysmith residents interested in discharging fireworks 
on Halloween night must use the application and 
permitting process that is being administered by the 
T own.
The Family Fireworks Permit Application fee is $10. Sep-
arate permit classes, fees and insurance requirements 
apply for display fireworks and pyrotechnics.
The Family Fireworks Permit replaces the permit process 
previously overseen by the Cowichan Valley Regional 
District. Permit approvals are subject to the current 
Coastal Fire Ban and any corresponding restrictions.
You can obtain application forms at Ladysmith City 
Hall, located at 410 Esplanade, during business hours 
(Monday to Friday, 8:30 am – 4:00 pm). Forms are also 
available by emailing info@ladysmith.ca or by visiting 
this link https://www.ladysmith.ca/docs/default-source/
bylaws-2021/fireworks-permit-application_2022_fill -
able.pdf. Once completed, the forms can be submitted 
and paid for in person at City Hall.
