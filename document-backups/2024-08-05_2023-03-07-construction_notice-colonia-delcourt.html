NOTICE OF CONSTRUCTION 
Colonia Dr. & Delcourt Ave. Sidewalks & Traffic Calming 
 
 
March 7, 2023  
 
 
Dear Resident: 
 
The Town would like to inform you of an upcoming project over the next several months in 
your neighbourhood that will provide for greater walkability and overall accessibility.  
 
The Town has retained contractor, Stone Pacific Contracting Ltd., to install concrete 
sidewalks, enhanced crosswalks, curbs , as well as  road markings and traffic calming  
measures.  Additionally, a solar powered flashing crosswalk and one accessible parking 
space will be installed in front of Kinsmen Park.  
 
The project is designed to improve pedestrian safety by connecting existing sidewalks 
along Colonia Drive from Malone Road to Davidson Road, and along Delcourt Avenue from 
Colonia Drive to Dunsmuir Crescent.  
 
Construction is expected to begin , weather permitting, in early to mid -March, with 
completion planned in summer 2023. The contractor is mobilizing and beginning survey 
layout activities this week.  
 
Parking will be restricted in the construction zone between the hours of 7am to 5pm on 
weekdays.  
 
Please relocate any moveable/stored items including trailers, hitch extensions and 
vehicles away from the boulevard area where the new sidewalk is being constructed.   
 
The contractor will attempt to knock on your door to let you know when driveway access 
to your property will be temporarily restricted. 
 
During this period, you may experience: 
 
• Construction activity and noise; 
• Trench excavation in front of your home; 
• Increased truck traffic;  
• Traffic control activities; 

 
 
• Delays when entering or exiting your property. Traffic control personnel will 
provide assistance/direction during construction. 
 
Garbage/Recycling/Organics Collection 
 
Please continue to place your garbage/recycling/organics containers at the curb/boulevard 
for collection.  
 
We understand the construction will be an inconvenience for you and will do our best to 
minimize the disturbance. If you have questions about the project, you may contact the  
undersigned at Infrastructure Services, 250 -245-6442 or email me directly at 
iadams@ladysmith.ca . 
 
Regards, 
 
Ira Adams, AScT
