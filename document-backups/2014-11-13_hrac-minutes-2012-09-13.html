MINUTES 
Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission (HRAC)

Council Chambers, City Hall 
 
 
  
   
PRESENT: Chair – Jennifer Robinson; Members – Tamara Hutchinson, Allen McDermid, Peter 
Blumel; Council Liaison – Councillor Bill Drysdale; Staff Liaison and Recorder – 
Angela Vincent

The meeting was called to order at 7:05 p.m.  
 
1. ELECTION OF CHAIR 
Staff Liaison A. Vincent called for nominations three times. J. Robinson was nominated and 
accepted the nomination as Chair. No further nominations were accepted.  J. Robinson was

2. AGENDA   
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of September 13, 2012 be approved with the 
addition of 8 (c) Friends of Morden Mine update, and the removal of 9 (a) Heritage BC Quarterly. 
 
3. MINUTES 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of July 12, 2012 and August 9, 2012 be

4. BUSINESS ARISING FROM THE MINUTES 
a. Outstanding Citizen Display 
 
Members asked for clarification regarding the scope of work and budget for the 
Outstanding Citizen Display. Councillor B. Drysdale confirmed that Council would like 
the HRAC to brainstorm ideas and provide a basic cost analysis for the design. 
Commission members noted that while the HRAC can provide ideas for content and 
heritage-oriented design that will fit in the downtown, other community interest 
groups should also be involved in the project. 
 
The sub-committee intends to invite representatives from the Parks, Recreation and 
Culture Commission and the Ladysmith Citizen of the Year Committee to attend the 
sub-committee meetings. Member T. Hutchinson volunteered to join the sub -
committee.

6.       SIGN/FAÇADE APPLICATIONS 
 a. Cross & MacDougall Jewellers – 25 Roberts Street 
 
The application for proposed signage for Cross & MacDougall Jewellers was 
distributed to Commission members for comment via email on August 16 th 2012. 
Commission members liked the size, placement and cursive font of the proposed 
signs; however, th e color combination of royal blue and white was felt to be too 
bright against the orange tone of the building. The Commission suggested that the 
sign colors could be similar, but in a more subdued heritage palette that would be in 
keeping with the heritage building. Feedback was provided to the owners on August 
22nd 2012. 
 
The owners of Cross & MacDougall Jewellers were invited to attend the September 
13th Commission meeting but were unable to attend. The owners would like to install 
the signage on a tempor ary basis to allow the Commission to view the signage on 
the building for further comment. Commission members hope the owners will attend 
the next meeting to discuss the proposed signage. 
 
 b. Music Hall – update 
 
A. Vincent informed the Commission that the owner of 18 Roberts Street (the old 
Music Hall) intends to complete some basic façade upgrades to the building. 
Upgrades will include a fresh coat of paint (in the existing colors) and minor repairs 
to the heritage millwork on the façade. The owner was very interested in obtaining a 
heritage building plaque. 
 
7. HERITAGE PROJECT UPDATES 
a.   Transfer Beach Pictorial 
 
 The Pictorial sub -committee will set up a meeting with Mayor Hutchins to discuss 
ideas for the design of the pictorial. J. Robinson volunteered to join the sub -
committee.  
 
 Councillor B. Drysdale suggested that the sub -committee could prepare 2 or 3 
design options to present at a Town Public meeting. T. Hutchinson volunteered to

 A. Vincent noted that the Stz’uminus First Nation is interested in providing historical 
information for the pictorial. The Commission is asked to communicate via staff who
