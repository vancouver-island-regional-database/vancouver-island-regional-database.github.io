• Regular Council Meetings begin at 7:00pm on the first and third Tuesday of each month (except in January, August, September and October). • Committee of the Whole Meetings begin at 6:30pm on the second Tuesday of every other month (except in January). • Meetings will be held at the Ladysmith Seniors Centre (630 2 nd Avenue). • Meetings are subject to change, so please contact City Hall to confirm.
