MINUTES 
Economic Development Commission (EDC) 
Tuesday, March 17 2009 at 7:00 p.m. 
Multi-Purpose Room, Ladysmith Secondary School 
 
LADYSMITH 
 
PRESENT: Chair – Peter Richmond; Members – Rob Waters, Alan Burrows, Moe Lessan, Joan 
Phillps; Councillor Liaison – Steve Arnett; City Manager – Ruth Malli; Manager of 
Development Services - Felicity Adams; Recorder – Diane Webber 
 
ABSENT/REGRETS:  Jeremy Lupien 
 
 
1. C A L L  T O  O R D E R                     
P. Richmond called the meeting to order at 7:10 p.m.   
 
2. AGENDA APPROVAL  
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of March 17, 2009 be approved with the 
addition of 8 a) Heritage Tourism Workshop, 8 b) Environmental Scan and 8 c) Coronation Mall. 
 
3. ADOPTION OF MINUTES  
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of the February 16, 2009 meeting be 
adopted. 
 
4. CHAIR’S REPORT 
Chair, P. Richmond reported on the following: 
• Met with Ray Gauthier, Business Development manager, Chemainus First Nation.  R 
Gauthier expressed an interest in forming a working relationship with the Town of 
Ladysmith and would like to connect on a projects basis. 
• Attended the Downtown Business Association AGM and suggested that the members 
have a look at their website. 
• Suggested that the EDC support Employment Navigators with their Labour Market 
and job Creation project.  Potential for a Youth Entrepreneur Program was also 
discussed. 
 
The EDC members agreed that there is a need for skilled workers right now and it is a time of 
opportunities.  J. Phillips who is also a member of the Rotary Club mentioned that they are 
looking for a model for the “Junior Achievers”.  P. Richmond will mention to A. McGilvary, 
Director of Employment Navigators, that this could be a joint project between themselves 
and the Rotary Club. 
 
P. Richmond reported that the Chamber of Commerce has organized the Home Hardware 
Open House event, which is being held on March 31, 2009, to recognize Home Hardware’s 
new store opening. 
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Economic Development Commission contribute up 
to $200.00 towards the Home Hardware Open House event which is being held on March 31, 
2009. 
 
P. Richmond talked to the members about keeping the Chamber of Commerce up to date on 
a monthly basis on prime items from the EDC meetings.  He requested help from Staff with

5. MANAGER’S REPORT  
F. Adams reported on the following topics: 
• The TAC Marketing and Promotions sub-committee is working with Victoria’s Boulevard 
Magazine on co-operative advertising for Ladysmith within the Cowichan pages.  The 
focus is business and events advertising to attract tourism. 
• The TAC Tourism industry Development sub-committee is sponsoring up to 5 seats for 
Ladysmith businesses to attend a sustainable tourism workshop. 
• The EDC has had coverage at three trade shows; Boat Show, BC Real Estate Show and 
Agriculture Show with thanks to OBMG and Economic Development Cowichan. 
• Council has supported new artist leases in the Machine Shop.  The Arts Council and 
Ladysmith Maritime Society are expanding their spaces there. 
• The top 5 projects for the Development Services Department are:  
1. Holland Creek Area Plan Review 
2. Mobile Home Park/Affordable Housing options 
3. Tourism Plan Implementation 
4. DCC Bylaw project 
5. Economic Development resource review 
 F. Adams requested the EDC members to provide input on the resources they would like 
to see for Economic Development support. 
• F. Adams and P. Richmond attended the District of North Cowichan EDC meeting to share 
experiences working regionally on economic development. Their EDC consists of 3 
Council members and 3 Staff members.  
• F. Adams provided a draft copy of the Economic Development Cowichan Clean 
Technology Study to A. Burrows and R. Waters, members of the sub-committee on Green 
Industry. 
 
6. BUSINESS ARISING FROM THE MINUTES 
Ruth Malli, City Manager, presented information to the Members regarding business taxes.  
Members identified that the tax paid for a typical business is an important communication 
tool for business retention and expansion. 
 
7.   COMMITTEE REPORTS                  
a.  Tourism Advisory Committee 
Members reviewed the “Signage Report” prepared by C. Bancroft, member of the 
TAC.  This report contains information from studies and audits that the Town and the 
TAC have undertaken regarding signage.  The TAC provided five signage 
recommendations for the consideration of the EDC. 
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Economic Development Commission support the 
Tourism Advisory Committee tourism signage recommendations contained in the signage report 
dated March 11, 2009. 
 
Councillor, S. Arnett was asked if he could provide the EDC with an update on the Trolley 
System. 
 
b. Local Harbour Management Committee  
No report.

8. NEW BUSINESS 
a. Heritage Tourism Workshop 
Member, A. Burrows attended the Heritage Tourism workshop and will distribute a

 b. Environmental Scan

9. NEXT MEETING                   
The EDC agreed to change the meeting date to the 4th Tuesday of the Month at 3:30 p.m.  
The next meeting is scheduled for Tuesday, April 28, 2009 at 3:30 p.m.
