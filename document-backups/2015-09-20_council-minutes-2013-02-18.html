Canada Summer Jobs Grant Application 
It was moved, seconded and carried staff be authorized to submit a 
Canada Summer Jobs 2013 grant application to assist with the

It was moved, seconded and carried that staff be requested to 
pursue a Blade Runner grant for trail building and repairs. 
 
Councillor Arnett returned to the meeting. 
 
Nomination of Alternate Appointee for Councillor Duck Paterson to 
Cowichan Valley Regional District Board 
It was moved, seconded and carried t hat Councillor Bill Drysdale be 
appointed as alternate to Mayor Rob Hutchins representing the 
Town of Ladysmith on the Board of Directors of the Cowichan Valley 
Regional District for the duration of Councillor Duck Paterson’s 
medical leave, effective immediately.

It w as moved, seconded and carried that staff be requested to

Council agreed that concerns regarding RCMP funding could be 
raised as a late item at the annual convention of the Association of 
Vancouver Island and Coastal Communities. 
 
Council expressed concern that the increase to funding for the 
Vancouver Island Regional Library exceeds the typical increases to 
local government budgets.  Councillor Arnett agreed to take 
Council’s concerns to the Board of the Vancouver Island Regional 
Library. 
 
Council agreed that aging infrastructure is a concern to be 
addressed.
