In accordance with Section 127 of the Community Charter, the Town of Ladysmith’s Council Meeting Calendar outlining the date, time and place of regular Council meetings is available to the public by visiting the Town’s website at www.ladysmith.ca or City Hall at 410 Esplanade, Ladysmith, BC, between the hours of 8:30 a.m. and 4:00 p.m. Monday – Friday, excluding statutory holidays.
