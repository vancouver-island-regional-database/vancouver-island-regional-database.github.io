SITE SURVEY AND HEIGHT CALCULATIONS
Building siting is to be based on a survey prepared by a BC Land Surveyor (BCLS).
On the survey drawing (also called a location certiﬁ  cate) the following information must be provided:
• Building envelope which means an illustration of the minimum required setbacks from all property lines for
the Zone that your property is in.  Setback information can be found in the Zoning Bylaw.
• The location of all buildings (existing and proposed) with setbacks from property lines
• Rights of way, easements, covenant areas,  Streamside Protection and Enhancement Area (SPEA) which is
usually associated with a watercourse on or near the property, if any
• A note or chart including: 
Zone that your property is in (refer to the Zoning Bylaw Map)
Proposed building height (m)
Maximum permitted building height (see the Zoning Bylaw)
Average natural grade (as deﬁ  ned in the Zoning Bylaw)
Average ﬁ  nished grade (as deﬁ  ned in the Zoning Bylaw)
Parcel size (m2)
Parcel coverage (%) (calculated as per the Zoning Bylaw)
• Proposed retaining wall height, if any. A building permit is needed if the height is over 1.2 metres (4 feet).
(Refer to the Zoning Bylaw for height regulations.)

BUILDING PERMIT FEE
Building Permit Fees are based on the value of construction, plus other fees for plumbing ﬁ  xtures, driveway 
access permit, service connections, refundable bond, DCCs, as applicable.  
Building Permit Fees

If the building was constructed or renovated prior to 1990, you may  need to provide a Hazardous Materials 
report. Has a Hazardous Materials Survey as outlined in WorkSafeBC Guideline 20.112 been completed for this 
project? Is there a presence of any potential asbestos-containing or other hazardous materials? If so, the survey 
and evidence that the disturbed material was removed and disposed of properly must be posted at the worksite 
entrance so that the Building Inspector can inspect the project.
