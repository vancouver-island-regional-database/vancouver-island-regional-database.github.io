 2011-364 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Purchase of Custom Multi-Use Fire Vehicle 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the contract for the 
purchase of a Ford chassis Custom Multi -Use Fire Vehicle by five-
year lease through the Municipal Finance Authority be awarded 
to DEKL Equipment Ltd. For a total price of $78,445.00 plus 
Harmonized Sales Tax to be funded through the Municipal 
Finance Authority. 
 
Alternative Approval Process — Water Works Project 
It was moved, seconded and carried that in accordance with 
Section 86(8) of the Community Charter , Council accept the 
results of the alternate approval process as certified by the 
Corporate Officer and further that Council adopt Town of 
Ladysmith Water System Construction Loan Authorization Bylaw 
2011, No. 1763 to authorize the borrowing of $1,000,000 to 
fund the water works project. 
 
Secondary Suites 
It was moved, seconded and carried that  the relevant clause in 
Sections 4, 5, 6, 7 , 8 and 10  of proposed Bylaw 1774  be 
amended to read “A Bed and Breakfast use is not permitted in a 
single family dwelling containing a secondary suite, unless the lot 
size is at least 1,000 square metres (10,764 square feet).”
