LOCAL BURNING & WILDFIRE 
BANS
BC Wildfire Service prohibits campfires 
throughout 
the Coastal Fire Centre, except 
for the Haida 
Gwaii Forest District. 
These prohibitions apply to all public and private land 
and help prevent human-caused wildfires 
and 
protect public safety. 
In addition to open 
fires being prohibited, the 
following activities and equipment are also restricted:
• Fireworks;
• Sky Lanterns;
• Burn Barrels or Burn Cages of any size or
description;
• Binary Exploding Targets;
• Air curtain burners; and
• Tiki and similar kind of torches
.

WILDLIFE AWARE
Nature is right on our doorstep and this means always 
being mindful of how human activity impacts wildlife. 
At this time of yea r, wildlife can be spotted more 
frequently. Don’t leave garbage or compost outdoors. 
Use c aution when on the trails. If you see a bear or
