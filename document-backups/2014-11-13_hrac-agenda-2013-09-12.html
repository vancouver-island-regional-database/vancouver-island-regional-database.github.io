e. CMF Construction – 521 First Avenue 
Members reviewed and accepted the proposed changes to the siding material, 
including replacing the existing 3” cedar siding with 4” hardi -plank on the south and 
west-facing facades, with the exception of the lower portion of the façade to the 
west.  
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission support 
the replacement of cedar siding with Hardi-plank as proposed at 521 First Avenue. 
 
Members discussed the benefits of upgrading the existing façade colours to coincide 
with the new siding. The following recommendations were made:    
 
 Select a paint colour that is not used in excess in the downtown (i.e. not grey or 
blue).  
 Colour suggestions include sage green with off -white trim, or cream/yellow with 
blue trim, etc., with a third or fourth complementary colour. 
 Consider reducing the visual impact of a large building by painting as two 
attached buildings, or using different paint colours , shades, or material  in the 
upper or lower aspects of the building. 
 
Once available, members will review the proposed paint colours via email to hasten 
the process.

Heritage BC is on the move and  
it’s time to celebrate!
Heritage BC has successfully emerged from various challenges with a new 
and exciting direction.  We’ve got a new business plan, renewed funding 
and new responsibilities with the Heritage Legacy Fund grant program.  
On top of this, we’ve hired a new Executive Director, Kathryn Molloy,  
who has been handed the baton from our recently retired long-time 
Executive Director, Rick Goodacre.
Get prepared by previewing our new business plan online: 
A Sustainable Business Model for Heritage BC.  An entire session with your 
new Executive Director is devoted to getting your feedback on the new 
plan – hard copies will also be available at the conference.
On Friday, October 18th the one-day program includes valuable 
presentations and discussion, member reports, displays and the AGM.   
The evening Awards Ceremony will celebrate and honour some important 
successes and achievements in heritage conservation.  On Saturday, 
October 19th join us for the half-day heritage bus tour of the provincially-
owned Riverview Hospital Site in Coquitlam, co-hosted by the Province of 
BC and Heritage BC.
