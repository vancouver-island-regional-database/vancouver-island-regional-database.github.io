Originally launched in 2 001, the Swim to Survive program was funded through national grants administered by the Lifesaving Society of Canada. INTRODUCTION/BACKGROUND: Each year, drowning remains one of the leading causes of preventable death for Canadian children. The Swim to Survive program, developed by the Lifesaving Society, teaches three essential survival skills every child should know:  How to roll into deep water Page 192 of 207

 Tread water for one minute  Swim 50 metres to safety These foundational skills can make the difference between life and death in an unexpected fall into water.
