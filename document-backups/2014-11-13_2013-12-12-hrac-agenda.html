PRESENT: Chair – Jennifer Robinson Members – Gerry Beltgens, Maureen Martin , 
Tamara Hutchinson, Allen McDermid, Ann Rogers ; LDHS Member -

 None.

a.  RBC – 521 First Avenue 
Members reviewed the proposed signage.

approve the sign proposal for RBC at 521 First Avenue.  
 
5. HERITAGE PROJECT UPDATES 
a.  Transfer Beach Pictorial 
On November 6 2013 A. McDermid and A. Vincent met with Archivist C. 
Meutzner and Director of Development Services F. Adams in regards to the 
pictorial text and photographs, as well as potential design concepts and 
locations. 
 
Members reviewed the pictorial content prepared by C. Meutzner and 
commented that the text was concise, well put-together and interesting. 
 
Next steps include obtaining quotes for graphic design and installation 
services. 
 
b.       Heritage Week 2014 (“Heritage Afloat!” February 17-23, 2014) 
B. Knol updated the Commission members with progress on contacting other 
local heritage organizations. Ladysmith Maritime Society (LMS) is interested 
in organizing a community event on Saturday February 27 2013 and will work 
with HRAC to organize and contribute some fund ing to the event. The Arts 
Council may create a “Heritage Afloat” art display in the Coast Realty window.

A. Vincent outlined the budget for proposed 201 4 projects. Members 
supported the inclusion of Heritage Week /Community Education, additional 
work on Interpretive History Program , and the Community Heritage Register 
update (completion of Statements of Significance). 
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission 
support the 2014 budget for proposed projects.

8. UPDATES 
a. Heritage BC Conference Report 
M. Martin and G. Beltgens provided a report on the Heritage BC Conference 
held on October 18 and 19, 2013. They reported on the changes taking place 
at Heritage BC, inc luding a new Executive Director, a new President, and a 
renewed focus on sustainability. Some concepts discussed at the conference 
included the integration of heritage and technology (i.e. QR codes on 
buildings), and the integration of heritage and economic development (i.e. 
affordable rent for businesses in heritage buildings to attract young 
entrepreneurs). The Dynamic Downtown s workbook was recommended for 
review by Commissioners. A. Vincent will email a link to this document. 
 
b. Other Commissioner Updates 
B. Knol noted that there is a citizen -led initiative towards purchasing and 
renovating the  Traveller’s Hotel for use as a boutique hotel and pub or 
restaurant. 
 
A. McDermid invited Commission members to attend the Ladysmith Archives 
5th Anniversary celebrations on November 12 , 2013. This coincides with BC 
Archives Week.
