Addendum 6
Issue Date: January 20, 2023
Project Name: Lot 108
Expanded Parking and Practice Pitch
Project Number: 1101-001
This Addendum is an integral part of the Tender Documents for the project referenced above. The
following revisions supersede the information contained in the original drawings, specifications or
previously issued Addenda. The Tenderer shall acknowledge this addendum by noting the number of
Addenda in their submission.
1.0 Tender Documents:
.1 Civil Design Drawing C04 – Practice Pitch and C06 – Details have been updated to Revision F and are
attached.
.2 The Schedule of Prices has been updated and is attached.  Changes include:
· Pay Item 2.10 has been renamed “200m PVC SDR35 Culvert”
· Pay items 2.12, 2.14, 2.15, and 3.5 have been removed.
· Pay item 2.19 “Field Drainage” has been added and is a lump sum payment item.
.4 Appendix B – 2.9 shall be renamed “200m PVC SDR35 Culvert”
.5 Appendix B – 2.13 shall be added and will read as the following:
.13  Field Drainage (Pay Item No. 2.19) Price based on lump sum
This item is for all cost associated with supplying and installing the drainage related to the artificial
turf field as described in the design drawings.  This price shall includes the 100Ø perforated pipe,
100Ø solid pipe, 200Ø perforated pipe, cleanouts, bends, wyes, drainrock, incidental materials,
excavation, testing and all work incidental to the completed installation.  To clarify; base stone, top
stone, the catch basin and 200Ø pipe adjacent to the north side of the field walkway, are not
included in this pay item.
Payment for this item will be made at the listed lump sum rate.
2.0 Clarification:
.1 The tender documents do not include page TF-8.
3.0 Questions and Answers:
Q1: Item 2.8 - Please clarify the design of the tie in of the existing 200mm perf to the proposed Type 1
CB's. It appears that the proposed Cb's are directly inline with the existing 200mm perf - do custom CB's
need to be poured to have an inlet and outlet? Is the invert of the existing 200mm perf pipe known?
A1: The existing perforated pipe has approximately 2m of cover. The new catch basins will be
installed above the existing perforate pipe.   No custom catch basins are required.

Q2: Item 2.10 - 300mm PVC SDR35 Culvert - but drawings called for 200mm - please confirm type and
size of culvert.
A2: The culverts are 200mm dia. PVC SDR 35. This item has been clarified in items 1.2 and 1.4 of this
addendum.
Q3: Item 2.18 - Adjust existing IC - Please confirm intent / extent of adjustment of IC's - current location
conflicts with proposed Curb and Gutter
A3: The existing inspections assembly and service boxes are near the proposed gutter line.  A portion
of the inspection assembly may need to be re-installed and/or excavated and shifted approximately
0.2m into the proposed sidewalk.
Q4: Also noticed that TF8 (tender form 8 is missing as well) not sure if that's on purpose or not because
it goes from TF7 to TF9.
A4: See item 2.1 in this addendum.
