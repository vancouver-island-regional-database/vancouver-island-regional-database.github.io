DELEGATIONS Ed Nicholson, Ladysmith & District Historical Society 
E. Nicholson of the Ladysmith and District Historical  Society made 
a presentation outlining the need for a Ladysmith museum , and 
requesting assistance from the Town in investigating the feasibility 
of establishing a museum and finding grant funding.    
 
Greg Roberts, Ladysmith Community Gardens Society 
G. Roberts made a presentation regarding successes of the Society 
in its first year of operating a community garden, and invited 
Council to make suggestions regarding other contributions the 
Society could be making to the community.  Committee members  
thanked Mr. Roberts for his presentation and thanked the  society 
for their hard work, and congratulated them on their successes to 
date.

Condition of the Trees at the Ladysmith Arboretum 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Committee 
recommend that Council request staff to schedule a seminar for 
staff of Vancouver Island municipalities on construction practices 
around existing trees and that the removal of a gingko and four 
other dead or dying trees in the Ladysmith Arboretum, and their 
replacement with the same species of trees. 
 
Staff were requested to ensure that the replacement trees will be 
suitable for the conditions at the Arboretum.   
 
Ladysmith Community Gardens 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Committee 
recommend that Council enter into a Licence of Occupation and 
Use Agreement with the Ladysmith Community Gardens Society for 
the Ladysmith Community Garden/ High Street Green on a portion 
of High Street Park.
