4. DEVELOPMENT APPLICATION REFERRALS  
 a. OCP / Zoning Amendment Application 3360-09-08 
1237 – 4th Avenue – Lot 1, District Lot 97, Oyster District, Plan VIP84065 – 
Maureen Pilcher 
 
Planner, L. Brinkman provided a summary of the development proposal for 48 
residential units at 1237 4th Ave.  
 
Advisory Design Panel members requested copies of the arborist report, preliminary 
landscape statement, and riparian area assessment report. 
 
Designer, W. Melville and Owner, C. Kerns provided information regarding the 
proposed design of the site. 
 
Consultant, M. Pilcher presented a ‘tree assessment map’. 
 
Items discussed by ADP members are as follows: 
• Desire to see a site plan with actual protected trees identified; 
• Ensure fire chief has reviewed the plans to review fire protection strategies 
onsite; 
• Importance of providing access to lands beyond to maximize flexibility for 
future land use opportunities; 
• Discussed protection of riparian area on lot 2.  
 
Owner, C. Kerns indicated that he is committed to permeable paving onsite. 
 
 
 

Advisory Design Panel 
December 15, 2009 
 2
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Advisory Design Panel advises Council that it 
supports the proposal (3360-09-08) with the proviso that the future use of Lot 2, Plan 
VIP84065 (owner’s parcel containing Rocky Creek riparian area) is consistent with the 
overall plan as presented (delinea design consultants Ltd.-Site Plan-September 10, 2009).
