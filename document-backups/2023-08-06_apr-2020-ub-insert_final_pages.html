COVID-19 - TOWN STAFF SAFELY 
SERVING THE COMMUNITY
The Town of Ladysmith is committed to safely providing 
services to the community as we prioritize physical distanc-
ing in the workplace. 
Some staff  across all our departments are continuing to per-
form regular duties from home and others are completing 
on-site duties while respecting Provinicial Health Offi   cer’ s 
orders to stay a minimum safe distance of two metres from 
others. 
During these extraordinary times, we ask that members of 
the public do not approach staff  who are performing duties 
and that you respect the requirements for physical distanc-
ing. 
Until the COVID-related provincial health orders have been 
lifted, our offi  ces and facilities will remain closed as we serve 
the community remotely and respect the health and well-
being of our residents. 
Staff  can be reached during regular business hours by call-
ing City Hall at 250.245.6400, or by emailing questions or 
concerns to info@ladysmith.ca. If you need to report a road, 
sewer or water emergency after hours, please call  Infra-
structure Services at 250-245-6445 and press 5 to be redi-
rected. 
Parks, Trails and Playgrounds
Staying healthy and breathing in fresh air by participating in 
activities that promote safe physical distancing is important 
for the mental and physical well-being of all Ladysmith resi-
dents during the COVID-19 pandemic.
We all have a civic duty to protect the health of our fami-
lies and neighbours and help stop the spread of COVID-19.  
Stay connected, stay apart and self-isolate as we all follow 
the Provincial Health Offi  cer’s mandatory orders.
When you do leave home to get some fresh air, all commu-
nity members must continue to obey orders to stay two 
metres from others at all times and not gather in groups, 
especially in parking lots, trail heads and viewpoints.
All playgrounds in our public parks as well as the skatepark 
at the Frank Jameson Community Centre will stay closed.

Backyard Burning
A reminder to all residents that backyard burning of yard 
or garden waste is not allowed in Ladysmith at any time. 
Please, for the sake of your neighbours and to avoid undue 
pressure on our health care system at this critical time, do 
no burn waste in your backyard. 
At the time of printing the Utility Bills, contained camp-
fi res not exceeding 0.5 meters in diameter and 0.5 meters 
in height using seasoned wood, charcoal briquettes, pro-
pane or natural gas as fuel were permitted in Ladysmith. 
However, we ask that out of respect for residents with re-
spiratory issues that you refrain from campfi res and help 
others breathe easier during this challenging period.  Your 
neighbours with breathing diffi  culties will thank you for 
this.
You also can help out by piling yard waste and other items 
from spring cleaning at home instead of taking them to 
the Peerless Road Recycling Centre.
The Peerless Road facility continues to operate on winter 
hours (Wed, Sat and Sun, 9 am to 5 pm) and access is cur-
rently limited to 10 vehicles on site at a time.
Keeping yard waste and other items at home for now
helps to ease the burden. Please hold off  visiting if you can.

GARBAGE, RECYCLING AND 
ORGANICS PICKUP
Garbage, recycling and organics collection is continuing 
to be carried out by our contractor, Waste Connections. 
The Town will keep residents updated about any changes 
to collection schedules through our website and social 
media accounts.  We understand that you also want to 
show your appreciation for our solid waste collectors but 
ask that you do not approach them and practice physical 
distancing. 
We also know that you may have bulk items that were set 
aside for the annual Spring Clean Up that was postponed 
earlier in April at the request of our garbage contractor. 
Thank you for understanding this postponement in light 
of the COVID-19 pandemic and keeping our wonderful 
solid waste collectors safe and healthy.
The Peerless Road Recycling Centre continues to operate 
on winter hours (Wed, Sat and Sun, 9 am to 5 pm) and 
access is limited to 10 vehicles on site at a time. 
If your trip to Peerless is not necessary , we ask that you 
hold items at home, including yard waste, for the time 
being to help ease the burden.  Free stores are also 
closed until further notice. 
All payments for disposal must be made with a debit or 
credit card.

COUNCIL CONFIRMS THE ELECTED 
FIRE CHIEF
Ladysmith Council has confirmed the results of a recent 
election of officers held by Ladysmith Fire Rescue, and 
appointed Chris Geiger as the new Fire Chief. 
The Town and Council would like to extend a sincere and 
heartfelt thank you to outgoing Fire Chief Ray Delcourt 
for his outstanding service to the Ladysmith community 
over the past 22 years. 
“On behalf of Ladysmith Council, I would like to extend 
our sincere gratitude to outgoing Fire Chief Ray Delcourt 
for his tireless service– our town is a safer place because 
of Ray’s dedication to emergency preparedness, 
community safety and fire prevention, ” said Mayor Aaron 
Stone.   
“Ray has been on call 24/7, 365 days a year for over two 
decades. He was the first to answer the call whatever 
time of day and respond to hundreds of emergencies, 
helping to save residents’ lives, and their homes filled 
with many precious possessions. He is a hero in the eyes 
of the community and well respected by young and old 
alike. We look forward to honouring Ray in a big way 
after the COVID-19 crisis. ”
Delcourt was named Ladysmith’s Citizen of the Year 
in 2018 and has helped lead much of Ladysmith Fire 
Rescue’s community outreach, such as fundraising for 
Muscular Dystrophy and Tour de Rock Cops for Cancer.
In light of provincial health orders to limit public 
gatherings and practice physical distancing, the Town, 
Ladysmith Council and Delcourt’s colleagues at the 
Fire Hall look forward to honouring his service to the 
community at a future date.
Geiger’s appointment to Fire Chief was effective as of 
April 1. 
Geiger is born and raised in Ladysmith and has served 
with CFB Esquimalt Fire/Rescue since June 2018.   He 
has been a member of Ladysmith Fire Rescue since 
2007, serving as Lieutenant, Captain and most recently 
holding the position of Chief Training Officer prior to his 
appointment as Fire Chief.
A married father with two teenage children, Geiger has 
experience as an instructor and evaluator for the Justice 
Institute of British Columbia. He is also looking forward 
to continued opportunities teaching with Vancouver 
Island Emergency Response Academy and CF Responder 
Training. 
“I am honoured to have been elected to lead this 
exceptional team, ” said Geiger. “I am very proud to 
be a member of this department, and I look forward 
to continuing the tradition of excellent service this 
community has become accustomed to from Ladysmith’s 
volunteer firefighters. ”
