NOTICE 
TRANSFER OF LEASE  
 
Notice is hereby given that the 
Town of Ladysmith intends to 
assign a crown land lease over 
District Lot 2016. A marina is 
currently located on the property. 
The Town intends to seek provincial 
approval to assign Lease 106431 to 
the Stz’uminus First Nation. Lease 
106431 is a crown land lease held 
by the Town and expires in 2029. 
The Town will not receive any 
compensation in exchange for 
transferring the lease. Marina 
infrastructure on site is not owned 
by the Town. 
 
Information regarding this 
proposed disposition can be 
obtained from Matt O’Halloran  
Corporate Officer, 250.245.6417,  
mohalloran@ladysmith.ca.  
 
This Notice of Disposition (sale, 
lease or licence) is provided in 
accordance with section 26(3) 
of the 
Community Charter. 
DISTRICT LOT 2016, 
COWICHAN DISTRICT
