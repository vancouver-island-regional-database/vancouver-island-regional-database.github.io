2011-2015 FINANCIAL 
PLAN 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 2011-209 
 
 
A discussion ensued regarding the 2011 -2015 financial plan. 
Correspondence dated May 3, 2011 from Guy MacIsaac, Chair, 
Gas Tax Management Committee for the UBCM advising the 
Town that its application for a gas tax general strategic priorities 
fund and/or innovations fund grant 
was not approved for early 
funding.  The applications (for water and sewer system upgrades) 
are now under consideration for the second round of approvals.  
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the sewer parcel tax be 
increased by $60 (total $154) for 2011. 
 
Council confirmed that Special Council meetings are scheduled 
for Thursday, May 5, 2011 at 5:00 p.m. and Monday, May 9, 
2011 at 4:00 p.m. to consider the Financial Plan bylaws.
