OLD BUSINESS Dogs Off-Leash Park – Commissioners discussed the areas they were given to 
consider. Staff to determine if the strip of land at the end of Methuen on Sixth 
Avenue can be considered for a dog-off leash park as well.  Decision will be made 
at the next meeting.  
 
Parks, Recreation & Culture Assessment – Staff have been in contact with a 
Ladysmith Leadership group to determine if they would like to carry out this 
assessment as part of their project. It wa s also suggested that if the Leadership 
group choose not to take this project on, Staff should contact Vancouver Island 
University. 
 
Sports Box at Lot 108 – Staff will determine if an area at Lot 108 would be 
appropriate or offer an alternate location.  Information will be provided at the 
next meeting.  
 
Parks, Recreation & Culture Strategi c Plan – Commissioners discussed this 
report and decided to form a committee to compare the Strategic Plan to the 
upcoming Assessment as well as the visioning report.  Committee members at 
this time are John Perry, Glenn Popenko, and Lorena Mueller. 
 
NEW BUSINESS Letter from the Cadets – Commissioners reviewed the letter sent to Council by 
the Cadets expressing their need for additional assistance with their program.
