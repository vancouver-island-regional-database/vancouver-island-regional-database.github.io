MINUTES 
Advisory Design Panel (ADP) 
Monday, December 16, 2013 at 1:30 p.m. 
Council Chambers, City Hall 
 
 
 
LADYSMITH 
 
PRESENT: Chair– Ron Kinney; Members - Brian Childs, John Craig; HRAC Member – Ann 
Rogers; Council Liaison – Jill Dashwood; Planner and Recorder– Lisa 
Brinkman; Director of Development Services – Felicity Adams 
 
ABSENT/REGRETS: Kaien Shimizu 
 
GUESTS: Brent Elliott and John Gauld (CitySpaces Consulting)  
 
 
The meeting was called to order at 1:35 p.m. 
 
1. AGENDA  
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of December 16, 2013 be approved. 
 
2. WELCOME, INTRODUCTION AND ORIENTATION 
Meeting attendees introduced themselves to the group.  R. Kinney provided an 
introduction and orientation to the A DP members. L. Brinkman also provided 
orientation information for ADP members.  The members asked questions and 
responses were provided. 
 
3. ELECTION OF CHAIR 
B. Childs nominated R Kinney for the position of chair of the ADP.  R. Kinney 
accepted the nomination. There were no further nominations.  R. Kinney was 
acclaimed to the position of ADP Chair. 
 
4. MINUTES 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of August 26, 2013 be approved.   
 
5. ZONING BYLAW PROJECT – NEW DEVELOPMENT PERMIT GUIDELINES 
a. Review of Draft Development Permit Area 2 – Downtown 
 Brent Elliott and John Gauld (CitySpaces Consulting) 
B. Elliot and J. Gauld presented the draft Development Permit Area 2 
guidelines and sought  feedback from the ADP members. The members were 
pleased with the direction of the proposed guidelines. 
 
6. NEW BUSINESS 
None. 
 
7. MONTHLY BRIEFING 
None.

8. NEXT MEETING – January 20, 2014, Council Chambers, City Hall 
 
9.       ADJOURNMENT 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the meeting be adjourned at 3:30 p.m.
