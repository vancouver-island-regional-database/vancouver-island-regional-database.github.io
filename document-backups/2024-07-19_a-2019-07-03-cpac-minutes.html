Community Planning Advisory Committee (CPAC)

Council Chambers, City Hall

The meeting was called to order at 7:00 p.m. 
 
1. WELCOME, INTRODUCTION & ORIENTATION 
Community Planning Advisory Committee  (CPAC) members introduced 
themselves.  L. Brinkman welcomed the members and provided a summary of the 
purpose of CPAC and the contents of the CPAC binder. 
 
2. ELECTION OF CHAIR 
Lacey McRae Williams volunteered to be  Chair of the Community Planning 
Advisory Committee. 
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that Lacey McRae Williams be acclaimed as Chair of 
the Community Planning Advisory Committee. 
 
3. AGENDA APPROVAL 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of July 3, 2019 be approved.
