2.   AGENDA APPROVAL

It was moved, seconded and carried that the minutes of April 27, 2010 be adopted. 
 
4.   CHAIR’S REPORT 
P. Richmond reported on the following: 
• Reviewed the Cowichan Regional Tourism Plan. 
• Lots of events happening in Town lately. 
• An increase in building permits. 
 
5.   DIRECTOR’S REPORT 
F. Adams reported on the following: 
• G. Millar’s Regional Tourism Plan presentation at the May 17, 2010 
Government Services Committee. 
- F. Adams provided an e-copy of the report to TAC and EDC. 
- G. Millar offered to have a follow-up meeting with Council, EDC and TAC. 
- TVI funding dedicated to Cowichan. New approach – Dollars go to the Region 
instead of directly to Ladysmith. 
• S. Blumensaat and D. Webber have created and will staff a booth at the 
Tourism Nanaimo Tradeshow today, to promote activities in Ladysmith.  
Donations have been made by LMS, Sealegs and Kiwi Cove for draw prizes. 
• Attending joint TOL/SFN Meetings.
