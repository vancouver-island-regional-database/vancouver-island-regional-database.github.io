Step 1 involves 3 components: Swim stroke & water safety skills screening, 
online modules & a teaching practicum.  The swim stroke & water safety skills 
screening is designed to ensure candidates can swim to a Red Cross Kids Level 
10 standard & will include an evaluation.  At the end of the stroke screening 
session, successful candidates will continue onto the online portion of the 
course. *Candidates must have a current Emergency First Aid Certificate.
