 2 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Advisory Design Panel recommends that 
Council support Development Permit application #3060 -15-12 (318, 320, 324 & 330 First 
Avenue) with the following comments: 
 As the representative of the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission (HRAC),   
A. Rogers feels that the Atrium Design is disruptive and does not enhance, protect or 
preserve the Heritage guidelines as required in the DPA 2 Guidelines. 
 The Advisory Design Panel is in favour of a water feature in the plaza area. 
 Attention should be paid to courtyard parking. 
 Lots of handrails for pedestrian movement should be included. 
 A green roof on the Atrium is encouraged.
