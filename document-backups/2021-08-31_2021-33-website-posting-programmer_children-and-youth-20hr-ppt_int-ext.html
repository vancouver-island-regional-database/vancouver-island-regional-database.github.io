POSITION DESCRIPTION 
DESCRIPTION
TITLE: PROGRAMMER – CHILDREN AND YOUTH  
DEPARTMENT: PARKS, RECREATION AND CULTURE 
DIVISION: COMMUNITY SERVICES 
CATEGORY:  UNION CUPE LOCAL 401  
BAND:  13 (SUBJECT TO JE REVIEW) 
GENERAL ACCOUNTABILITY 
Reporting to the Recreation and Culture Coordinator, the Programmer - Children and Youth is responsible 
for developing and monitoring programs and services related to children and youth.  The incumbent 
develops and implements a variety of programs and activities for the community, as well as assisting 
with the ongoing evaluation and modification of the Town’s children and youth services.  The Programmer  
provides excellence in customer service as well as maintaining and developing partnerships with the 
objective of increasing awareness and participation relating to children, youth and community services. 
PRIMARY DUTIES AND ACCOUNTABILITIES 
o Develops, promotes, monitors and evaluates programs and services related to children and youth
and special events.
o Plans, organizes, schedules, trains and supervises children and youth staff.
o Assists in recruiting contract instructors and service providers , and negotiates and executes
contracts for children and youth programs.
o Assists the Recreation & Culture Coordinator in the development of budget information.
o Monitors and reports expenditures relating to areas of responsibility as required.
o Works collaboratively with other programmers on program delivery and planning.
o On a regular basis, directly l eads programs and classes as it relates to children and youth, including
supervision of the youth centre. 
o Enhances programs by working with regional, provincial and national affiliates .
o Purchases supplies relevant to departmental operations .
o Participates and provides input into the department marketing plan.
o Identifies and makes recommendations relating to staff training strategies.
o Recommends changes to and maintains procedures related to area of responsibility .
o Supports service delivery to align with the department and organizational strategic objectives .
o Participates in the Emergency Social Services (ESS) Program in a supervisory role .
o Assists with coordination relating to occupational health and safety activities for department.
o Facilitates and attends various community meetings, creating and distributing reports as required.
o Exercises considerable independent initiative and judgement, thinks strategically and is action
oriented. 
o Provides leadership, direction and support to the department team member s.
o Responsible for compliance with safe work practices and safety guidelines as established by the
Town and Worksafe BC. 
o Responsible for compliance of procedures and policies as established by the Town.
o Other related duties as required.
REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS, KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS AND ABILITY 
1. A minimum grade 12 education, a diploma in recreation/leisure services, social services, child and
youth care or a related field plus two (2) years’ recent (within the last 5 years)/relevant employment
experience or an equivalent combination of training and recent relevant experience.
2. A minimum of one-year recent relevant (within the last 5 years) supervisory experience.
