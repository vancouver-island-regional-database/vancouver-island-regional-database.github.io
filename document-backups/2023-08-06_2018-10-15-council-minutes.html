CS 2018-388  
 
Moved and seconded: 
That, in accordance with section 90(1) of the Community Charter,  
Council re tire into closed session at 5:01  p.m. in order to consider 
items related to the following: 
• personal information about an identifiable individual who holds 
or is being considered for a position as an officer, employee or 
agent of the municipality or another position appointed by the 
municipality - section 90 (1) (a); 
• the acquisition, disposition or expropriation of land or 
improvements, if the council considers that disclosure could 
reasonably be expected to harm the interests of the municipality  
- section 90 (1) (e); 
• the receipt of advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege, 
including communications necessary for that purpose  - section 
90 (1) (i) 
Motion carried.

Remuneration Committee Report to Council

Remuneration, made a presentation to Council regarding the 
committee’s research . She  reviewed the committee’s membership,

comparison municipalities.
