DELEGATION DELEGATION DELEGATION DELEGATION     Cowichan Valley Regional District  (CVRD) , Sports Tourism - John 
Elzinga presented the CVRD initiative of creating  a calculated 
approach in advancing sports tourism opportunities by working 
regionally to encourage larger sporting events to our shared area 
resulting in positive economic impacts throughout the region. 
 
It is anticipated that once staff have outlined a framework, two 
members of the Parks, Recreation and Culture Commission to join 
the committee to create the initial framework this initiative.
