1. C A L L  T O  O R D E R                     
P. Richmond called the meeting to order at 7:15 a.m.   
 
2. AGENDA APPROVAL 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the agenda of March 23, 2010 be approved. 
 
3. ADOPTION OF MINUTES 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the minutes of January 26, 2010 be adopted. 
 
4. CORPORATE UPDATE 
City Manager, R. Malli presented the following updates: 
• Greenhouse Gas emission reductions initiatives are to be incorporated into the 
Town’s financial plan as outlined in the visioning document. 
• Community resiliency – the Town is looking at micro power generation as a 
potential microloan initiative for sustainability.  P. Richmond indicated an interest 
in working with the Town on green initiatives. 
• Waterfront – Environmental report is nearing completion and will be useful for 
future development. 
• High Street project (Community Centre/Civic Space) is being funded in part by the 
Federal Government economic stimulus funding. The Town will register this 
project for LEED Gold. 
• DL 108 playing fields.  Economic development for the Town is in the forefront of 
this project.  
            
             Councillor, S. Arnett joined the meeting at 7:26 am. 
 
             City Manager, R. Malli, left the meeting at 7:28 am. 
 
5. CHAIR’S REPORT 
Chair, P. Richmond reported that he attended the Town’s financial plan discussion focusing 
on the need to become carbon neutral.  Also met with Stz’uminus First Nation Business 
Development Manager, R. Gauthier and reported the following: 
• The Stz’uminus First Nation land use plan has been completed. This will be a long 
term plan for the whole area.  The plan  includes a Hotel and a new residential 
area.

7. SECTOR REPORTS 
a. Tourism Advisory Committee  
       The following was discussed: 
• The great work on marketing initiatives done by C. Randall. 
• 2010 marketing has been prepurchased. 
• Rack Card and Heritage Route brochure look good. 
• There are a variety of different maps being put out by different community 
groups.  Would like to see one fully functional map. 
• Friends and Family campaign postcards are out.  Drives people to the website, 
which is tracked. 
• Would like to see the business community keep their websites updated. It was 
suggested that this is something that summer students could possibly do.   
• The Chronicle is working with the Ladysmith marinas on a coupon book. 
• Entrance to Transfer Beach Park signage has been ordered. 
• New bright and colourful theme banners will be put up on Transfer Beach 
Boulevard. 
• R. Waters, C. Randall, F. Adams and Councillor S. Arnett plan to attend the final 
session of the Cowichan Region Tourism planning workshop tonight. 
 
b. Marine Sector

members.  D. Ehrismann also spoke on packages that are being prepared for Marina 
Tours with rendezvous groups and the upcoming Maritime Festival at which they will 
be celebrating 100 year of the Canadian Navy. 
 
A discussion has taken place that a potential fuel dock will be re-established at the 
Page Point Marina.
