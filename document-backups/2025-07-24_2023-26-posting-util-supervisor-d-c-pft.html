CATEGORY:  UNION CUPE LOCAL 401

GENERAL ACCOUNTABILITY 
Reporting to the Manager of Operations, the Utilities Supervisor is responsible for the leadership, operation, 
and maintenance of the Town’s wastewater collection/water distribution systems. As the Utilities Supervisor, 
the incumbent  is accountable for prioritizing, planning, coordinating and directing staff involved in water  
distribution and wastewater collection . Further supervisory responsibilities include the preparation of 
schedules and operational plans , organization of staff, material s, vehicles, and equipment, while  working 
within budgetary constraints.  Work involves making operational decisions, performing operational  and 
maintenance duties in order to maintain the Town’ s water distribution and wastewater collection systems 
under the Ministry of Environment guidelines and applicable Provincial and Federal legislation.  
 
PRIMARY DUTIES AND ACCOUNTABILITIES 
o Supervise utilities staff and maintain safe and efficient day -to-day operation of the water  distribution 
and wastewater collection systems. 
o Operating and maintaining all pumps, motors, electric equipment, flow-measuring devices, meters, 
regulating equipment and structures, including lift stations and any other related facilities. 
o Manual labor in construction, repair and maintenance required within the water and wastewater 
department. 
o Excavation, pipework installation (to lines and grades as directed), backfilling and compacti on of 
trenches for construction of sewer and water mains and services.  
o Construction of sewer manholes and the installation of water valves , curb stops, hydrants, bends and 
fittings, including thrust blocks. 
o Maintaining daily, monthly and annual records of  water / sewer utility; infrastructure  in accordance 
with Federal and Provincial Regulations. 
o Under the direction of the Manager of Operations , ensures that all permit requirements and 
maintenance objectives are met. 
o Adheres to safety policies and regulations as established by WorkSafe BC, the Occupational Health 
and Safety committee, the Town of Ladysmith, and regional and municipal statutes.  
o Assists in the development of safety protocols and efficient work practices.  
o Required to work weekends and shift work as scheduled and overtime as directed.  
o Directs and assists in the training of new employees to ensure familiarity with safety regulations, safe 
work practices and the correct and safe use of all tools and equipment nece ssary to perform their 
duties. 
o Responsible to document various goods and materials upon removal from inventory to ensure current 
supply levels are reflected accurately.   
o Required to procure and purchase goods and materials associated with the daily mainte nance of the 
Water and Wastewater department. 
o Required to participate as a member of the standby group and fulfills stand-by shift requirements on a 
rotating basis. 
o Maintains courteous and professional interactions with the public, user groups and Town employees. 
o Attends professional development training as directed. 
o Other related duties as required. 

 
 
 
REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS, KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS AND ABILITY 
1. Minimum grade 12 educat ion and a minimum of five (5) years recent and relevant experience in 
Municipal water  distribution and sewer collection . An equivalent combination of education and 
experience may be considered. 
2. Minimum of two (2) years supervisory experience.  Follows and models all Town of Ladysmith policies, 
procedures and practices. 
3. Water Distribution Level II Certification of Qualification or Competency from Environmental 
Operators Certification Program. 
4. Waste Water Collection Level II Certification of Qualification or Competency from Environmental 
Operators Certification Program. 
5. Water Treatment Level I Certification of Qualification or Competency from Environmental Operators 
Certification Program 
6. Waste Water Treatment Level I Certification of Qualification or Competency from Environmental 
Operators Certification Program 
7. Cross Connection Control and Confined Space certificates. 
8. Valid Occupational First Aid Level 1 and Workplace Hazardous Material Information System (WHMIS) 
certificates. 
9. Valid B.C. driver’s license - class 3 with airbrake endorsement. 
10. Ability to report, in person, to the Town of Ladysmith Main Office within a timeframe no greater than 
twenty-five (25) minutes from receipt of call-in. 
11. Proficient use of Microsoft Office Suite applications and other database applications.  
12. Demonstrated proficiency in the safe operation of mot orized vehicles and equipment and, various 
infrastructure services tools and light industrial equipment including but not limited to:  
• Tandem axle dump truck  
• Single axle dump truck  
• Vactor truck  
• Backhoe 
• Skid steer  
• Forklift  
13. Demonstrated ability to fulfill assigned duties in all weather conditions. 
14. Demonstrated thorough knowledge of various water and wastewater  methods, procedures, 
techniques, materials, and a proficiency in the safe operation of relevant tools and equipment.  
15. Ability to pass an annual full face respirator fit test. 
16. Demonstrated ability to interpret and evaluate data relevant to the operation and implem entation of 
operational changes. 
17. Demonstrated thorough knowledge of the laws, rules and regulations, standards and specifications, 
municipal bylaws and policies pertaining to the water and wastewater  utilities. 
18. Demonstrated ability to make timely, appropriate decisions in response to emergent  needs and 
regarding issues of major importance to the organization, its people,  and the community. 
19. Demonstrated initiative with minimum direction; completing tasks by removing barriers and locating 
necessary resources. 
20. Demonstrated ability to communicate effectively in a wide variety of situations applying diplomacy and 
interpersonal skill to establish and maintain productive relationships.  
21. Demonstrated high level of focus on internal and external client service continuously seeking ways to 
meet and exceed expectations. 
22. Demonstrated ability to work cooperatively within a team and with Town employees, stakeholders and

23. Demonstrates valuing diversity.
