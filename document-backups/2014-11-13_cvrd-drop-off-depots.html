Please Note: 
1. All loads must be secure and covered by a tarp or other overlay so materials cannot blow off or fall out while in 
transit.  Unsecure loads may be fined! 
2. All waste cannot exceed 1.25 m in length or 100 kg in weight and must be free from recyclables. 
3. No person shall deposit prohibited waste at the CVRD depots.  Call the recycling hotline for more information. 
4. All fiberglass insulation and dusty material must be contained in a double bagged system. 
5. Vermiculite is not accepted at any CVRD recycling depot unless proper documentation is provided certifying 
that it is asbestos-free. 
6. Children and pets must remain in vehicle at all times.
