DELEGATIONS 
 
 
James van Hemert, Kate Miller, Cowichan Valley Regional District 
Regional Environmental Strategic Plan – Input from Town of 
Ladysmith 

 
 
Council Meeting Minutes: December 17, 2012 Page 2 
 Kate Miller and James van Hemert  provided Council with a brief 
overview of the CVRD Regional Environmental Strategic Plan  and 
requested Council’s input on the following: 
- What are the Town’s environmental priorities in the next one 
to five years and beyond 20 years? 
- What role can the CVRD play in supporting the Town’s 
environmental programs? 
- How can the CVRD’s environmental plan support the Town’s 
priorities? 
A discussion ensued regarding this issue. Council thanked the 
CVRD representatives for their presentation.
