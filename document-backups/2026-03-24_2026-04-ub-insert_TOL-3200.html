NEW RATES EFFECTIVE 2026
The Utility Bill enclosed with this newsletter covers the 
period January to March 2026. You will notice your bill is 
calculated based on the new water rates that came into 
effect January 1, 2026. The water rates increased by 3% 
to the base rate and consumption steps. This equates to a 
$1.92 increase to the base rate for a single family dwelling 
and $2.88 increase to the base rate for a single-family 
dwelling with a suite for the quarter. The sewer rates also 
increased by 3% for all of the classes.  This equates to a 
$2.94 increase for each dwelling unit for the quarter.  The 
solid waste fees increased by $10.50 for the quarter per 
unit. The rate increase helps to cover the rising costs of 
providing safe, reliable drinking water and treating our 
waste water, and contractor rates for solid waste services. 
The funds also go towards building reserves for future 
improvements to our sewer and water supply systems.

TOWN NEWS YOU CAN USE
COMING THIS JUNE: Spring Yard Waste Pickup - The Town and GFL are partnering to bring you a Spring Yard 
Waste Pickup June 1-5, 2026. Visit the ‘Latest News & Information’ section on the Town’s Website homepage for 
more information in the near future.
Pet Responsibility - We encourage community members to be responsible pet owners and please pick up after your 
animal(s) when out walking on sidewalks, trails and visiting parks. Properly disposing of your waste bags by using 
the nearest available trash container helps keep our public spaces enjoyable for everyone. We a ppreciate your 
cooperation.
Emergency and Routine Notifications through the CVRD -  Sign up for the Cowichan Valley Regional District 
(CVRD) Emergency Alert Registration service to receive information on Evacuation Routes, Alerts and Orders and 
available/open ESS Reception Centres. T o register, visit www.cowichanalert.ca .
Organics - Bag Enforcement -  As of March 31, 2026 GFL will stop collecting organics contained in compostable, 
bio-degradable, or oxo-degradable plastic bags, as the Cowichan Valley Regional District will no longer accept 
waste delivered in these types of bags.
What You Can Do Instead:  
- Line your container with newspaper or a 100% compostable paper bag  
- Freeze extra messy or smelly scraps until collection day  
- Add cardboard (tape and labels removed), boxboard, newspaper, a paper bag, or yard waste to the bottom of      
...your tote before adding kitchen scraps  
- Rinse your bin regularly
Water Rates - Remember - water usage over 200 m3 from Apr-Sept is charged at a higher rate.
