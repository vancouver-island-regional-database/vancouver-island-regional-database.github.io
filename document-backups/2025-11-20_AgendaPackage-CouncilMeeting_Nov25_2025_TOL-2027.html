ANALYSIS: Small-scale Commercial Development Permit Guidelines OCP policy 2.3(t) supports “Small -scale, neighbourhood-serving commercial uses such as cafes, corner stores” in the Multi-family designation. As such, the proposed commercial accessory uses are consistent with the OCP. The property is currently located within the DPA 4 Multi -Unit Residential Development Permit Area. DPA 4 guidelines do not address small -scale neighbourhood commercial development. Commercial development guidelines are addressed in the DPA 3 – Commercial guidelines, however, the subject pr operty does not fall within this designation. The site has significant changes in topography / elevation through different portions of the site.
