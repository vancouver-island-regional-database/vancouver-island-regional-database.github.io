provided at the November 14, 2013 HRAC meeting.

b. Community Heritage Register Update 
A copy of the completed Statements of Significance (SOS’s ) for the 13 selected 
heritage buildings were provided to HRAC members. Members were asked to 
review the SOS’s and provide any feedback at the next HRAC meeting. 
 
A. Vincent reviewed the next steps in updating the Community Heritage Register, 
including notification of building owners, approval by Council, and reporting to 
the BC Register for Historic Places.  
 
c. Heritage Week 2014 
B. Knol reported that the Ladysmith Maritime Society (LMS) will be organizing the 
Heritage Week event. The event will be held on Saturday, February 27, 2014 and 
will feature the new Harbour Heritage Centre, the Maritime Museum, and 
heritage boats, along with activities for children and warm refreshments. 
 
A. McDermid will work with the Chronicle and Take 5 to ensure there are relevant 
articles advertising the Heritage Week event. B. Knol will draft a notice to be 
included in the Town’s utility bill, whether  it be a paragraph within the newsletter 
or a separate in sert. External communication will be reviewed by Corporate 
Services prior to publication. 
 
J. Robinson and T. Hutchinson volunteered to coordinate a potential design and 
quote for a large coroplast sign advertising Heritage Week . Options will be 
presented at the January 9, 2014 HRAC meeting.  Members recommended that 
LMS should pursue the placement of an event advertisement hand -out to local 
schools.  
 
6. NEW BUSINESS 
 a.   Machine Shop Opportunity 
A. McDermid reported that there is a group of residents who are interested in 
the lease of space at the Machine Shop on Oyster Bay Drive. The space would 
be used for the purpose of restoring and maintaining heritage rail and machine 
artifacts, potentially in cluding the Dunsmuir Coach and Locomotive #11. The 
group is looking for additional volunteers to assist with the administration and 
formation of a society, and also support for the project. Members invited the  
group to make a presentation to HRAC. 
 
7.      CORRESPONDENCE 
 a.   Heritage BC Quarterly: Fall 2013 
A limited number of copies of the latest Heritage BC Quarterly were sent to HRAC. 
Members were asked to share the copies amongst members. This edition 
includes an article on the restoration of the MV Saravan by LMS. 
 
b. BC Association of Heritage Professionals 
A letter was sent  by the BC Association of Heritage Professionals (BCAHP) to 
notify the Commission  of the BCAHP’s mandate, and to request that HRAC hire 
individuals who are accredited through the association.

8. UPDATES 
Member A. Rogers will attend the Advisory Design Panel meeting on December 16, 
2013 as the HRAC representative to review the draft Development Permit guidelines 
for Development Permit Area 2. A summary will be provided at the HRAC meeting on 
January 9, 2014. It was noted that HRAC members should maintain awareness of 
the review process, and are welcome to attend the public consultation session in the 
new year. 
 
The Commission note d that member A. Rogers will be unable to attend HRAC 
meeting from January to April as a  result of a conflicting work schedule. A. Rogers 
will continue to work on various HRAC projects and will provide updates to be 
discussed at the meeting until her return.
