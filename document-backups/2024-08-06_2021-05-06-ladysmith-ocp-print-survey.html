At its heart, an Official Community Plan (OCP) 
is about managing land use and physical 
growth of the town. The OCP determines the 
type and location of homes, businesses, parks, 
public spaces, and other places. 
As a result, an OCP influences transportation 
and housing choices, greenhouse gas 
emissions, community character, health and 
equity, housing affordability, protection of 
ecological areas, resource management, 
and how much it costs to pay for Town 
infrastructure such a pipes and streets.
The Town of Ladysmith sits within the unceded territories of the 
Stz’uminus First Nation, whose people have been the inhabitants and 
stewards of the lands and waters since Time Immemorial.
Imagine a future Ladysmith that is ideal in 
all ways. How would you describe it?
