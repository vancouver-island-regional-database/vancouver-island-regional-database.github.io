GENERAL ACCOUNTABILITY 
Reporting to the Chief Administrative Officer , the Manager of Human Resources is 
responsible for providing coordination and consistency for strategic labour relations and 
human resources processes throughout the Town of Ladysmith. This encompasses leading 
negotiations, contract administration, collective agreement  interpretation, workforce 
planning/recruitment, and leading the development, enhancement and implementation of 
various progressive strategies and policy initiatives designed to assist the Town in 
effectively managing its employee relations and human resources.  The incumbent plays a 
strategic leadership role for employee relations, human resources and occupational health 
and safety services and exercises considerable independent judgment, initiative, tact, 
courtesy, and diplomacy in processing initiatives and assignments, many of which are 
confidential.  
 
SPECIFIC ACCOUNTABILITIES 
Operations Management 
 Lead the human resources department in the provision of timely, effective, and 
consistent service to management and staff  on all employee relations and human 
resources matters. 
 Organize, l ead, participate and collaborate on committees and project teams to 
develop and maintain effective employee relations, human resource s and, 
occupational health and safety service practices within the Town of Ladysmith. 
 Partner with management and staff on organizational projects to support strategic 
and operational initiatives. 
 Provide timely, effective and consistent advice to senior leadership, managers, and 
supervisors on a range of employee relations and human resource matters, including 
performance deficiencies, grievances , collective agreement interpretation, 
workforce planning, and employee recruitment/retention. 
 Provide objective and impartial assessment of al l safety issues and make 
recommendations to senior leadership to help and ensure staff mitigate and manage 
risks to protect the safety and wellbeing of staff. 
 Provide programs under WorkSafe BC and other regulations to prevent 
occupational injuries and dis eases and reduce the organization's human and 
financial impact. 
 Leads the preparation and negotiation of collective agreements and works to 
identify the organization’s needs in this process.   

 
 
 Respond to internal and external inquiries regarding labour  relations, human 
resources and, occupational health and safety. 
 Provide functional expertise to the organization and strategic and administrative

 Lead the  development and implement ation of human resource policies and 
procedures to ensure consistent application throughout the Town of Ladysmith. 
 Provide advice and leadership to exempt staff on employee relations , human 
resources and, occupational health and safety matters. 
 Lead training delivery and orientation for Town of Ladysmith management and staff 
regarding employee relations, human resource and, occupational health and safety

 Prepare, present and manage operational budgets for the  Human Resources 
Department. 
 Lead, coordinate, and manage the job evaluation program. 
 Actively participate and/or support or lead labour  relations, wellness, and 
occupational health and safety committees. 
 Responsible for Occupational Health and Safety (OHS). Including claims

 Develop, implement and manage recruitment initiatives, processes and programs. 
 Provide proactive management of sick leave and return to work programs. 
 Undertakes and manages the completion of special projects and priorities as

 Manage the annual employee development review program. 
 
Relationship Management 
 Foster and maintain partnerships and positive, solution -oriented working 
relationships with the union, management and staff to ensur e timely and relevant 
services are provided. 
 Collaborate with the union and management to create an organizational culture that 
is supportive, positive, cooperative, action-oriented, and client service-focused. 
 
People Management 
 Ensure a positive, diverse and inclusive work environment. 
 Plan and manage the development of human resource staff. 
 Coach and advise on first-line operational issues and HR processes/practices. 
 Ensure the effective use of human resource policies and practices , including 
recruiting, selecting, training, developing, motivating, and evaluating employees. 
 Address people management issues within the human resources department  
promptly while ensuring compliance with Town of Ladysmith policies and practices. 
 Create a supportive, positive, collaborative, action-oriented and customer service-
focused organizational culture. 
 

 
 
REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS, SKILLS, AND ABILITY 
A post -secondary degree in Human Resources Management, Commerce, or Business 
Administration combined and a minimum of five years ’ progressive, recent and relevant 
experience.  Human resource leadership and demonstrated knowledge of labour relations, 
human resources and, occupational health and safety is required.  Experience in a union 
workplace is required. An equivalent combinati on of education and experience may be 
considered.  A professional human resources designation is desired. 
 
REQUIRED COMPETENCIES 
Presenting and Communicating Information  
Demonstrates an ability to communicate effectively in a wide variety of methods and 
situations, applying diplomacy and interpersonal skill to successfully complete positional 
duties and establish and maintain productive relationships. 
 
Initiative  
Demonstrates initiative without specific direction; completes tasks by removing barriers 
and locating necessary resources.  
 
Client Focus  
The ability to demonstrate a high level of focus on internal and external client service 
delivery, setting priorities based on client needs and continuously seeking ways to meet and 
exceed client expectations. 
 
Teamwork, Collaboration, and Partnering 
Demonstrates the ability to work cooperatively within a team and with individuals 
throughout the organization to achieve optimal results.  Seeks improvement in business 
outcomes by actively seeking and forming stra tegic partnerships with individuals, groups 
and organizations both within and outside the organization. 
 
Knowledge and Expertise  
The ability to make use of the skills, knowledge, and professional proficiencies required by 
the position, and the use of this  expertise to serve the objectives of both the department 
and Town as a whole. 
 
Cultivates Strong Relationships 
Building strong and trusting relationships and bringing a taxpayer -centric mindset and 
focus to all elements of the organization. 
 
Organizational Awareness  
Demonstrates understanding of the Town of Ladysmith – including plans, policies, systems 
and structure and uses this knowledge to improve both personal and team effectiveness.  
 
 
 

 
 
Conflict Management 
Demonstrates an ability to intervene effectively in conflict situations, focusing on common 
objectives and maintaining productive relationships by understanding the underlying 
interests of others. 
 
Results-Oriented 
Demonstrates a consistent focus on achieving results despite challenges or obs tacles; 
demonstrating a concern for doing a good job and achieving or surpassing standards of 
excellence. 
 
Coaching and Developing Others 
Demonstrates the desire and ability to help others develop by accurately assessing 
employees’ interests, needs and capabilities then coaching or supporting that individual as 
necessary. 
 
Analytical Thinking  
Understands situations by breaking them down into smaller pieces, tracing implications 
and organizing elements to explain situations, resolve problems or identify solutions. 
 
Flexibility and Adaptability  
Demonstrates an ability to adapt and thrive in an atmosphere of changing priorities and 
circumstances. 
 
Valuing Diversity  
The ability to promote a workplace where diversity is welcomed, valued and expressed in 
ancestry, thought, and practice.
