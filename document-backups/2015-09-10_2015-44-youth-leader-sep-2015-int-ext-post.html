TOWN OF LADYSMITH JOB DESCRIPTION 
 
 
TITLE:    YOUTH LEADER     
 
DEPARTMENT:  PARKS, RECREATION & CULTURE 
 
JOB SUMMARY 
Under the supervision of the Child & Youth Program Coordinator, the Youth Leader contributes to 
the effective operation of the Department of Parks,  Recreation and Culture as a key member of 
the youth services team.  The Youth Leader assists in the planning, coordination and supervision 
of youth programs, services and events.  This posit ion supports the Department by assisting team 
members in their duties as required, to meet servic e expectations, departmental goals and 
objectives. 
DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES 
/square4 Assist in the planning, coordination and supervisi on of activities for youth 12 - 18 years; 
/square4 Ensure the safety and well-being of all program pa rticipants and volunteers; 
/square4 Monitor the use and care of facilities and equipme nt; 
/square4 Assist in training of youth program staff and volu nteers; 
/square4 Assist in use of informed consents, medical inform ation forms and other forms as required; 
/square4 Establish an atmosphere of fair play; 
/square4 Be attentive to safety concerns; 
/square4 Other duties as assigned. 
REQUIRED SKILLS, KNOWLEDGE & ABILITIES: 
/square4 Completion of minimum of Grade 12 education; 
/square4 Current First Aid and CPR C certification; 
/square4 Experience working with youth 12- 18 years in recr eation programs and social activities; 
/square4 Ability to safely and effectively supervise partic ipants and volunteers in recreation programs 
and social activities, and react to any emergency s ituation in a competent manner with 
excellent decision-making abilities and judgment; 
/square4 Knowledge of age-appropriate activities and behavi our management strategies; 
/square4 Ability to meet and deal tactfully and effectively  with parents, member of the public and fellow 
employees; 
/square4 Ability to work with minimal direct supervision an d in a team setting 
/square4 A team player.  Highly collaborative and collegial ’
