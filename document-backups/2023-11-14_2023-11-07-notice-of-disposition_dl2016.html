NOTICE 
ABANDONMENT OF LEASE  
 
Notice is hereby given that the 
Town of Ladysmith intends to 
abandon Crown land lease 
106431, file 1407751, over 
District Lot 2016 to the Province 
in accordance with section 45 of 
the 
Land Act. This abandonment 
will take place no later than 
December 31, 2023. The Town 
will not receive any 
compensation upon 
abandonment. 
 
Information regarding this 
proposed disposition can be 
obtained from Allison 
McCarrick, Chief Administrative 
Officer, 250.245.6401, 
amccarrick@ladysmith.ca.  
 
This Notice of Disposition (sale, 
lease or licence) is provided in 
accordance with section 26(3) 
of the 
Community Charter. 
DISTRICT LOT 2016, 
COWICHAN DISTRICT
