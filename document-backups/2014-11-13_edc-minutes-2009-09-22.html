MINUTES 
Economic Development Commission (EDC) 
Tuesday, September 22, 2009 
Council Chambers, City Hall at 7:00 a.m. 
 
LADYSMITH 
  
PRESENT: Chair – Peter Richmond; Members – Jeremy Lupien, Joan Phillps; Council 
Liaison – Steve Arnett; Manager of Development Services - Felicity Adams 
 
ABSENT/REGRETS:   John Craig, Alan Burrows, Moe Lessan  
 
 
1. C A L L  T O  O R D E R                     
P. Richmond called the meeting to order at 7:10 a.m.  
       
2. NOMINATION/ELECTION OF CHAIR 
Peter Richmond was nominated and accepted the nomination as Chair.  There were no 
further nominations.  P. Richmond was acclaimed as Chair. 
 
3. AGENDA APPROVAL 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the agenda of September 22, 2009 be approved. 
 
4. ADOPTION OF MINUTES 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the minutes of June 30, 2009 be adopted. 
 
5. BUSINESS ARISING FROM THE MINUTES 
 a. Consideration of resolution regarding support for the community marina 
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Economic Development Commission supports in 
principle the steps that Council is taking with respect to development of the community marina 
as a tourism infrastructure asset.  
 
6. CHAIR’S REPORT 
P. Richmond welcomed back the members and spoke to the EDC about focusing on priorities 
and simplifying its work.   
 
P. Richmond also reported that Community Futures will be setting up an office in Ladysmith.  
The office will be open one day a week for business planning and marketing.  They are 
currently looking for office space. 
 
7. NEW BUSINESS 
 a. District Lot 108 Update  
F. Adams, Manager of Development Services, provided an update on the DL108 
plans.  Councillor Arnett reported that Alex Campbell Jr. offered his expertise on 
sports field planning and marketing.  It was discussed that sports tournaments could 
provide leverage for moving forward with the new hotel.  The hotel project may be an 
opportunity to work with ED Cowichan to create an investor template. 
 
b. VIU Session  
J. Lupien will be attending the VIU session for the EDC.
