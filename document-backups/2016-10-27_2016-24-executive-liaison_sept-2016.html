The Town of Ladysmith is a vibrant community nestled on the eastern shores of spectacular Vancouver 
Island.  With a population of approximately 8,000 people, the Town provides an inviting small town atmosphere 
and yet is only a short commute to all of the amenities of a major urban centre.  Residents enjoy excellent 
community and recreational facilities, including year-round opportunities for outdoor enthusiasts. 
 
This is a newly created position that reports directly to the Chief Administrative Officer, providing executive 
level corporate administrative support (includes confidential matters) to the Chief Administrative Officer and 
Mayor and Council. 
    
The successful candidate will have a reputation as a results orientated professional who is passionate about 
public service and improving service delivery. Specifically, the candidate will have the ability and can 
demonstrate the following: 
•  ability to thrive in a fast-paced environment and successfully deal with multiple priorities; 
•  ability to organize and build administrative services systems that support the effectiveness of the 
Chief Administrative Officer, Mayor and Council and as required, the senior management team; 
•  ability to build and maintain positive relationships with people within and outside the organization; 
•  ability to draft and interpret policies, bylaws, resolutions and official documents; 
•  ability to ensure the respect, dignity and confidentiality of the Chief Administrative Office and Mayor 
and Council are maintained.  
The ideal candidate will have a Certificate in Local Government Administration and five (5) years related 
experience in a local government setting, specifically within corporate administration, as well as considerable 
knowledge of Council, committees and related official bodies and boards. The candidate will have considerable 
knowledge of the Community Charter, Local Government Act , and Freedom of Information and Protection of 
Privacy Act .   
 
The Town offers a generous benefits package and a competitive salary range, negotiable and commensurate 
with experience.  Many other advantages are also offered, including career and professional development 
opportunities, a progressive employee wellness program, a flexible schedule and a supportive work 
environment. The Town strives to be one of the Top 100 Employers in Canada.

TITLE: EXECUTIVE LIAISON 
 
DEPARTMENT: CITY MANAGER (FULL TIME, EXEMPT) 
 
 
JOB  SUMMARY:  
 
Reporting to the Chief Administrative Officer (CAO) , the Executive Liaison acts in a liaison capacity and 
provides executive-level corporate administrative s upport, often of a confidential nature, to the CAO,  the 
Mayor, members of Council, and members of the senio r management team.  
 
PRIMARY DUTIES &  RESPONSIBILITIES:  
 
 
•  In consultation with the CAO and senior staff, pro motes the Town’s vision to advance key corporate 
priorities through monitoring and evaluating the progress of Council’s strategic plan; 
•  A high degree of confidentiality is required in ma tters of a sensitive, political or controversial nature; 
•  Interacts with representatives of the provincial a nd federal government, boards, commissions, media 
and the general public in a professional manner wit h a sound appreciation of and sensitivity to the 
political issues affecting the Town; 
•  Maintains a high level of day-to-day contact with Council to ensure effective communication between 
the CAO and Council regarding operational matters; 
•  Provides coordination and follow-up for Council re ferrals and special projects; 
•  Maintains the meeting schedule and electronic cale ndar for the Mayor and Council and ensures the 
Mayor is informed of priorities, commitments and deadlines; 
•  Manages telephone calls, schedules appointments, r eceives visitors and responds to enquiries for the 
CAO, Mayor and members of Council; 
•  Investigates and coordinates the resolution of com plaints and problems brought to the attention of th e 
CAO, Mayor and members of Council;  
•  Contributes to the development of corporate polici es and strategic directions based on information 
from a variety of sources; 
•  Drafts reports for the CAO and Mayor as required; 
•  Facilitates meetings of the Interdepartmental Admi nistration Team to ensure ongoing 
communications, consistency and administrative best  practices amongst departments; 
•  Systemically reviews policies to ensure consistenc y with the corporation’s current priorities and 
strategic directions; 
•  Assists with Freedom of Information and Protection  of Privacy requests; 
•  Conducts research assignments, undertakes special projects and assists in the development of 
written and verbal staff reports to Council;
