UNFINISHED BUSINESS Derelict and Abandoned Vessels in Ladysmith Harbour 
 
 
Council reviewed past actions and options for further action with 
respect to the issue of derelict and abandoned vessels in Ladysmith 
Harbour.  Members expressed disappointment that the new 
provincial manual Dealing with Problem Vessels and Structures in BC 
Waters does not resemble a successful program used in Washington 
State, and frustration with the lack of control over the issue due to 
multiple jurisdictions and legislation regulating coastal waters.
