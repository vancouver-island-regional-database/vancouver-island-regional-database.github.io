BEAR ALERT 
NOTICE TO HOLLAND CREEK 
TRAIL USERS 
 
 
Please be aware that a baby bear has been spotted at Mackie 
Park area connecting to Holland Creek.  Please keep pets on a 
leash and children close to you.  Never approach or feed the 
bears or other animals. 
 
If you see a bear please call: 
Conservation Office 1-877-952-7277 
Town of Ladysmith Parks Supervisor 250-245-6448
