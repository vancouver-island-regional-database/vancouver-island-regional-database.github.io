MINUTES 
Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission (HRAC) 
Thursday, February 11, 2010 at 7:00 p.m. 
Council Chambers, City Hall 
 
 
   
PRESENT: Chair – Maureen Martin;  Members – Bruce Laxdal, John Hinde, Ann Rogers, Peter 
Blumel, Jennifer Lush; Council Liaison – Lori Evans; Planning Technician & Recorder 
– Stephanie Blumensaat 
 
 
The Chair called the meeting to order at 7:02 p.m. 
 
1.         AGENDA       
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of February 11, 2010 be approved as amended 
with the following additions: 
7.a - Ladysmith Taglines 
7.b - Heritage Plaques 
7.c - Aboriginal Tourism Workshop 
8.a - Traveller’s Hotel 
 
2. MINUTES 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of January 14, 2010 be approved. 
  
3.         COUNCIL REFERRALS/UPDATES 
            None. 
 
4.         SIGN/FAÇADE APPLICATIONS 
             Façade/Sign Development Permit Application 3060-09-12 Update 
 Brian & Cheryl Bancroft – 411 First Avenue and 30 Roberts Street 
 
S. Blumensaat reported that the HRAC’s feed back regarding the proposed awning for 411 
First Avenue was forwarded to the applicant. The applicant maintained their desire to keep 
with the original awning design. The permit has been approved. 
  
At future meetings, to assist with the review of façade/sign applications, S. Blumensaat will 
circulate a Downtown Specified Area binder which includes photos of the facades, signage 
and awnings of all buildings in the downtown area.  
  
5.  CURRENT PROJECT UPDATES/NEXT STEPS  
a. Community Heritage Register Update Project 
S. Blumensaat confirmed the final drafts of the Statements of Significance have been 
completed by the Consultant. The SOSs will be forwarded to Council for their review 
at the March 1, 2010 Council meeting. 
 
b. St. John’s Anglican Church Feasibility Study Update 
S. Blumensaat reported that the St. John ’s Anglican Church Feasibility Study has 
been completed. The Study will now be reviewed by the St. John’s building committee 
and then by the entire St. John’s congregation.  
 
Members discussed the news that the three nearby Anglican Churches, including St. 
John’s, may be amalgamating and how this may affect whether the St. John’s 
Anglican Church follows through on the recommendations outlined in the Feasibility 
Study.

c. Heritage Building Brochure Update 
Members reviewed and discussed the two proposed heritage building brochures. 
Members agreed that a determination of th e most acceptable way to identify/name 
buildings in the brochure(s) needs to be completed. Members and Staff will consult 
with heritage contacts regarding this matter. 
 
6. CORRESPONDENCE 
 None. 
 
7. NEW BUSINESS  
a. Ladysmith Taglines  
P. Blumel requested clarification regarding the usage of two Ladysmith taglines, “A 
View to Sea” and “Heritage by the Sea”. L. Evans confirmed “A View to Sea” was 
adopted three years ago by the Tourism Advisory Committee to be utilized for tourism 
promotional activities. 
 
S. Blumensaat explained the Communications Plan, recently adopted by Council, will 
include a review of Ladysmith taglines. 
 
b. Heritage Plaques 
P. Blumel circulated photos of heritage plaques found in other towns. L. Evans 
reminded Members that Council had inquired whether buildings in Ladysmith 
receiving Statements of Significance would be given a recognition plaque.  
 
c. Aboriginal Tourism Workshop 
 A. Rogers reported the Aboriginal Tourism workshop will not be proceeding.  
  
8. BUSINESS ARISING FROM THE MINUTES 
 
 a. Traveller’s Hotel 
B. Laxdal shared his analysis of the BC Buil ding Code section pertaining to buildings 
which are on a Community Heri tage Register, such as the Traveller’s Hotel. Members 
would still like the opportunity to have the Mayor attend a HRAC to further discuss 
the Traveller’s Hotel. 
 
9. NEXT MEETING 
 Thursday, March 11, 2010 at 7:00 p.m.  – Council Chambers, City Hall  
 
10. ADJOURNMENT 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the meeting be adjourned at 8:20 p.m.
