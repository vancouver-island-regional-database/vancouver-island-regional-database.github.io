DELEGATIONS Len Manuel, Boys and Girls Clubs of Central Vancouver Island 
Pink Shirt Day, February 20, 2012 
L. Manuel presented Council members with a pin to mark Pink 
Shirt Day , provided a brief overview  of the annual occasion 
designed to raise awareness of and prevent bullying amongst  
youth, and thanked the Town of Ladysmith for its support of Pink 
Shirt Day. 
 
Kayley McKinley, Cassandra Plourde, Brendan Wilkinson,  
Project: Reel Life 
K. McKinley, C. Plourde and B. Wilkinson presented a short video 
and Power Point presentation regarding the proposed youth -run 
movie theatre in Ladysmith, and requested Council’s support of 
the initiative.  The priority for the group is to find a suitable 
location for the movie theatre as soon as possible.   

 
Council Meeting Minutes: February 20, 2012 Page 2 
 
Council thanked the members of Project: Reel Life for their 
initiative and offered to assist  them in their search for location 
and funding. 
 
Karen Fediuk, Parent Advisory Council, Ecole Davis Road 
Request for Town Support in Improving Parking at Ecole Davis 
Road 
K. Fediuk provided a brief presentation of the difficulties with the 
current drop-off and parking situation in the vicinity of Ecole 
Davis Road on Parkhill Terrace and requested that the Town 
consider providing funding and, if necessary, staff support to 
address the issues. 
 
Council acknowledged the concerns of parents at the school, and 
advised that while school parking and drop -off locations are the 
responsibility of School District 68, the Town has offered to 
partner with the School District to address the concerns 
expressed by Ms Fediuk and the specific issues associated with 
the location. 
 
Council encouraged Ms Fe diuk to make a presentation to the 
Board of School District 68 and to advise Council when this will 
take place, in order for Council members to attend in support.

Stage Two Completion — Waste Water Treatment Plant 
It was moved, seconded and carried that 2012 Budget pre -
approval be given for the expenditure of up to $550,000 to 
complete the Stage Two Waste Water Treatment Plant upgrade 
project, in order to facilitate the trucking of ‘dry’ biosolids to the 
Comox Valley Regional District, starting in summer 2012; and 
that direction be given to staff to negotiate with the current 
contractor, Knappett Projects , to obtain prices to undertake this 
work in a timely manner. 
 
Preliminary Report on 2012 to 2016 Financial Plan – General 
Discussion on Water and Sewer Budgets 
Staff gave a presentation on the proposed budget for the Town’s 
sewer and water services, including capital costs for system 
upgrades, for the period 2012 to 2016.  
 
Council and staff responded to questions from members of the 
public regarding various aspects of the proposed budget 
including: 
• The availability of grant funding to support improvements to 
the water treatment system 
• The cost of borrowing funds to support improvements to the 
sewer and water systems 
• Options for cost recovery in the Town’s water system 
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the preliminary report 
on the 2012 to 2016 Financial Plan ( Water and Sewer Funds) be 
received.

 CS 2012-051 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Proposed Resolution Regarding Abandoned and Derelict Vessels 
for Consideration at the Association of Vancouver Island 
Municipalities Annual Convention 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the following resolution 
regarding derelict and abandoned vessels be adopted and that it 
be submitted for consideration at the 2012 Annual Convention of 
the Association of Vancouver Island and Coastal Communities. 
 
WHEREAS derelict and abandoned vessels in the waters of 
coastal British Columbia can pose a threat to the aesthetics, 
environment, health and safety of coastal communities;  
 
AND WHEREAS the current regulatory regime for the removal of 
derelict and abandoned vessels from the waters of coastal 
British Columbia is not serving our communities with effective 

 
Council Meeting Minutes: February 20, 2012 Page 5 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 CS 2012-052 
and timely removal of such vessels; 
 
THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT the Association of Vancouver 
Island and Coastal Communities call upon the federal and 
provincial governments to implement a Dereli ct Vessel Removal 
Program model ed after the Washington State program, and to 
designate the Canadian Coast Guard as the receiver of wreck in 
the case of every abandoned or derelict vessel in the waters of 
coastal British Columbia.
