INFORMATION FOR SURVEYORS 
BUILDING PERMIT SURVEY INFORMATION 
 
Please provide the following information on site surveys prepared for 
building permits in the Town of Ladysmith. 
 
1. 
 Building envelope  - illustration of minimum required zoning setbacks 
from all property lines with measurements (m) 
2.  Building location  - all buildings and structures (proposed and existing) 
with setbacks from property lines  
3.  Other requirements  such as rights of way, easements, covenant areas, 
SPEA, if applicable 
4.  Retaining wall height , if applicable 
5.  Driveway  – garage slab elevation, centreline elevation at back of 
sidewalk/curb, and length and width of new driveway 
6.  Land use regulations - provide a table on the survey (reference Town 
of Ladysmith Zoning Bylaw) 
Zone  
Parcel Size  
Parcel Coverage (%)  
Proposed building height (m)  
Maximum permitted building height (m)  
Proposed perimeter wall height (R-1 Zone only)  
Average natural grade  
Average finished grade
