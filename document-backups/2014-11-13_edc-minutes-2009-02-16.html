MINUTES 
Economic Development Commission (EDC) 
Monday, February 16, 2009 at 7:00 a.m. 
Council Chambers, City Hall 
 
LADYSMITH 
 
PRESENT: Chair – Peter Richmond ; Members – Rob Waters, Alan Burrows, Moe Lessan, 
Joan Phillps; Councillor Liaison – Steve Arnett; Manager of Development 
Services -  Felicity Adams; Recorder – Diane Webber;  
 
ABSENT/REGRETS:  Jeremy Lupien 
 
 
1. C A L L  T O  O R D E R                     
P. Richmond called the meeting to order at 7:05 a.m.   
 
2. AGENDA APPROVAL  
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of February 16, 2009 be approved 
with the addition of 6 b) Property Taxes, 7 e) Visioning Report and 8 b) Home Hardware 
Opening.          
 
3. ADOPTION OF MINUTES  
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of the January 26, 2009 meeting 
be adopted. 
 
4. CHAIRS REPORT 
Chair, P. Richmond reported on the following: 
• Employment Navigators hired TORO to prepare a report “Ladysmith Labour 
Forecast”.  The report compared Ladysmith to Summerland.  Summerland has a 
youth entrepreneur program that the EDC sponsored.  Ann McGillvary, Director of 
Employment Navigators, would like to partner with the EDC to recruit workers to 
the area.   
• Attended the TAC meeting.  Suggested Victoria’s “Be a Tourist in Your Own Town” 
strategy and asked R. Waters to put this idea forward to the TAC. 
• Attended the Home Business Show, it was a full house and there are lots of new 
businesses in the area.  Some of the businesses at the show had a couple of 
issues:   
1. The parking in the downtown area  -  construction workers parking in customer 
parking spots.   
2. Lack of affordable housing – would like to see legalized suites.  
• Spoke at the Leadership BC Ladysmith -  Recommended supporting the 
Leadership BC, a program through the Chamber of Commerce – in 3rd year. 
• Economic Cowichan has a new website.  Opportunity for local businesses to be 
included in a resource directory.  EDC will encourage Chamber of Commerce, 
Ladysmith Downtown Business Association and Coronation Mall to jump on 
board. 
• February issue of Business Vancouver Island – there were no articles on 
Ladysmith – will talk to the Chamber of Commerce about getting news in the

• Langford Mayor – attracting new businesses through tax breaks.  Asked that

5. MANAGER’S REPORT       
a. Tourism Initiatives 
UBCM Tourism funding has been awarded and the TAC is working on a plan for the 
funds with a focus on signage, marketing, promoting the website and using the old 
train station as a VIC. 
 
The members asked for an update on the following project: 
• GPM Developments Ltd. – Rezoning application – green initiatives 
F. Adams reported this application is going to Public Hearing on March 2, 2009 and 
that it is for 190 residential units.  Staff are working with the developers regarding 
the impact they will have on the sewage system and recycling of the grey water. 
 
b. Budget 
The EDC budget will focus on Tourism and EDC priorities. 
 
6. COMMITTEE REPORTS                  
a.  Tourism Advisory Committee 
R. Waters reported the following: 
• The TAC met in January for the first time since September, 2008.  Meetings 
have been cancelled because of weather and staff shortage.  The meeting 
was primarily a catch up meeting.  P. Richmond and John Surtees, of the 
LDBA, attended this meeting. 
• UBCM funding approved $53,000 and Tourism BC funding was reduced to a 
potential $14,000.  
• There is a new tourism industry e-newsletter introduced by Take 5 and they 
are looking to the TAC to adopt it.  The TAC members are waiting for another 
month to look at it.   
• Mayor R. Hutchins also attended the meeting and reported that Council has 
given direction to relocate two of the Town event signs. 
• The TAC has a new meeting schedule and will start to meet on the first 
Wednesday of the month at 4:00 p.m. 
• There will be a Heritage Tourism workshop on Saturday, February 28, 2009 at 
the Eagles hall from 9:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. organized by HRAC. 
• The VIC should be moving back to First Avenue on or around May 1, 2009.

     b. Property Taxes  
The Chair raised business property tax rates and how Ladysmith compares to other 
similar or near communities.  Also what is the “typical” cost to do business in Town? 
ie; Business Licence, Development Permit and Property Tax.  And, what incentives 
were offered to keep Black Press in Ladysmith. 
 
7. BUSINESS ARISING FROM THE MINUTES 
a. EDC Project Priorities 
Members reviewed the EDC projects from the strategic Plan.  The following projects 
were prioritized. 
• Business Retention and Expansion, including connections with LDBA, 
Chamber of Commerce and Coronation Mall Merchants. 
• Action planning (communications) for recruitment of Professional Services 
and Knowledge-based Business. 
• Marine sector survey follow-up. 
• Tourism sector activities. 
• Visioning – Green Industry and Food sector. 
• Budget funding was requested for communications, business retention and 
enhancement and touring investors. 
• Two new workgroups were formed. 
-     Green Industry – R. Water and A. Burrows. 
- Food and Beverage/Agriculture – P. Richmond, M. Lessan and J. 
Phillips. 
 
b. Local Harbour Management Committee 
R. Waters met with Doug Bell.  Once a new Chief for the Chemainus First Nation has 
been elected then the Regional District, Town of Ladysmith and Chemainus First 
Nation could meet. 
 
c. Marine Sector Survey 
J. Phillps will be meeting with J. Lupien to discuss the survey.  P. Richmond requested 
that they prepare a story to report on the results for the next EDC meeting. 
 
d. Professional Service and Knowledge Based Business Sector 
P. Richmond reported that the next step is to publicize stories from new businesses. 
 
e. Visioning Report 
A. Burrows has reviewed the Visioning report and requested the members to review 
the report.  A. Burrows reported that a short-term implementation activity is a 
sustainable economic development strategy.  Key areas are tourism; green and food 
industries; green business initiatives with LDBA and others; industrial lands strategy; 
waterfront; and others.
