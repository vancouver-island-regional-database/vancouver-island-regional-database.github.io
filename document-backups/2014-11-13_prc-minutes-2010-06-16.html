AGENDA APPROVAL 2010-12:  It was moved, seconded and carried that the agenda for the 
Commission meeting of Wednesday, Jun 16, 2010 be approved as 
amended.    
 
MINUTES APPROVAL 2010-13:  It was moved, seconded and carried that the minutes for the 
Commission meeting held Wednesday, May 19, 2010 be approved as 
presented.    
 
OLD BUSINESS Lot 108 Update – Field preparation progressing as scheduled. 
 
Invasive Species – Chantal Blumel, Wendy Waage and Lorena Mueller 
will be charting locations of several invasive species in Ladysmith and will 
present information at the next meeting. 
 
Brown Drive Park – Kinsmen Playground – Commission members 
working on this subcommittee discussed the donations received to date 
including $50,000 from the Town and if acceptable, $15,000 worth of 
wood fibre from  Mike Steeves.  Playground equipment has been selected 
from Henderson Playground, which is a treated scotch pine.  The total 
cost of the playground will be approximately $232,000.  
 
Gazebo & Washroom – The grand opening went well.  Staff will be 
working with Shirley Blackstaff to have interpretative signage completed. 
 
NEW BUSINESS Special Occasion Licence Request - Special Occasion License Requests –  
Commissioners reviewed the two applications.  
 
2010-14:  It was moved, seconded and carried that the Parks, 
Recreation & Culture Commission re commend that Council consider 
approving the Special Occasion requests for
 Ladysmith Kinsmen 
Tournament to be held at Aggie Fields on Jun 19 & 20 11:00am to 
6:30pm and Fraternal Order of Eagles to be held at Transfer Beach 
in the Horseshoe Pitch area on August 8 from 10:00am to 4:00pm.
