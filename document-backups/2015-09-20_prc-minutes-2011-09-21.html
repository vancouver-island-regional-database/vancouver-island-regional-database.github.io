OLD BUSINESS Kinsmen Park at Brown Drive – Second phase was installed in July; area near 
play equipment has been seeded; picnic tables will be installed soon. 
 
Dogs Off-Leash Park – Commissioners discussed the areas they were given to 
consider. Staff to determine if the strip of land at the end of Methuen on Sixth 
Avenue can be considered for a dog-off leash park as well.  Further discussion 
will take place at the next meeting. 
 
 
NEW BUSINESS Parks, Recreation & Culture Assessment – Commissioners considered delaying 
the assessment in the event that a larger-scale assessment will take place if it is 
decided to update the strategic plan.  It was decided to continue with plans for 
the assessment to be carried out. 
 
Letter from Rita Cawthra, Pickleball player – Commissioners reviewed the letter 
dated September 18, 2011 that requested that the Commission consider 
recommending to Council that a multi-purpose sports box be constructed at 
Forrest Field.
