Public Art Committee 
Agenda for Meeting to be held on  
Monday, November 6th, 2023 at 4:00pm  
Frank Jameson Community Centre at 810 6th Avenue 
 
 
The purpose of the Public Art Committee is to assist the Town with providing advice and 
recommendations to Council on specific Public Art projects, including selection processes, 
acceptance of grants, gifts, donations, bequests and deaccession as referred by Council.   
 
The Public Art Committee has two functions:  
  (a) To advise the Town on public art issues and trends relevant to public art initiative in Town.  
 (b) To advise on specific issues, such as donations, deaccession, and commissions of work.
