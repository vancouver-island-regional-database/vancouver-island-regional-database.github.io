TOWN OF LADYSMITH JOB DESCRIPTION 
 
 
TITLE:    LIFEGUARD INSTRUCTOR I 
 
DEPARTMENT:  PARKS, RECREATION AND CULTURE 
 
REPORTING TO: 
•  Aquatic Programmer  
JOB SUMMARY: 
Under the direction of the Aquatic Programmer, this  position is responsible for 
ensuring the safety of patrons in and around the po ol area, maintaining the pool 
area in a safe and clean condition, exhibiting lead ership qualities while instructing 
and lifeguarding within the aquatic programs offere d, and providing general 
supervision of pool activities. 
 
PRIMARY RESPONSIBILITIES: 
/circle6 Lifeguards and instructs assigned programs; 
/circle6 Promotes an atmosphere of good relations with the public and staff; 
/circle6 Ensures that aquatic activities are carried out in a safe manner and in accordance with 
established rules and regulations; 
/circle6 Performs various janitorial tasks including: cleani ng, disinfecting and scrubbing the deck, 
hot tub tile, sauna and pool change room floors; 
/circle6 Occasionally takes cash for swim sessions and answers public inquiries; 
/circle6 Performs pool maintenance tasks as required, includ ing such duties as indicated on the 
Daily Guard Report; 
/circle6 Performs related work as required. 
DESIRED TRAINING AND EXPERIENCE: 
The incumbent must be able to work with other membe rs of the aquatic staff as a team, together 
sharing professional skills in planning, organizing  and executing programs, and should display 
excellent public relation skills. Minimum 1 year experience 
CURRENT KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS & ABILITIES: 
•  Grade 12  Education 
•  Red Cross Water Safety Instructor 
•  National Lifeguard Service Award – Pool Option 
•  CPR, Level C     
•  Increased consideration may be given to those who hold any of the following: 
*Aquatic Emergency Care  *First Aid Instructor 
*BCRPA Aquafit Instructor  *Pool Operators Level 1 and 2 
*Lifesaving Society Instructor  *Post Secondary Edu cation
