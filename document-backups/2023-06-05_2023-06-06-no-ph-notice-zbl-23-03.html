NOTICE OF BYLAW CONSIDERATION  
WITHOUT A PUBLIC HEARING 
 
Zoning Bylaw Amendment 
Bylaw No. 2140 
June 6, 2023, 7:00pm 
 
Notice is hereby given that pursuant to section 467 of the Local Government Act, the Town of Ladysmith 
will consider “Town of Ladysmith Zoning Bylaw 2014, No. 1860, Amendment Bylaw 2023, No. 2140” and 
that a public hearing is not required under section 464 (2) of the Local Government Act . Council will 
consider first reading on Tuesday, June 6, 2023 at 7:00pm and may give up to three readings of the bylaw 
at that meeting. 
 
Affected Lands 
Bylaw No. 2140 will apply to all properties that: 
• Are within a zone that permits secondary suites, coach houses, two-unit dwellings (duplexes), 
multiple-unit dwellings or any non-residential use; or 
• Are within the Downtown area; or 
• Are zoned R-3 or R-3-A. 
 
What is the Proposal About? 
Bylaw No. 2140 proposes amendments to the “Town of Ladysmith Zoning Bylaw 2014, No. 1860” to align 
with policies in the recently adopted “Official Community Plan Bylaw 2022, No. 2200”. If approved, 
Bylaw No. 2140 will: 
• Amend the definitions for secondary suite and two-unit dwelling. 
• Amend the secondary suite and coach house regulations, including:  
o to allow secondary suites within townhouse dwellings and two -unit dwellings , where 
permitted by the applicable zone. 
o to prohibit secondary suites and coach houses from being stratified or subdivided from the 
main dwelling. 
• Allow secondary suites as an accessory use within the Medium Density Residential (R-3) and Low 
Density Residential (R-3-A) zones. 
• Add definitions for “long-term bicycle parking space ”, “oversized bicycle parking space ”, and 
“short-term bicycle parking space”. 
• Amend the parking and loading regulations, including: 
o not requiring on-site vehicle parking for non-residential uses in the Downtown. 
o new “payment in-lieu of parking” regulations. 
o new bicycle parking regulations, including new design standards. 
 
Where Can I Get More Information? 
A copy of the proposed bylaw  may be viewed at www.ladysmith.ca/currentapplications or in person at 
the Town of Ladysmith City Hall located at 410 Esplanade or the Development Services Office located at 
132C Roberts Street, Ladysmith, BC, until June 6, 2023.  Office hours are Monday to Friday 8:30am to 
4:00pm, excluding statutory holidays.
