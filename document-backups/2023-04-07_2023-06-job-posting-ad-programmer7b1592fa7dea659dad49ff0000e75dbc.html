Position Overview
Reporting to the Supervisor Community Programs and Services, the 
Programmer - Recreation and Culture is responsible for developing and 
monitoring programs related to the areas of recreation, culture, special 
events, and health and wellness.  The incumbent coordinates and imple-
ments a variety of programs and activities for the community and assists 
with ongoing evaluation and modification of services as required.  The 
Programmer - Recreation and Culture provides excellence in customer 
service while maintaining and developing partnerships with the objective 
of increasing community service awareness and participation.
Qualifications
 
• A diploma in recreation administration, leisure services management 
or relevant field, plus three (3) years’ recent (within the last five (5) 
years)/relevant employment experience or an equivalent combination 
of training and recent/relevant experience.
• Minimum of two (2) years’ prior job-related experience or an 
equivalent combination of training and experience, and one (1) year in 
a supervisory capacity.
• Valid standard first aid certification including CPR-C.
• Successful completion of high five trainer certification (BAHFT).
• Successful completion of Autism Awareness for Recreation Leaders 
course.
• Satisfactory police information and vulnerable sector check.
• Valid B.C. Driver’s License – Class 5.
 
Additional Information
• Employees currently receive Wellness Initiatives, including use of 
facilities at Frank Jameson Community Center. 
• Benefits including Extended Health, Dental, Group Life and AD&D.
• Municipal Pension Plan with employer and employee contributions.
• Paid sick leave. 
• Hours of work:  35 hours/week
• $36.81/hour
