Addendum 3
Issue Date: January 10, 2023
Project Name: Lot 108
Expanded Parking and Practice Pitch
Project Number: 1101-001
This Addendum is an integral part of the Tender Documents for the project referenced above. The
following revisions supersede the information contained in the original drawings, specifications or
previously issued Addenda. The Tenderer shall acknowledge this addendum by noting the number of
Addenda in their submission.
A non-mandatory site meeting was held onsite on January 10, 2023 as per Addendum 1.  The
following questions were asked during the site meeting.
1.0 Questions and Answers:
.1 What is the expected construction timeframe for the project?
A1: The construction schedule is flexible, but it is expected that the contractor will provide their
expected schedule of construction for the project.
.2 Is percolation testing required as part of this project?
A2: No, percolation testing is not required.
.3 There are concrete curb stops in the onsite dump area identified in Addendum 2.  Is moving the curb
stop the responsibility of the contractor?
A3: No, Town Forces will move the concrete curb stops.
