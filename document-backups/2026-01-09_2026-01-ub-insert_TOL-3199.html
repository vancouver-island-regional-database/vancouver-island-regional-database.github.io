BUSINESS LICENCE RENEWALS 
DUE BY FEBRUARY 28
The T own of Ladysmith inter-municipal business licences 
with Duncan, North Cowichan and Lake Cowichan are now 
available. 
The cost of an annual business licence is $100. Renew by 
February 28, 2026 to avoid a $25 late payment fee.
Ladysmith business  owners who  carry out work in Central 
Vancouver Island municipalities can purchase an Inter-
Community Business Licence for an additional $170.00, 
making it $270.00 for the pair. Following are the participating 
municipalities and districts: Campbell River, Parksville, 
Qualicum Beach, Courtenay, Port Alberni, Cumberland, 
Duncan, Lake Cowichan, Comox, Lantzville, Sooke, North 
Cowichan and Nanaimo.

2026 DOG LICENSES
All dogs over the age of six months must be licensed in the 
T own of Ladysmith. Annual dog licences are available at City 
Hall (410 Esplanade) during regular office hours, the Frank 
Jameson Community Centre (810 6th Avenue) and For Pet’s 
Sake pet store (416 1st Avenue).
The fees for dog licences are as follows: For neutered or 
spayed dogs, the fee is $31 if purchased by January 31, and 
$41 if purchased between February 1 and December 31. For 
dogs that are not neutered or spayed, the fee is $49 until Jan-
uary 31, and $59 between February 1 and December 31.
Coastal Animal Control Services provides animal control ser-
vices and pound facilities for the T own of Ladysmith. Coastal 
Animal Control is located at 2202 Herd Road, Duncan, BC, 
and can be reached on a 24-hour basis at 1.250.732.7720.

NEW GARBAGE COLLECTION 
VENDOR
The town has a new garbage collection contractor - GFL 
Environmental, and 2026 collection schedules are now 
available. In 2026, each household can place one garbage 
container at the curb. The container must be no larger 
than 120 litres and weigh no more than 20 kilograms (44 
pounds) when full.
Rules for recycling and organic containers remain the 
same: 
Residents can place unlimited blue recycling bins or yel-
low recycling bags at the curb, that contain accepetd 
items.
Each household may place one organic container at the 
curb, no larger than 45 litres (10 gallons). Compostable 
or biodegradable plastics bags are not permitted in your 
tote/bin. Paper bags or newspaper can be used as an 
alternative.
The list of acceptable items remains unchanged. T o find 
out what is acceptable and what route you’re on, please 
visit www.ladysmith.ca/our-services/garbage-recycling.
In case of inclement weather, GFL plans to deploy a sec-
ond truck the following day to pick up any missed collec-
tions. If severe weather causes delays beyond one day, 
residents will need to hold their materials until their next 
scheduled collection day.
For future service issues, please call 250.245.6400 and 
select option 8 to connect directly with GFL.
