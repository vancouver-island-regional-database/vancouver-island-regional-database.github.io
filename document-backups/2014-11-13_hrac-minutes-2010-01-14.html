MINUTES 
Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission (HRAC) 
Thursday, January 14, 2010 at 7:00 p.m. 
Council Chambers, City Hall 
 
 
   
PRESENT: Chair – Maureen Martin;  Members – Bruce Laxdal, John Hinde, Ann Rogers, Peter 
Blumel, Jennifer Lush; Council Liaison – Lori Evans; Planning Technician & Recorder 
– Stephanie Blumensaat 
 
 
The Chair called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m. 
 
1.         AGENDA       
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of January 14, 2010 be approved. 
 
2. MINUTES 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of December 10, 2009 be approved. 
  
3.         COUNCIL REFERRALS/UPDATES 
             Councillor Evans reported that at the January 4, 2010 Council meeting, Council made a 
 motion  to support the preparation of Statements of Significance for the five buildings the 
 Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission recommended be added to the Ladysmith 
 Community Heritage Register. L. Evans also  shared Council’s questions and suggestions 
 regarding the Ladysmith Community Heritage Register. 
  
4.         SIGN/FAÇADE APPLICATIONS 
             Façade/Sign Development Permit Application 3060-09-12 
 Brian & Cheryl Bancroft – 411 First Avenue and 30 Roberts Street 
 
 The Development Permit proposal (3060-09-12) for the façade improvement was circulated 
 to Members.   
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission 
recommend a continuous awning be installed, at 411 First Avenue and 30 Roberts Street, for 
aesthetic purposes and to provide pedestrians with protection from the elements. 
 
 Members supported the style and colour of the signage. It was noted that if a continuous 
 awning is installed the signage would need to be relocated.
