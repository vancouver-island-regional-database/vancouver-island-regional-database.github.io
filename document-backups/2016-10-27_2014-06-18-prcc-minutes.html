OLD BUSINESS 
Sports Tourism – Clayton Postings and John Perry  are working with CVRD and 
North Cowichan staff to identify a pl an for managing large-scale sporting 
events.  Upon completion, it will be presented to the CVRD board for approval. 
The meeting will be held on July 30, 6:30pm at the CVRD office. 
 
Dogs on Holland Creek Trail – Council has invited the Animal Control Officer to 
attend an upcoming meeting of Council in order to discuss bylaw enforcement 
options for the regulation of dogs in Town parks prior to considering the 
recommendations contained in the staff report from the Director of Parks, 
Recreation and Culture entitled Dogs on Holland Creek Trail dated May 1, 
2014.  
 
Playground Equipment at Transfer Be ach – Kinsmen Club of Ladysmith are 
seeking fundraising opportunities to ra ise funds to replace aging playground 
equipment at Transfer Beach.   
 
2014-11:  It was moved, seconded and carried that the Parks, Recreation and 
Culture Commission issue a letter of support to the Kinsmen Club of Ladysmith 
to be used for fundraising applications. 
 
Frisbee Golf – Kinsmen Club of Ladysmith have withdrawn their request to 
create a Frisbee golf course in Ladysmith. 
 
Trans Canada Trail – There is a push to have the trail completed by 2017 
(Canada’s 150
th Anniversary); regional funds may be available soon. 
 
2014-12:  It was moved, seconded and carried that the Parks, Recreation and 
Culture Commission recommend that Counci l consider directing staff to begin 
planning for further Trans Canada Trail development.

2014-13:  It was moved, seconded and carried that the Parks, Recreation and

amendments to Town of Ladysmith Community Centre and Facilities Fees and 
Charges Bylaw 2013, No. 1835.  
 
 increase admission and pass fees by three percent effective September 
1, 2014 for  adult, youth, child, family and senior (continue no charge for 
children under three or seniors over eighty);  
 increase facility rental fees by four percent effective September 1, 2014;  
 increase sport field light rates by nine percent September 1, 2014; 
 add Aggie and FJCC sport field lighting  to fees and charges schedule at 
half the rate of Forrest field lights; 
 establish fees for adult use of the sports fields; 
 add a special event and park permit fee for the non-facility areas of the 
parks; 
 standardize facility room rental rates to ensure consistency of like 
facilities;  
 update the policy to include replace the existing user classifications to 
adult, youth, child, senior and family and the update age ranges.  
 
Upper Transfer Beach Park – Commissi on members reviewed staff report

Gas Tax Funding (GTF) – Each year, the federal GTF assists municipalities by 
providing funding for local infrastructure projects.  As of April 1, 2014, 
communities are able to use GTF towards a wider range of projects including: 
culture; tourism; sport and recreation.   
Lot 108 –Commission was informed that completing the park at Lot 108 is a
