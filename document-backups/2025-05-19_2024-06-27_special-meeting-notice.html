NOTICE 
SPECIAL COUNCIL MEETING  
 
 
 
Pursuant to “Council Procedure Bylaw 2009, No. 1666” notice is provided that Council will 
hold a Special Meeting on June 27, 2024.  The meeting will include the following business: 
• Small Scale Multi-Unit Housing and other related Housing Amendment Bylaws for 
the purposes of complying with section 481.3 (Zoning bylaws and small-scale multi-
family housing) of the Local Government Act. 
 
Please note:  the location of this Special Meeting is City Hall Council Chambers, 410 
Esplanade.
