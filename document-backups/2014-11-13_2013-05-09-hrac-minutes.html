MINUTES 
Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission (HRAC) 
Thursday, May 9, 2013 at 5:00 p.m. 
Council Chambers, City Hall 
 
 
  
    
PRESENT: Chair – Jennifer Robinson; Members – Allen McDermid, Tamara Hutchinson, 
Maureen Martin, Ann Rogers , Gerry Beltgens;  LDHS Member – Bernardien 
Knol; Council Liaison – Councillor Bill Drysdale; Staff Liaison and Recorder – 
Angela Vincent 
 
GUESTS: Ladysmith Maritime Society – Marnie Craig 
    Ladysmith Air Cadets – Cathy Gilroy 
 
REGRETS: Member – Roman Buta 
 
The meeting was called to order at 5:00 p.m.  
 
1. AGENDA   
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of May 9, 2013 be approved with the 
following addition: 4(a) Sign Permit Application – Ladysmith Air Cadets for Aggie Hall, 1110 
First Avenue.  
 
2. MINUTES 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of March 14, 2013 be adopted.  
 
3. COUNCIL REFERRALS/UPDATES 
a. Dunsmuir Railway Car 
 Councillor Drysdale explained that the Town of Ladysmith had been offered 
 the opportunity to acquire the Dunsmuir railway carriage. Council has recently 
 expressed interest in acquiring and rebuilding the railway carriage. A. Rogers 
 noted that having both a railway carriage and locomotive may split available 
 resources. 
 
b. Cadet’s Photo Mural 
 Councillor Drysdale reviewed a proposal from the Ladysmith Air Cadets to 
 work with the Town on the creation of a heritage photo mural display for the 
 interior of Aggie Hall, in celebration of the Cadet’s 70th anniversary.  
 
 B. Knol volunteered to assist the Cadets with the design of the mural. 
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission 
share half the cost of a heritage photo mural display with the Ladysmith Air Cadets. 
 
4. SIGN/FAÇADE APPLICTIONS 
a. Ladysmith Air Cadets at Aggie Hall, 1110 First Avenue 
 HRAC members had previously reviewed the proposed location of the Cadet 
 signage and the basic design concepts. C. Gilroy explained that the signage is 
 intended to be completed, but not installed, in time for the Cadet’s 
celebration in September. M. Martin noted that Town, LaFF and heritage 
signage should also be a priority for Aggie Hall.

recommends the signage proposal for the Ladysmith Air Cadets at Aggie Hall (1110 First

5. HERITAGE PROJECT UPDATES 
a.  Transfer Beach Pictorial 
  A. McDermid outlined his correspondence with the following groups: 
 Archives – interested in searching for appropriate photographs. 
 Christine Meutzner, Archivist – interested in writing content for project. 
 Project Reel Life – interested in interviewing the public (seniors  in 
 particular) regarding their memories of Transfer Beach.  
 
 Members considered the potential to incorporate additional video -taped 
 interview material, along with images or additional information into the 
 Pictorial through the use of QR codes. 
 
 Members reviewed the theme of the Pictorial, and determined that it should 
 provide a balanced outline of Transfer Beach ’s heritage , including Fist 
 Nations industrial use and recreational use. 
 
 A. Vincent presented photographs illustrating various panel  and pictorial 
 design concepts, and members discussed which concepts would b e 
 appropriate for the waterfront location.   
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that funds from the HRAC budget (when available) be 
used towards the Transfer Beach Pictorial by hiring an Archivist to assist in content writing, 
providing a donation to the Ladysmith Archives in exchange for researching archival photos, 
and hiring a designer to create a design concept for the panels and pictorial.  
 
  A. McDermid will invite Christine Meutzner to the next HRAC meeting to 
  discuss the Pictorial. 
 
b. Community Heritage Register Update 
  Members reviewed the evaluation criteria to be used in determining a priority 
  order for adding buildings to the Community Heritage Register  from the 
  Heritage Inventory . A. Rogers, G. Beltgens and J. Robinson volunteered to 
  complete the evaluation criteria . The completed priority list will be presented 
  at the next HRAC meeting. 
 
c. Heritage Strategic Plan Update 
  Members discussed options for updating the Heritage Strategic Plan,  
  intended to cover a 5-year period from 2008 to 2012. It was determined that 
  due to the number of strategies that are still pertinent, the Heritage Strategic 
  Plan would undergo a review by HRAC members and a minor update. J. 
  Robinson and A. Rogers volunteered to undertake the review during Jul y and 
  August 2013.

6. NEW BUSINESS 
 a. Ladysmith Maritime Society’s Heritage Strategic Plan 
 Ladysmith Maritime Society (LMS) representative M. Craig reviewed projects 
 completed by LMS, along with ideas for future projects , such as a dry dock 
 display, storyboards and a diorama, joint projects with the museum/archives.  
 
 LMS would like to take a more interactive role in the community, especially 
 with local heritage groups. They hope to encourage open communication and 
 a consistent heritage strategy. 
 
 HRAC members were interested in the potential for the groups to work 
 together, including sharing ideas for heritage strategic plans, and 
 involvement on the Transfer Beach pictorial.
