TOWN VEHICLES TO CARRY 
EVERY CHILD MATTERS DECAL 
The T own of Ladysmith’s fleet of vehicles will carry the 
‘Every Child Matters’ decal designed by Stz’uminus artist 
John Marston as we raise awareness about the impact of 
residential schools.
The decal initiative is being led by FortisBC in cooperation 
with Marston, who created the Welcome Figure 
for Ladysmith Secondary School and is currently also 
completing the Welcome to Ladysmith signs.
Every Child Matters was established to acknowledge 
the lost and missing children and the lasting impact of 
residential schools and colonization on Indigenous people, 
their families and communities.   
Marston’s decal design represents an eagle holding a child.
An event was held at City Hall in advance of National 
Indigenous Peoples Day and included the raising of the 
Stz’uminus First Nation flag. 
As part of the T own’s participation in the FortisBC 
initiative, Council authorized that $1,000 be donated to 
the Indian Residential School Survivor Society.
STAGE 3 WATER RESTRICTIONS
Stage 3 Water Restrictions are in effect for the Town of Ladysmith, Stz’uminus First Nation and the Diamond 
Improvement District and will remain in effect until further notice.
The Town moved to Stage 3 in early July due to the dry weather that Ladysmith and area is experiencing. 
Residents are encouraged to follow the guidelines for watering times and restrictions:  https://www.ladysmith.ca/
our-services/water.
Thank you for your cooperation in making every drop count.

ATVS ON TOWN TRAILS
All-terrain vehicles (ATVs) are fun to drive for many 
and allow riders to access off-road trails they might not 
easily reach on foot.
During the warmer summer months, please take 
extreme care with any activity in forested areas that 
could pose a risk of wildfire. 
Please be aware that only authorized ATVs and other 
motorized vehicles are allowed on all Town of Ladysmith 
trails. 
POVERTY REDUCTION 
STRATEGY SUPPORTS FRUIT 
RESCUE PROGRAM
Do you have extra fruit on trees or veggies in your 
garden that you could use some help picking? Each year, 
an abundance of apples, cherries, plums and more are 
grown locally, but not always harvested.
As part of the CommUNITY Together to End Poverty 
Hw-nuts’-ulwum Poverty Reduction Strategy, the Town 
of Ladysmith is supporting the Ladysmith Resource 
Centre Association (LRCA) in launching a Fruit Rescue 
project to make sure any extra food grown is harvested 
and shared within the community. 
How it will work: Area residents are invited to contact the 
LRCA’s Fruit Rescue Coordinator, Shannon Crowards, by 
emailing shannon@lrca.ca or calling 250-245-3079, if 
you have fruit trees or gardens that you would like help 
harvesting. 
After each harvest, one third of what is collected will go 
to the owner, one third will be shared with the volunteers 
and the last third will go to the LRCA Food Bank and/or 
other partner organizations who will distribute the fruit 
within the Ladysmith and Stz’uminus communities to 
those who need it.

LOCAL BURNING AND 
WILDFIRE BANS
BC Wildfire Service prohibits campfires throughout the 
Coastal Fire Centre, except for the Haida Gwaii Forest 
District. 
These prohibitions apply to all public and private land 
and help prevent human-caused wildfires and protect 
public safety. 
In addition to open fires being prohibited, the following 
activities and equipment are also restricted:
• Fireworks;
• Sky Lanterns;
• Burn Barrels or Burn Cages of any size or description;
• Binary Exploding T argets;
• Air curtain burners;
• Tiki and similar kind of torches; and,
• Chimineas.
WILDLIFE AWARE
Nature is right on our doorstep and this means always 
being mindful of how human activity impacts wildlife. 
At this time of year, wildlife can be spotted more 
frequently. Don’t leave garbage or compost outdoors. Use 
caution when on the trails. If you see a bear or cougar, 
please report it immediately. 
Reports of wildlife in an urban setting or reports of 
wildlife in conflict should always be reported to the BC 
Conservation Officer Service Conservation reporting 
line 1-877-952-7277 (RAPP) or #7277 on the TELUS 
Mobility Network. 
RECYCLING AUDITS
The T own sustainability program is back this summer, with 
our ambassadors Ava and T aylor completing recycling 
audits across the community. 
Ladysmith currently sits at a 4.2% contamination rate for 
its recycling, which includes only non-recyclable items. 
However, this statistic is likely higher as it doesn’t include 
items such as glass, soft plastics and flexible plastics. 
These items contribute to contamination and can instead 
be brought to either the CVRD Peerless Road Recycling 
Centre or the Junction Bottle Depot. 
We’re encouraging households to be aware of recycling 
best practices as we all work together towards our goal 
of waste reduction. If any contamination is found, it will 
be left in a clear bag along with a flyer containing more 
information on disposal options.
