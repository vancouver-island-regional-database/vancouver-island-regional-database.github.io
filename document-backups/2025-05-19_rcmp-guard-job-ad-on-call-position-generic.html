We’re Hiring at your local RCMP Detachment! 
Seeking a casual employment opportunity? Commissionaires Victoria, the Islands and Yukon is looking for 
candidates that have the commitment and flexibility to fill casual positions as an RCMP Detention Cell 
Guard. This position has no fixed work schedule and candidates are called in to work on an as-needed 
basis. To qualify for the position, you must: 
 Be 19 years of age or older 
 Undergo and pass an RCMP Reliability Clearance and Criminal Record Checks 
 Possess a valid First Aid Certification or willing to obtain one once hired 
 Live within 30 minutes traveling to you local RCMP Detachment 
NOTE: Physical handling of prisoners is not a job requirement for Detention Guards. Any movement of 
prisoners is conducted by the RCMP. 
 
 
Why Work for Commissionaires? 
 You will join a not-for-profit organization that ensures the maximum return to its employees 
 You will join a team of dedicated individuals that help make a difference in your community 
 You will receive a competitive pay package which includes: 
(Paid Sick, Vacation and Personal needs time off) + RRSP contributions
