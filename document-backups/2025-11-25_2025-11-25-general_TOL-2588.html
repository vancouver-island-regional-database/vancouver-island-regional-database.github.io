Higher L evel Service Request Cybersecurity Detection & Response Services (IT) Justification Cybersecurity Detection and Response service ensures 24/7 monitoring, expert-led incident response, and best practice compliance support. This will reduce risk exposure, minimize breach impact, and strengthening our overall IT security posture. Aligns with Strategic Priority: Core Infrastructure Consequence of not funding: Not funding a Cybersecurity service, the organization faces increased risk of undetected cyber threats that can result in corporate data loss, financial loss, legal liability, and reputational damage from leaked customer and employee data. Estimated Cost: $50,000 annually Funding Source: Taxation Estimated tax increase: 0.57% budget increase Celebrate our Present. Embrace our Future. Honour our Past.

Higher L evel Service Request Exempt Staff Compensation Consultants Justification As per the Exempt Staff Policy and Compensation,(Sec. 1 - Salary Administration) every four (4) years, the Town will complete an exempt employee total compensation review taking into consideration market data for cash compensation. Aligns with Strategic Priority: Leadership Consequence of not funding: Loss of Market Competitiveness - failure to conduct the required compensation review or maintain salaries that are within the 85th. Percentile could make the Town less competitive leading to difficulties attracting and retaining staff. Estimated Cost: $25,000 Funding Source: Surplus Estimated tax increase: 0 Celebrate our Present. Embrace our Future. Honour our Past.
