What is the Native Plant 
Giveway?
 
The Town’s Sustainability Program will be offering 
native, drought-resistant, and fire-resistant plants to 
Ladysmith residents. These will include trees, shrubs, 
perennials, fruit, flowering plants etc. There is a limit 
of 1 for each household. FireSmart BC will also be 
there to educate the community on how these fire-
resistant plants create a firesmart home.
What is the purpose?
 
An excellent and easy way to conserve water is 
by planting native plants that are adjusted to the 
local climate and are drought-resistant. This helps 
the community use less water throughout the dry 
summer and help conserve our limited water source.  
Native plants also attract pollinators which are 
important for our ecosystems and biodiversity.

How many plants will be 
available? 
 
The Town has approximately 120 plants that we 
will be giving away. To encourage widespread 
participation throughout the community, there is a 
limit of one plant for each household. 
Where can I find out 
more information? 
 
Visit our website (www.ladysmith.ca/sustainability) 
for an online brochures which includes a description 
of each of the plants, what conditions they prefer, 
and other tips for ensuring their success.
