Position Overview
Reporting to the Manager of Operations, the Student Sustainability 
Ambassador is responsible for providing water conservation, recycling, 
compost, and waste disposal education to residents and businesses, as 
well as providing irrigation audit services to the infrastructure services 
division.
Qualiﬁ cations
 
• Minimum grade 12 education or equivalent and some experience with 
public education and working directly with the general public.
• Valid B.C. driver’s license - class 5 with a clean abstract.
• Ability to safely operate an electronic or unassisted bicycle inclusive 
of necessary personal protective equipment.
