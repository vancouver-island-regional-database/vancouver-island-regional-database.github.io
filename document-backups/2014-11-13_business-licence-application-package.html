Do you plan on operating a business in Ladysmith? 
 
 
Ladysmith is actively creating a vibrant, healthy economy  
by encouraging and supporting growth and diversification. 
 
 
   
   
  
 
The Town of Ladysmith requires every business operating in Ladysmith to get a business licence, 
renew it yearly, update us with changes to your business name and/or address, and cancel the 
licence if your business is no longer in operation. 
 
Ladysmith Business Licence 
If your business is located in Ladysmith, you need a Town of Ladysmith Business Licence. 
 
Inter-Municipal Business Licence 
The Town of Ladysmith has a cooperative agreement for inter-municipal business licences with the 
City of Duncan, the District of North Cowichan, and the Town of Lake Cowichan.  If your business is 
located in one of these municipalities and you provide mobile services in Ladysmith, you do not need 
to purchase a separate business licence as long as you hold a valid business licence from the 
municipality where your business is located.   
 
If you open a business premise here, you will also need a Ladysmith business licence.   
 
Application Steps 
Please complete the attached Business Licence application form and submit it with your payment.  The 
cost of an annual business licence is $100.00.  If your business start-up falls after July 1
st, your business 
licence fee may be pro-rated to $50.00.  Payment may be made at City Hall by cash, Interac or cheque, or 
you can send a cheque by mail. Please ensure the cheque is payable to the “Town of Ladysmith”. 
 
Business Licence forms (application, cancellation, or name/address change) are available at City Hall or 
on the website at www.ladysmith.ca/business-development/business-resources. 
 
Business Regulations and Requirements 
Before submitting your business licence application, please review the “Regulations and Requirements 
Checklist” in this document for both commercial/industrial businesses and home-based businesses.  
Bylaw information is available at City Hall or at www.ladysmith.ca. 
 
Is your Business Located Downtown? 
Please review the special information package about Downtown Business/Buildings which outlines the 
process for applying for a sign permit or a façade development permit if you are planning to make exterior 
changes to the building where your business is located. 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 
Business Support Services 
Getting all of the paperwork together when starting a new business can be confusing.   In partnership 
with the Government of Canada and Province of British Columbia, the Town of Ladysmith is pleased to 
offer the service called “BizPal” through the Business & Development page on the Town’s website. This 
service assists entrepreneurs to determine which permits and licenses they need (federally, provincially 
and municipally) in order to open their business.  
 
Town of Ladysmith staff is available to provide assistance to entrepreneurs wishing to set up a new 
business in town whether it is relocating an existing company, expanding to Ladysmith or starting a new 
venture.  A variety of business resources are available online at www.ladysmith.ca/business-
development/business-resources. For information contact the Development Services Department at  
250-245-6415. 
 
The regional Economic Development Cowichan office is also available to help you grow and expand your 
business through the “Cowichan First” program   (www.cvrd.bc.ca/index.aspx?NID=590).  For information, 
contact Economic Development Cowichan 250-746-7880 or 1-866-746-2508.
