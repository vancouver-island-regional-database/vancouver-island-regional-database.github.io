NOTICE 
SPECIAL COUNCIL MEETING  
 
 
 
Pursuant to “Council Procedure Bylaw 2009, No. 1666” notice is provided that Council will 
hold a Special Meeting on Tuesday, May 9, 2023 at 6:30 p.m.  The meeting will include the 
following business: 
• Adoption of “Financial Plan Bylaw 2023, No. 2141” 
• Adoption of “Property Tax Rates Bylaw 2023, No. 2142” 
• Adoption of "Sewer Parcel Tax Bylaw 2023, No. 2143" 
• Adoption of "Water Parcel Tax Bylaw 2023, No. 2144”
