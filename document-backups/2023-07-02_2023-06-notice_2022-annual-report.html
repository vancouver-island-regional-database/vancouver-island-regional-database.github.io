Notice is given under section 99 of the Community Charter  that Town of Ladysmith Council will consider 
its 2022 Annual Municipal Report and receive submissions and questions from the public at its Regular 
Meeting as follows:
Date:  Tuesday, July 4, 2023
Time:  7:00pm
Location: Ladysmith Seniors Centre, 630 2nd Avenue, Ladysmith
The 2022 Annual Municipal Report is available for public inspection online or may be viewed at City Hall 
during regular business hours.
Anyone wishing to provide written comments to Council can submit them prior to noon on July 4, 2023, 
as follows:
Email:  info@ladysmith.ca;
In person: City Hall, 410 Esplanade
Mail:  Town of Ladysmith, PO Box 220, Ladysmith, BC, V9G 1A2
The meeting will be held in person at the Ladysmith Seniors Centre located at 630 2nd Avenue and also 
livestreamed on the Town’s YouTube channel.  To view the livestream, please go to our “Council Minutes, 
Agendas and Video” webpage.
Corporate Officer (M. O’Halloran)
