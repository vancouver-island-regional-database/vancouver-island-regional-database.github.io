BAND:   STUDENT  
 
 
GENERAL ACCOUNTABILITY 
Reporting to the Manager of Operations, the Student Sustainability Ambassador is responsible for providing 
water conservation, recycling, compost, and waste disposal education to residents and businesses, as well as 
providing irrigation audit services to the infrastructure services division.  
 
PRIMARY DUTIES AND ACCOUNTABILITIES 
o Monitor residential water use, identify water waste and distribute water conservation information. 
o Identify public events (festivals, community events) throughout the summer where public displays can be 
set up and hosted. 
o Regularly set up and host a public display to distribute water conservation and recycling information. 
o Identify community groups (e.g.: Eagles, LRCA, Clubs, strata councils etc.) that would be interested in a 
water conservation and recycling presentation; deliver presentations. 
o Conduct curbside recycling audits 
o Develop strategies and information to promote and educate the public on solid waste reduction and 
recycling 
o Collect, compile, and analyze qualitative and quantitative fixture and behavior survey data from businesses 
and/or residents. 
o Submit weekly reports on residential visits, public interaction and irrigation audits. 
o Work with various non-profit/non-governmental organizations to provide information for advertising or 
other information going out to the public. 
o Provide end-of-summer report to Infrastructure Services. 
o Maintain courteous and professional interactions with the public, user groups and Town employees. 
o Attends professional development training as directed. 
o Other related duties as required. 
 
REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS, KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS AND ABILITY 
1. Minimum grade 12 educat ion or equivalent and some experience with public education and working 
directly with the general public. 
2. Valid B.C. driver’s license - class 5 with a clean abstract. 
3. Ability to safely operate an electronic or unassisted bicycle inclusive of necessary personal protective 
equipment. 
4. Demonstrated ability to fulfill assigned duties in all weather conditions.  
5. Demonstrated basic ability in the use of Microsoft Office and similar computer applications. 
6. Demonstrated passion for water conservation, recycling, the environment, and a strong desire to make 
positive social change regarding climate action. 
7. Demonstrated initiative with minimum direction; completing tasks by removing barriers and locating 
necessary resources. 
8. Demonstrated ability to communicate effectively in a wide variety of situations applying diplomacy and 
interpersonal skill to establish and maintain productive relationships.
