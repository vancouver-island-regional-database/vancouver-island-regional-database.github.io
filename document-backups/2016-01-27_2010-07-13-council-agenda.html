To:   Council

Re: AWARD OF TENDERS FOR CONCRETE FORMING AND REINFORCING BAR 
INSTALLATION FOR LADYSMITH COMMUNITY RESOURCES CENTRE AND CIVIC 
SQUARE 
 
RECOMMENDATION(S): 
It is recommended that Council award the concrete forming contract for the 
Ladysmith Community Resources Centre and Spirit Square to Dalcon Construction in 
the amount of $147,747 plus applicable taxe s; and that Council award the concrete 
reinforcing bar installation contract for the Ladysmith Community Resources Centre 
and Spirit Square to Allsta r Rebar Ltd. in the amount of $24,535 plus applicable 
taxes. 
  
PURPOSE: 
The purpose of this staff report is to seek Council’s approval to award contracts for 
concrete forming and reinforcing bar installation for Ladysmith Community Services 
Centre and Civic Square. 
 
INTRODUCTION/BACKGROUND: 
Both tenders were advertised on the Town’s website.  In addition, the Construction 
Manager, Windley Contracting Ltd., invited four contractors to bid on the forming 
contract as they had confirmed budget pric ing in the past.  Competitive bids were 
received from two companies for the concrete forming, and from four companies for 
the reinforcing bar installation. 
 
SCOPE OF WORK: 
Both companies have completed similar work in the past and have a good 
reputation.  The bids conform to the work required and the Construction Manager is 
satisfied with both bids. 
 
ALTERNATIVES: 
Council can choose not to award one or both of these contracts. 
 
FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS; 
The work falls within the overall approv ed budget of the Ladysmith Community 
Services Centre/Civic Square project. 
 
LEGAL IMPLICATIONS; 
Not applicable. 
…/2 

 
CITIZEN/PUBLIC RELATIONS IMPLICATIONS: 
The tenders were advertised in accordance with the Town’s purchasing policy. 
 
INTERDEPARTMENTAL INVOLVEMENT/IMPLICATIONS: 
Not applicable. 
 
RESOURCE IMPLICATIONS:
Not applicable 
 
ALIGNMENT WITH STRATEGIC PRIORITIES: 
The Ladysmith Community Services Centre / Civic Square initiative is one of Council’s 
Top 25 Strategic Priorities. 
 
SUMMARY: 
It is recommended that Council approve the awarding of the Concrete Forming 
contract for the Ladysmith Community Resources Centre / Civic Square to Dalcon 
construction, and it is recommended that Council approve the awarding of the 
Reinforcing Bar Installation contract to Allstar Rebar Ltd. 
 
 
 
 
ATTACHMENTS:
