LADYSMITH MARITIME SOCIETY – SUPPORT FOR 
COMMUNITY MARINA VISITOR FACILITIES PROJECT 
  
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Ladysmith 
Maritime Society be advised that Council supports in 
principle the installation of a sewage pump out station at 
the Ladysmith Maritime Society facility, and will provide 
financial assistance up to $25,000 subject to 
confirmation of funding and project feasibility by staff.
