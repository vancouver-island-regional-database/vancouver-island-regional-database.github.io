NOTICE PROCEDURE BYLAW AMENDMENTS In accordance with Section 124 of the Community Charter , notice is given that the Council of the T own of Ladysmith intends to give ﬁnal consideration to amendments to “Council Procedure Bylaw 2009, No. 1666” at its Regular meeting on August 6, 2024. The proposed amendments are intended to align Council procedures with new Provincial requirements for public hearings. In general terms, the proposed amendments include: 1. The prohibition of delegations wishing to speak to Zoning or OCP amendments outside of the public hearing process. 2. Procedures outlining the steps Council will follow when making a decision not to hold a public hearing. 3. A general prohibition on receiving representations when a hearing will not b e he ld and exceptions to this prohibition. 4. Additional changes intended to align with new legislative requirements.
