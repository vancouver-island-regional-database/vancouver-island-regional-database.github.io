5. DEVELOPMENT PERMIT APPLICATIONS 
 a. Development Permit application #3060-15-05 
  Lot C, District Lot 38, Oyster District, Plan EPP36585 (1146 Rocky Creek Rd) 
  Brad Harley – Densen Investments Ltd. 
 
L. Brinkman provided an introduction for the proposed new industrial building 
for the ‘Ladysmith Auto Supply Ltd.’ business as well as the proposed site 
works plans.  
 
The ADP asked questions and discussed the proposal.  The applicant, Brad 
Harley answered member’s questions. 
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Advisory Design Panel recommends that 
Council support Development Permit application #3060-15-05 (1146 Rocky Creek Road) 
after further review, by the Development Services Staff, of the pedestrian access, and the 
boulevard landscaping.
