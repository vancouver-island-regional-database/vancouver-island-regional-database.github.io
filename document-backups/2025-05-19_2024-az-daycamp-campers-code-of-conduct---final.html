CAMPER'S CODE OF CONDUCT:   
The Campers Code of Conduct is to ensure Campers understand the procedures at Ladysmith Parks 
Recreation & Culture Adventure Zone Daycamp to keep themselves, other campers, and leaders safe  
to ensure a positive experience at camp!  
Please read this list with your child to ensure they understand each point. Once you have done so, please have both 
you and your child sign and return. 
In order to keep a positive and safe environment at Adventure Zone Daycamp we ask that you understand the 
following: 
• For your safety, follow the instructions of the Leaders.
• Listen carefully when the Leaders are talking - to be respectful, one person talks at a time. When playing
games, listen carefully to the directions.
• Ask questions and speak to your Leader if you need something or do not understand the instructions.
• Respect others’ personal belongings by only touching your belongings, and not others, including backpacks,
lunch kits, water bottles, first aid kits, leader’s fanny packs, daycamp trolly, leader’s radios, the speaker, etc.
• Leave personal toys and electronics at home. We want campers to experience camp and engage directly
with each other. Adventure Zone Daycamp is also not responsible for lost personal toys and electronics.
• Keep personal things neat and in a designated space.
• Practice proper hand hygiene – to help keep campers and leaders healthy, and to help prevent the spread of
infectious diseases to others.
• Practice safe cough/sneeze etiquette by doing both in our elbows or tissue and washing our hands directly
afterwards.
