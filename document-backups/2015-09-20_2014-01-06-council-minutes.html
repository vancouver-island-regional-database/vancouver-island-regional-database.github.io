CORRESPONDENCE  
 
 
CS 2014-016   
 
Mayor Jan Allen, Village of Port Alice 
Objection to Cut to B.C. Ferries Coastal Service 
It was moved, seconded and carried that Council write to the 
Minister Responsible for B.C. Ferries and the Premier, noting the 
following: 
• That the Town is very concerned about the proposed cuts to 
the B.C. coastal ferry service and the subsequent impact of 
these cuts on local economies; 
• Requesting the province to consider including the B.C. ferry 
service within the provincial highway system, given that for 
many communities, the ferry service is considered a major 
means of access to the community; 
• Requesting the province to seek  alternate funding sources to 
fund the ferry system in order to disperse the financial impact 
of the rising costs of the service throughout the province
