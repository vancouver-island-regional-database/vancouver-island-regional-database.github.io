Notice of Participation – Canoe Procurement 
 
Notice is given that the Town of Ladysmith intends to participate in one or more 
procurements conducted by Canoe Procurement Group of Canada March 13, 2025 – 
February 1, 2026 . For further information and access to the Canoe RFP notices, please visit 
the Canoe Procurement Portal at https://canoe.bidsandtenders.ca/Module/Tenders/en or 
review the BC Bid designated tendering site at https://bcbid.gov.bc.ca. For further 
information about Canoe, please visit their website at http://www.canoeprocurement.ca.
