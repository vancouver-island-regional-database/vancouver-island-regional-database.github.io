5. HERITAGE PROJECT UPDATES 
a.  Transfer Beach Pictorial 
 A. Vincent provided a summary of the progress of the Transfer Beach Pictorial project,        
including the work by a graphic designer to create four interpretive boards with the 
following themes: introduction to  Ladysmith history, industrial use of Trans fer Beach , 
mining and forestry, and the transformation to a recreation space. Members 
appreciated the design concept and style. The pictorial is intended to be installed in 
September. 
 
6.       NEW BUSINESS 
a.    Joint Meeting with Ladysmith Heritage Groups 
The Ladysmith & District Heritage Society (LDHS) initiated a mapping project to identify 
heritage locations with links to stories and photos, to be mapped on a cadastral map 
base, ideally GIS. LDHS feels there are opportunities to work closely with other local 
heritage interest groups to integrate the maps with tourism products (i.e. historic 
walking tour apps).  
 
Staff have suggested that holding a joint meeting of HRAC, the Ladysmith Maritime 
Society and LDHS would be beneficial for sharing in formation and would provide an 
opportunity to update each other with projects or initiatives re cently completed or in 
progress, and to increase communicati on, awareness and understanding . This would 
help to determine opportunities for collaboration and cou ld set the stage for the 
heritage tourism mapping project. Members were very supportive of holding a joint 
meeting.

b. Tour of Harbour Heritage Centre and Transfer Beach Diorama 
Ladysmith Maritime Society (LMS) members S. Blackstaff, B. McDonald, and B. Pearson 
provided an informative tour of the Harbour Heritage Centre, showca sing the industries 
of the Ladysmith harbour. The newest feature is a diorama/model of Transfer Beach 
depicting the large coal wharf in the early 190 0s. HRAC members noted that the 
Transfer Beach Pictorial could reference the Harbour Heritage Centre and the wharf 
diorama in particular.
