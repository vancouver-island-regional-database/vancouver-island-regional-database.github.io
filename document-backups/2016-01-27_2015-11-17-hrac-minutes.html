3. COUNCIL REFERRALS/UPDATES  
 
4. SIGN/FAÇADE APPLICATIONS 
a) Proposed new sign for Benjamin Moore (132B Roberts Street) 
 
A sign permit application was presented by Planner, M. Paridaen for Benjamin Moore 
(Chroma Paint & Design Inc.) at 132B Roberts Street. HRAC members discussed the 
proposed sign. 
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission supports 
the proposed sign for Benjamin Moore (Chroma Paint & Design Inc.) at 132B Roberts Street subject 
to the applicant considering: a) using the blue Benjamin Moore corporate colour; and b) framing the 
sign with a material and colour similar to the building trim. 
 
HRAC members discussed with staff how to  gain downtown business compliance with the 
sign permit application process in general. 
 
5. HERITAGE PROJECT UPDATES 
a. Heritage Building Plaques 
 
Planner M. Paridaen provided a print out of the draft heritage building plaques for the 
HRAC members to review. A pdf of the draft heritage building plaques will also be sent to 
HRAC members via email to obtain member input. HRAC members discussed the 
heritage building plaque design process to date.  
 
HRAC members discussed celebrating the heritage building  plaque information in the 
form of a poster for the heritage week in February 2016.
