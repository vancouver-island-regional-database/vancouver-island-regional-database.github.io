POSITION DESCRIPTION 
 
 
 
TITLE:   PARKS MAINTENANCE WORKER III – ARBORIST  
DEPARTMENT: PARKS 
DIVISION:  INFRASTRUCTURE SERVICES 
CATEGORY:  UNION CUPE LOCAL 401 
BAND:   Band 14 
 
 
GENERAL ACCOUNTABILITY 
Reporting to the Parks Maintenance Supervisor  the Parks Maintenance Worker III - Arborist is responsible 
for carrying out skilled arboricultural work of considerable variety for an assortment of native and non-native 
tree species . General duties  include planting, cultivation, pruning and removing trees , pest and disease 
control, and climbing trees of considerable height for pruning or branch removal. In addition, t he Parks 
Maintenance Worker III – Arborist is expected to participate in the construction, renovation and 
maintenance of various landscaped and horticultural areas  on civic grounds throughout the Town of 
Ladysmith.  The se duties will normally be carried out under limited supervision with tasks assigned and 
inspected as necessary. 
 
PRIMARY DUTIES AND ACCOUNTABILITIES 
o Plant and transplant woody trees, including site preparation , backfilling, staking, watering, and 
mulching. 
o Remove trees and perform stump grinding as needed. 
o Identify plants, select rigging gear, and fall, limb, buck, and prune trees for various reasons . Haul and 
chip any resulting brush. 
o Evaluate trees for disease and pest problems, and perform treatments as needed . 
o Support trees and breaking branches by securing cables and braces . 
o Manage tree nutrient and water supply and consumption.  
o Maintain records of work performed. 
o Identify potentially hazardous trees and other tree-related problems. 
o Carry out the assessment, evaluation and rating of trees for hazard and retention potential  and 
recommend suitable species/varieties for specific sites and the Town as a whole.  
o Calculate the monetary value of trees. 
o Direct various parks maintenance employees and contractors in a number of tasks involving tree work 
such as pruning, wildlifing, hazard tree removals, tree planting and transporting, transplanting, 
watering, fertilizing and spraying as required. 
o Exercise considerable independence of judgment within established guidelines and refer any unusual 
or contentious matters to the Senior Parks Maintenance Worker . 
o Operate an aerial lift truck. 
o Fertilize, weed, lime, top dress, aerate, and irrigate parks, boulevards, lawns, planters and flower beds.  
o Plant and prune flowers, shrubs, trees and grasses. 
o Operate push mowers, power mowers, tractor mowers, weed eaters, spray equipment aerators, 
fertilizer spreaders and seeding equipment. 
o Operate tractor mounted boom type flail mowers. 
o Adhere to safety policies and regulations as established by WorkSafe BC, the Occupational Health and 
Safety committee, the Town of Ladysmith and regional and municipal statutes.

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS, KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS AND ABILITY 
1. Completion of Grade 12 or equivalent (GED) 
2. ISA arborist certification, ITA a rborist technician certificate, ITA  climbing a rborist certificate of 
qualification, ITA bucking and falling endorsement , Arboriculture Canada tree appraiser certificate, 
Tree Risk Assessment Qualification Certification 
3. Minimum of three (3) years recent and relevant experience.  An equivalent combination of education 
and experience may be considered. 
4. Considerable knowledge of arboricultural principles, practices and techniques, and of tree species, 
diseases, pests and treatment methods. 
5. Demonstrated a bility to develop, implement and coordinate arboricultural plans, policies and 
procedures and arboricultural care standards. 
6. Demonstrated ability to conduct inspections, assess tree related problems, diagnose tree diseases and 
determine required action, and perform skilled arboricultural work including pruning, planting and tree 
removal as required.  
7. Demonstrated ability to fulfill assigned duties in all weather conditions . 
8. Valid flagging certificate. 
9. Valid Workplace Hazardous Material Information System (WHMIS) certificate.  
10. Valid B.C. driver’s license - class 5 with air brake endorsement. 
11. BC Pesticide Applicator’s certificate is an asset 
12. Demonstrated knowledge of safe work procedures, practices and obligations.  
13. Demonstrated proficiency in the safe operation of motorized vehicles and  equipment and, various 
infrastructure services tools and light industrial equipment including but limited to:  
o Aerial lift truck 
o Chipper 
o Tractor / Flail / Implements 
o Skid Steer 
o Bobcat Trail Unit 
o Rotary / Zero Turn Mowers 
o Bucket Truck 
o Single Axle Dump Truck 
o Forklift 
o Small Equipment 
14. Demonstrated initiative with specific direction; completing tasks by removing barriers and locating 
necessary resources. 
15. Demonstrated ability to communicate effectively in a wide variety of situations applying diplomacy and 
interpersonal skill to establish and maintain productive relationships.  
16. Demonstrated high level of focus on internal and external client service continuously seeking ways to 
meet and exceed expectations. 
17. Demonstrated ability to work cooperatively within a team and with Town employees, stakeholders and 
partners to achieve optimal results. 
18. Demonstrates valuing diversity.
