CS 2014-333  
Development Variance Permit Application – 920 Malone Road 
(Bourodemos) – Lot 2, District Lot 96, Oyster District, Plan 
VIP57370 
Moved and seconded: 
That Council not support the encroachment of the accessory 
structure located at 920 Malone Road on the Town boulevard; 
and 
That Council direct staff to proceed with the statutory notice for 
Development Variance Permit application (3090 -14-03) for Lot 2, 
District Lot 96, Oyster District, Plan VIP57370 (920 Malone Road) 
to permit the siting of the accessory structure to be as close as 
zero metres from the side and rear lot lines; and that the applicant 
be required to provide a survey prepared by a B .C. Land Surveyor 
showing the location of the structure, prior to the statutory 
notification. 
Motion carried.

awarded a total of $11 million in funding for the new Waste Water 
Treatment Plant from the Green Municipal Fund.  The funding is a 
combination of a $1 million grant and a $10 million low -interest-
rate loan at 3.35 per cent.  Mayor Hutchins indicated that a formal 
event with a representativ e of the federal government will take 
place at a later date. 
 
Mayor Hutchins provided a summary of three legal actions in 
which the Cowichan Valley Regional District is involved – 
Cowichan Lake Weir, South Island Aggregate and the crematorium 
in Paldi, all  of which could have an impact on other local 
governments, in particular the crematorium question. 
 
Municipal Services Committee Recommendations 
Moved and seconded: 
That Council receive the Waterfront Area Plan Review – 
Developing the Scope of Work  document considered at the 
September 15, 2014 Municipal Services Committee meeting, and 
direct staff to continue to work with the Federation of Canadian 
Municipalities Green Municipal Fund to secure funding for the 
project; 
 
That Council establish the geo graphic area to be included in the 
Waterfront Area Plan Review to be the Waterfront Sub -Area as 
outlined on page 22 of the staff report titled Waterfront Area Plan 
Review – Determining the Scope of Work;  and  
 
That Council direct staff to report back with  recommendations for 
an appropriate timeline for completion of a Review of the 
Waterfront Area Plan. 
Motion carried.
