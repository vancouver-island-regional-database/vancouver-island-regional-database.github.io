Advisory Planning Commission (APC)

Council Chambers, City Hall

Laxdal; Council Liaison – Gord Horth; Staff Liaison – Lisa Brinkman, 
Planner and Recorder. 
 
ABSENT/REGRETS: Tina Donovan 
 
GUESTS:  Applicant – Brian Senini; Owners – Earl Wallace and Angela Jurisic      
  
 
The quorum requirement was not met. 
 
The Acting Chair (P. Fraser) called the meeting to order at 7:05 p.m. 
 
1. AGENDA 
The APC recommends that the agenda of August 27, 2013 be approved. 
 
2. ADOPTION OF MINUTES 
The APC recommends that the APC minutes of February 13, 2013 be adopted.  
 
3. COUNCIL REFERRALS 
 See 4a. 
 
4. OFFICIAL COMMUNITY PLAN (OCP)/ZONING BYLAW PROPOSED AMENDMENTS 
a. OCP Amendment and Rezoning Application 3360-13-02 
 Portion of Lot A, District Lot 146, Oyster District, Plan VIP78848 Except Part of 
Plan VIP81319 (Cook Street) Brian Senini        
 
APC members asked questions and discussed the proposed rezoning.  
 
The Advisory Planning Commission recommends that proposed lots 5 and 6 
be removed from the park designation and park zone to permit residential use 
on the condition that an area for a children’s playground of a standard size be 
secured in the immediate  neighbourhood of the subject property ; and that a 
suitable amenity be secured for park land development such as a multi -use 
path, playground or both.
