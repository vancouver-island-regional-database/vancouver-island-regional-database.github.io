4. SIGN/FAÇADE APPLICATIONS 
 A. Vincent reviewed the role of the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission in 
 consideration of signs and facades. 
  Section 3.2.2 of the Sign & Canopy Bylaw (Bylaw 1176):  
The Building Inspector shall refer every sign application(s) for property located in the 
Downtown Specified Area to the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commissio n for 
consideration and report to him on colour, design, location and style prior to issuance 
of the permit. 
a. Stelur Eatery and Catering Company, #2-341 First Avenue 
Lurene Haines of the Stelur Eatery presented the proposed signage and façade for the 
building.  She noted that the awning would be made from canvas material.  Members 
appreciated the fresh looks of the design and colours. 
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Heritage Revitalization Advisory Commission 
recommend the sign and façade proposal for the Stelur Eatery and Catering Company at 341 First 
Avenue, unit #2.
