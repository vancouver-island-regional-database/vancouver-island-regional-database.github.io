Townhouses are designed with three storeys including a half lower storey for the garages. Four of seven townhouse buildings comply with the maximum height regulations and do not require a variance. The proposed location of these buildings does not impact views of other units. The proposed variances for apartment buildings 8 and 9 are to facilitate a fourth storey for increased units and underground parking for each building. The proposed elevation of the two buildings is approximately 13.3m above grade at the cul-de-sac at the end of Stringer Way.
