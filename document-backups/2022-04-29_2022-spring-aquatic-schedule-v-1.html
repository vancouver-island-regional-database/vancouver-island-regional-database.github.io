  Children 7yrs+ can attend unaccompanied, children under 7yrs must be accompanied by an adult 19yrs+ (2 children/1 adult).  
  Pool is cleared 5 minutes BEFORE end time.
Aquatic Visits

Drop In Aquatic Fitness ClassesAquatic Visits
Length Swims
Kick start your day with some early morning laps, get your kilometer of 
lengths in at lunch, or end your day perfecting your strokes during our length 
swims.  There will be four lanes; casual, slow, medium and fast.  Please pick 
the lane most appropriate for your swim speed.
Leisure Swim
If you need the warmth of the Therapy T each Pool to loosen up your muscles, 
decrease the gravity on your joints, to work on pre or post-surgery exercises 
or just to get in some unassisted walking with the support of the water then 
the Ladysmith Leisure Swim is for you.
Please note: if you require assistance to and from the change room or in and out 
of the pool, you will be required to have an aid with you to assist you. 
Parent and T ot Swim  
Do you have a tiny human who needs to burn off energy in the morning?  We 
have just the thing to make that happen while having fun in the pool!  The 
Parent & T ot swim is geared towards children not yet school aged and their 
caregiver.  
Everyone Welcome Swim
A great time to enjoy the main pool and the therapy pool, diving board, 
climbing wall and slides.  No length swimming during this time.  
Adaptive Aqua Fitness
A program for clients with arthritis and other mobility limitations. T ake 
advantage of our qualiﬁed, experienced staff that have the expertise to 
personalize your workout  by accommodating individual needs. Be sure to 
bring your own full water bottle to class each session.
Aquafit
A highly energized aquatic ﬁtness workout delivered by certiﬁed ﬁtness 
instructors. These classes provide a well-rounded, head-to-toe workout. Be 
sure to bring your own water bottle ﬁlled for each class.
Deep H2O
These invigorating, impact-free classes work and tone all muscle groups. 
Participants combine cardiovascular work, deep-water running, jogging, 
bicycling and cross-country skiing movements. Class focuses on muscular 
strength motions that work the body’s major muscle groups as well as 
motions that work the core and improve balance. A buoyancy belt is 
required to create and maintain proper balance and posture. Be sure to 
bring your own full water bottle to class.

The Aquatic area at the FJCC features a large and a smaller, warmer pool, perfect for everyone.  Bring tHE whole family for a swim or STOP BY FOR A soak in the hot tub after a workout. **Children under 7 years must be accompanied in water and within arms reach of parent or person 19 years plus.  (2 children/1 adult)**.
Our Aquatic area has:• 4-lane, 25-metre pool• Diving board, basketball hoops, rope swing, & climbing wall• Aquatic wheelchairs, removable stairs and water run-way• Therapy pool accessible by ramp with a shallow depth of zero sloping to 1.25 metres• Whirlpool, DRY SAUNA
For information on admission prices, check on the 2022 Spring Activity Guide online at www.ladysmith.ca/programs
