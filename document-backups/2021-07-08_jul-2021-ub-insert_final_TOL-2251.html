New emergency response app 
lauched for Ladysmith and area
The Cowichan Valley Regional District (CVRD), its 
partner municipalities and First Nations have launched 
a new and improved system to send emergency alerts 
and advisories to residents and visitors to the Cowichan 
region.
Cowichan Alert is a robust yet easy-to-use platform for 
ensuring the timely delivery of information to residents, 
covering everything from fires and floods to road closures. 
As we enter an important period for wildfire awareness, 
this is an opportunity to ensure we are prepared to 
protect ourselves, our family and our community during 
an emergency. 
Through the Alertable app, users have the ability to 
personalize their notifications by type, severity and 
location, ensuring they are only receiving information 
that is relevant to them.
“This new system will improve the ability of local 
governments and emergency agencies in the Cowichan 
Valley to get critical and timely information into the 
hands of residents,” said Mayor Aaron Stone. “It is our 
hope that every resident and business owner signs up 
so they can stay informed about what’s happening in our 
communities, avoid dangerous situations and potentially 
even save lives.”
Cowichan Alert is a tool provided under the regional 
emergency program service. Staff from all partnering 
local governments and First Nations have the ability 
to provide alerts and notifications specifically to their 
residents, as well as send region-wide alerts as required. 
During the COVID-19 pandemic, Alertable has been 
used by local governments for contact tracing, closures, 
physical distancing reminders, PPE donation drives, 
community surveys and more.
To sign up for Cowichan Alert and download the Alertable 
app, go to www.cowichanalert.ca.

Sustainability Ambassadors 
leading outreach initiatives
The T own of Ladysmith is pleased to introduce its two 
Sustainability Ambassadors Gwen and Chloe who will 
be leading community outreach initiatives this summer. 
The sustainability program supports Council’s strategic 
goals for mitigating the impacts of climate change as we 
work together as stewards of our environment. A key 
focus is partnering with the community to improve waste 
disposal education and recycling efficiency.  Recent 
data showed many non-recyclable items or garbage are 
ending up in the recycling system.
Additionally, the Ambassadors will use their knowledge 
of green initiatives to increase awareness among 
residents and businesses about water conservation and 
our distribution system.

Fire Ban now in effect
The hot, dry weather is creating significant forest fire hazards with the fire danger for our area currently rated as 
extreme. Therefore, campfires and cooking fires will not be permitted across BC in order to reduce risk. 
This fire ban will be in effect until October 15, 2021.

TOWN NEWS YOU CAN USE
Graffiti Removal Rebate Program - Council has approved a new Graffiti Removal Rebate Program that runs now until 
September 30, 2021.  A one-time rebate of $50 is available to all property owners to cover the cost of removing 
graffiti. Please complete a form available at City Hall or my emailing info@ladysmith.ca and submit it with photos of 
the work to be completed.      
Holland Lake Intake at Chicken Ladder  - T own crews completed the dredging of the Holland Lake intake at Chicken 
Ladder over three days in late June.  The work ensures the intake basin is clear of sediment buildup - particularly fine 
sand - which causes added maintenance at our Water Filtration Plant. 
Art Donation - Council approved a public art donation request from Duck and Tracy Paterson for 13 signed, original, 
limited edition Roy Henry Vickers art prints that are currently on display in the City Hall Council Chamber. Vickers is 
a well-known BC artist who is beloved for his colourful landscapes. Thank you Duck and Tracy for your generousity. 
Pet Responsibility -  We encourage community members to be responsible pet owners and please pick up after your 
animal(s) when out walking on sidewalks, trails and visiting parks.  Properly disposing of your waste bags by using the 
nearest available trash is helping to keep our public spaces enjoyable for everyone. We appreciate your cooperation. 
Y ard Waste Pilot Project - The T own’s Yard Waste Pilot Project took place over two weeks in the spring and was highly 
successful.  A report will be brought to Council in the fall with recommendations for potential future initiatives. In the 
meantime, you can always bring your yard waste free of charge to the CVRD’s Peerless Recycling Depot. 
Aggie Hall being used as a COVID-19 Vaccination Site  - The T own of Ladysmith continues to support Island Health in 
facilitating a COVID-19 immunization clinic at Aggie Hall. All appointments for vaccinations must be pre-booked.  
For information on eligibility and how to register: https://www.islandhealth.ca/learn-about-health/covid-19/covid-19-
vaccine
