MINUTES 
Advisory Design Panel (ADP) 
Tuesday, April 21, 2009 at 1:00 p.m. 
Council Chambers, City Hall 
 
 
LADYSMITH 
PRESENT: Chair – Brian Bancroft; Members – Brian Childs, Ron Kinney, Kaien Shimizu; Council 
Members– Bruce Whittington, Jillian Dashwood; Planner – Lisa Brinkman; 
Administrative Assistant and Recorder – Diane Webber 
  
GUESTS: Steven Pabla - Applicant; Michael Ker -  Square One Design Ltd.; Moni Khan – Shoa 
Organizational Services Ltd.; Ken Brault -  Straight Street Design. 
   
 
The meeting was called to order at 1:02 p.m. 
 
1. AGENDA  
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Agenda of April 21, 2009 be approved with the addition 
of 3 c)  Zoning Amendment Application 3360-09-01 1142 Trans Canada Highway (M. Huntjens). 
                   
2. MINUTES 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Minutes of the November 18, 2008 meeting be 
adopted.  
 
3. DEVELOPMENT APPLICATION REFERRALS  
 
 a. Zoning Amendment Application 3360-09-01
  11 & 17 Warren Street (Satpal Pabla) 
 
Planner, L. Brinkman introduced the application.  An approved design for the 
neighbouring  Browsorium was also provided by Planner, L. Brinkman to the ADP 
members as the applicant intends to design a building that will complement and fit 
with the neighbouring building. 
 
M. Ker presented a colour board of the proposed mixed-use, commercial/residential 
building and answered questions asked by the Members. 
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Advisory Design Panel deny the approval of the design 
as presented for the Zoning Amendment Application (3360-09-01) for 11 & 17 Warren Street. 
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Advisory Design Panel advises Council that it approves 
of the proposed use for the site of the Zoning Amendment Application (3360-09-01), 11 & 17 
Warren Street but do not approve of the design proposal as submitted. 
 
b. Development Variance Permit Application 3090-08-07  
Thetis Drive – Shoa Organizational Services Ltd. (Moni Khan)      
  
Planner, L Brinkman provided a summary of the Development Variance Permit 
application for Lots 1 and  5 to 19.    
 
M. Khan presented her proposed designs and explained her desire to build level 
entry homes on the lots. New information provided by M. Khan was that the three 
sample rear elevations presented to staff, the Advisory Design Panel and to Council 
on April 6, 2009 is not what she intends to build, with the exception of perhaps one 
spec home.

K. Brault presented a display board showing potential landscape treatments for the 
rear of lots 5 to 19 and expressed that design guidelines may be a good solution to 
mitigate impacts of three storey rear elevations. 
 
Councillor, B. Whittington requested that the Advisory Design Panel provide 
comments regarding the height variance request for Lot 1. 
 
The Advisory Design Panel expressed concerns regarding the designs presented, 
including: 
• The character and quality of the designs – not suited to Ladysmith - would 
like to see more variation in materials and house sizes; 
• An artistic blend of houses is needed for the neighbourhood; 
• Neighbourhood context is important (i.e. designs for lots 20 to 30 as well);  
• Not enough information presented – would like to see a clearer proposal. 
 
It was moved, seconded and carried that after serious consideration and discussion with the 
developer, the Advisory Design Panel does not have enough information to make a qualified decision 
on this application. 
 
  Councillor, J. Dashwood left the meeting at 3:30 p.m. 
 
c. Rezoning Application 3360-08-04 – 1142 Trans Canada Highway – M. Huntjens.  
 
Planner, L Brinkman presented updated plans for the rezoning application and 
requested comments from the ADP. 
 
The Members commented that they did not approve of the combination of hardiplank 
and cedar.  The ADP found the project not acceptable and requires a new design.
