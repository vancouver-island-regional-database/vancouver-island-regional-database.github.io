Family Change Rooms
There are 5 family change rooms, one equipped for special 
needs with a ceiling lift and a changing bed. These rooms have 
been designed for patrons with special needs, parents with 
small children and those requiring additional privacy.  
Age & Height Restrictions
All children under the age of 7 years and/or are less than 1.3 
metres (52 inches) tall must be accompanied in the water and 
within arms reach of a parent or other person at least 19 years 
of age. Ratio of children under 7 to adult must be no greater 
than 2 children to one adult.  
The Sauna 
Step into this hot, dry, relaxing area that is kept at an average 
of 55°C (131°F). 
The Whirlpool
The whirlpool is a combination of turbulent air and 40°C 
(104°F) water pumped into the pool.  
Main Pool 
A four-lane, 25-metre pool heated at an inviting 28°C (84°F).  
The main pool features: trampoline • diving board • skywalker 
• slides • Tarzan rope •  aquatic lift • basketball hoops • 
removable stairs • climbing wall • water run-ways.
Therapy/Leisure Pool
Accessible by ramp; it is heated at a warm 34°C (94°F) and has 
a shallow end depth of zero sloping to four feet.

Red Cross Swim Preschool
Canada’s best swimming program for children ages 6 mos to 6 
years. Songs, fun activities, and introductory swimming skills 
will make you child comfortable and happy in the water.
Red Cross PS Level 1 - Sea Otter
3-5 years. Transition for swimmers from Sea Turtle as they move 
to greater independence & manageable for new swimmers 
starting at age 3. fi  rst level without parent participation.
Red Cross PS Level 2 – Salamander 
3 - 5 years. Preschoolers actively learn new swimming skills 
including fl  oats & glides through games & songs. The fun 
includes learning to jump into chest-deep water. 
Red Cross PS Level 3 – Sunfish 
3 - 5 years. Preschoolers work on stroke & skill progressions. 
Learning also focuses on good judgement in, on, & around the 
water as well as entries & fl  oats in deep water. 
Red Cross PS Level 4 – Crocodile 
3 - 5 years. Preschoolers start independent glides & kicking in 
deep water. Endurance is built through increasing distance for 
front & back swims.
Red Cross PS Level 5 – Whale 
3 - 5 years. Preschoolers learn to swim independently & join 
in team games, increase their distance & improve front & back 
swims and learn about helping others in the water.   
Red Cross Swim Kids
Canada’s most enjoyable and complete swimming program for 
children ages 5-12 years. Your child will learn to swim, be fi  t 
and stay safe in a fun environment that promotes achieving a 
personal best and celebrates individual success.   Swimming 
lessons foster an active lifestyle by promoting fun in the water 
and encouraging continued fi  tness.
Red Cross Level 1 (6-rs & up)
Entry level for those who didn’t take the Preschool lessons. 
providing an orientation to the water & the pool area & 
introduces fl  oats & glides with kicks. 
Red Cross Level 2 (6-rs & up)
A second orientation level which helps the child build skills in 
front & back swims.  Introduction to deep water & proper use of 
a PDF . Endurance is built on fl  utter kicking with assisted glides.
Red Cross Level 3 (6-rs & up)
Introduction to front crawl & wise choices on where & when to 
swim. Diving is introduced & fl  oats.  Endurance is achieved by 
building strength in fl  utter kick & a 15-meter swim.
Red Cross Level 4 (6-rs & up)
6 & up. Front crawl, back glide & shoulder roll for back crawl 
are further developed. Children work on kneeling dives, surface 
support & developing self-safety by knowing their own limits. 
Endurance is built through a 25-meter swim.
Red Cross Level 5 (6-rs & up)
Back crawl is introduced, along with sculling skills & whip kick 
on back. Children try stride dives & receive an introduction to 
safe boating skills. Endurance is developed through dolphin 
kick & a 50-meter swim.
Red Cross Level 6 (6-rs & up)
Front & back crawl continue to be refi  ned as backstroke is 
introduced. Safety on ice, elementary rescue of others with 
throwing assists, treading water & front dives introduced. 
Endurance is built through a 75-meter swim.
Red Cross Level 7 (6-rs & up)
Continues to build skills & endurance for front crawl, back crawl 
& back stroke.  Introduction to  whip kick on the front, airway 
& breathing obstructions, timed treading water for increasing 
endurance as well as an endurance swim of 150-meter. 
Red Cross Level 8 (6-rs & up)
Introduction to breaststroke, feet fi  rst surface dives, & rescue 
entries. Learn about the dangers of open water, hypothermia, 
& the performance of rescue breathing on children & adults.  
Endurance is built through dolphin kick & 300-meter swim. 
Red Cross Level 9 (6-rs & up)
Front & back crawl, elementary back, side & breast strokes 
continue to be refi  ned. Dives & wise choices, peer infl  uences & 
self rescue from ice are covered. Endurance is built through a 
400-meter swim.
Red Cross Level 10 (6-rs & up)
This level covers refi  nement of all strokes, fi  tness & sun safety, 
rescue of others from ice, & head & foot fi  rst surface dives. 
Endurance is built through a 500-meter swim.

Aquatic Fitness
Fun
The aquatic environment and facility atmosphere 
make it a great place to laugh and have fun while 
doing something great for your mind and body.
Water Resistance
Using the resistance of water in exercise is like 
exercising with weights or machines on land but 
safer. (According to your needs and limitations)
Water Temperature
The water temperature is consistent all year 
long.  You do not have to contend with variable 
elements such as heat, cold, rain, snow, etc.
Buoyancy
In chest deep water, approximately 90% of your 
body weight is buoyant; therefore you really 
are bearing only 10% of your weight when you 
exercise. Exercise in the water is much safer and 
reduces stress on your joints and muscles. 
Cooling Eff  ect
With your continual movement in cool water your 
body is kept from overheating.  Drinking water 
while exercising in water is just as important as 
when exercising on dry land.
