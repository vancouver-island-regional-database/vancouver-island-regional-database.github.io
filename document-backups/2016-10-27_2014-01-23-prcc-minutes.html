PARKS, RECREATION & CULTURE COMMISSION 
Minutes of the Commission Meeting held at Ladysmith Waterfront Art Gallery 
Wednesday January 22, 2014 at 7:00pm 
 
 
PRESENT: Will Vandergrift, Chair; John Perry; Kathy Holmes; Alison Bailie; Tim Godau, Area 
G; Brad Uytterhagen; Duck Paterson, Councillor; Clayton Postings, Staff; Julie 
Tierney, Recorder. 
 
ABSENT:   Bryon Adams; Chantal Blumel; Glenn Popenko; Area H, Gord Horth, Councillor. 
 
 
AGENDA  APPROVAL 2014-01:  It was moved, seconded and carried that the agenda for 
the Commis sion meeting for Wednesday, January 22, 2014 be 
approved as presented. 
 
MINUTES APPROVAL 2014-02:  It was moved, seconded and carried that the minutes 
from the Commission meeting of Wednesday, November 20 , 2013 
be approved as presented. 
 
OLD BUSINESS Trans Canada Trail – Chairperson, Will Vandergrift reported on 
possible upcoming grant opportunities. 
 
NEW BUSINESS Dogs off Leash – At the Council meeting held January 20, 2014, it 
was requested that Commission consider the com mittee’s 
recommendation that Holland Creek Trail be deemed a dog -on-leash 
trail. 
 
2014-03:  It was moved, seconded and carried that the Parks, 
Recreation and Culture Commission recommend that Council 
consider amending Parks Usage Bylaw 1995, No. 1158, Amen dment 
Bylaw 2000, No. 1396, to state that dogs must be leashed  at all 
times  while on Holland Creek Trail. 
 
Sports Tourism Representative –  Following the presentation by John 
Elzinga on September 18 , 2013, a formal request has been made  to 
Council from the CVRD requesting a representative from Ladysmith be 
appointed to serve on the Cowichan Valley Regional District Sport 
Tourism Working committee.   
 
2014-04: It was moved, seconded and carried that the Parks, 
Recreation & Culture Commission recommend that C ouncil appoint 
John Perry and that Alison Bailie be considered as an alternate 
member of the Sports Tourism Committee.
