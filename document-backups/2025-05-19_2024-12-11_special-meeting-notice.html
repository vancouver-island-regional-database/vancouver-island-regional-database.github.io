NOTICE 
SPECIAL COUNCIL MEETING  
 
 
 
Pursuant to “Council Procedure Bylaw 2009, No. 1666” notice is provided that Council will 
hold a Special Meeting on December 11, 2024.  The meeting will include the following 
business: 
• Ladysmith Harbour Economic Development Corporation 2024 Update; and 
• Appointment of Election Officials for the 2025 By-Election. 
 
Please note:  Although this meeting will be held electronically, the public may attend in 
person at City Hall Council Chambers, 410 Esplanade.
