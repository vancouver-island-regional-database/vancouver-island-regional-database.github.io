ATTACHMENT C TOWN OF LADYSMITH BYLAW NO. 2222 The Council of the Town of Ladysmith in open meeting assembled enacts the following amendments to “Town of Ladysmith Zoning Bylaw 2014, No. 1860”: 1. Schedule A – Zoning Bylaw Text is amended by: a. relabeling “Figure 10.8.1” under subsection (e) of section 10.8.8 (Site Specific Regulations of the R-3-A Zone) as “Figure 10.8.3” and updating all references in subsection 10.8.8(e)(i) to 10.8.8(e)(iv) accordingly; and b. adding as subsection (f) under section 10.8.8 the following: “f) The maximum Units Per Hectare regulation in subsection 10.8.4(b), does not apply to the area shown in figure 10.8.4. FIGURE 10.8.4 AREA WHERE UNITS PER HECTARE REGULATION DOES NOT APPLY A bylaw to amend "Town of Ladysmith Zoning Bylaw 2014, No. 1860"

ATTACHMENT C Citation 2. This bylaw may be cited for all purposes as "Town of Ladysmith Zoning Bylaw 2014, No. 1860, Amendment Bylaw 2025, No. 2222”.
