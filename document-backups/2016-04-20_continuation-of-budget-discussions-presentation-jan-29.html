Notification of sale of property 
 The listings are all public information 
 When Council discusses purchase offers and negotiations in closed session, it 
is to protect the Town’s position as well as the privacy of potential 
purchasers, as permitted in s. 90(1) of the Community Charter 
 Council rises and reports on the matter, and that is when staff are directed 
to publish notice of Council’s intent to sell the property 
 Notice of the sale of a property is to be: 
 Published in accordance with Section 94 of the Community Charter 
 Placed in the public notice posting place 
 Section 26 of the Charter– property description, who is acquiring the property, whether 
it is a sale or lease, how much the Town will receive in compensation
