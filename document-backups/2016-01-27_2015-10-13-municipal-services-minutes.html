MS 2015-050   
 
 
City Manager’s Quarterly Report 
The City Manager introduced her quarterly report to the Committee, 
and responded to questions. 
 
Council requested that staff ensure there is a photograph of the sign 
at the Stz’uminus Midden on the Town’s website. 
 
Moved and seconded: 
That the Municipal Services Committee recommend that Council 
direct staff  to investigate options for preventing vehicles from 
impeding traffic flow on First Avenue in the vicinicy of Tim Horton’s 
as well as delivery trucks on First Ave. Motion carried. 
 
Moved and seconded: 
That  staff provide a cost breakdown of expenses and the source of 
funds for the Gatacre Street construction project. 
Motion carried.
