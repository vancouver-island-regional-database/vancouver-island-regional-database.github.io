Current nameLadysmith Harbour Economic Development Corporation (LHEDC).[1]
Former nameDL2016 Holdings Corporation, also written DL 2016 Holdings Corporation and DL2016 Holding Corp.[1] [2]
Ownership“A wholly owned subsidiary of the Town of Ladysmith.” Town Council acts as sole shareholder at the annual general meeting.[1] [2]
Registration“A registered business corporation (Inc. #BC0857041) through Articles of Incorporation.” The BC Company Summary dated October 31, 2023 states: incorporated July 17, 2003; previous name DL 2016 HOLDINGS CORPORATION, name change April 24, 2023; registered office and records office 1300 - 128 PENDER STREET WEST, VANCOUVER BC V6B 1R8; directors listed (as printed): Belobaba, Jake; Erin, Anderson; McCarrick, Allison; Ray, Gauthier; no officer information filed as at July 17, 2023.[1] [2]
BoardFive directors. Section 13.2 as quoted in 2019: three directors from the Town or carrying on business in Ladysmith and two from the Ladysmith Maritime Society. Later appointments refer to Articles 13.2(a), (b) and (c).[1] [2]
Agreement termThe 2019 staff report quotes a “20-year term to July 1, 2029” for the Management and Operating Agreement and License Agreement. The agreements in the Province’s FOI package FOR-2023-31761 state a term to November 30, 2029 (Community Marina Sublease and Management and Operating Agreement). The Province’s interest report lists Lease #1407751 with expiry 2029-Dec-01.[1] [2] [3]
Stated purposeDescribed differently across documents. See “Stated purpose, by date” below.[1] [2] [3]
ActivityDecember 11, 2024: “the LHEDC has not met or conducted any business” since the January 9, 2024 annual general meeting. January 20, 2026: “The corporation did not undertake any activities in 2025.”[1] [2]
Annual cost to the TownApproximately $900 (reports from 2020 to January 2024). Approximately $3,000 (January 2025 and January 2026 reports), for the corporate tax return and associated legal costs.[1] [2] [3] [4]
Financial statementsApproved each year by Council resolution as sole shareholder. The attachment is the corporate income tax return, which staff reports describe as summarizing the financial statements. The Town’s consolidated statements describe the corporation in a note.[1] [2]
LeaseThe Town held Crown leases over District Lot 2016. As of December 31, 2023 the Town no longer leases the area from the Province. On January 1, 2024 Stz’uminus took possession.[1] [2]
Annual update to the publicSection 28.2 of the Articles of Incorporation requires an update to the public on the corporation’s business and activities.[1]
